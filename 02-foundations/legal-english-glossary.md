# Legal English glossary (English–中文)

> **Read this first.**
> - **Every Chinese rendering below is approximate (approx.).** Many US terms have no exact PRC equivalent, and a familiar-looking translation can carry the wrong doctrine (see the ⚠ notes).
> - **On the exam, write the English term of art, never the translation.** Graders look for the precise English label (e.g., "proximate cause", "consideration", "hearsay").
> - The one-line meanings are memory aids, not rule statements. Full rules with authority are in each subject's `rule-statements.md` (Phase 4). The PRC→US concept map is in [common-law-for-civil-lawyers.md](common-law-for-civil-lawyers.md) §7.

## 1. General, sources and procedure

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| common law | 普通法；判例法 | Judge-made law developed case by case; also the English-derived legal tradition as a whole |
| civil law (two meanings) | 大陆法系；民法 | ⚠ (1) The code-based legal family; (2) private law as opposed to criminal law. Context decides. |
| equity | 衡平法 | Body of law and remedies from the Court of Chancery: injunctions, specific performance, trusts |
| precedent | 先例 | An earlier decision whose holding governs later similar cases |
| binding (mandatory) authority | 有拘束力的法源 | Must be followed: a higher court in the same hierarchy, on that jurisdiction's law |
| persuasive authority | 说服性法源 | May be followed: other states' cases, lower courts, Restatements, treatises |
| holding | 判决要旨；判决规则 | The rule necessary to decide the case on its material facts; the only binding part |
| majority rule | 多数规则 | Rule followed by most US jurisdictions; the MBE default unless stated otherwise |
| minority rule | 少数规则 | Rule followed by fewer jurisdictions; flag on an essay only if it changes the result |
| modern trend | 现代趋势 | The direction courts and Restatements are moving |
| statute | 制定法；成文法 | A legislative enactment |
| regulation | 行政法规；规章 | Agency rule issued under statutory authority |
| ordinance | 地方性法规 | Municipal or county enactment (e.g., zoning) |
| Restatement | 法律重述 | ALI's black-letter summary of the common law; persuasive unless adopted |
| Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) | 统一商法典 | Uniform act enacted by states; Article 2 governs sales of goods |
| uniform act / model code | 统一法 / 示范法 | Drafted for states to adopt; law only where enacted |
| black-letter law | 基本法律规则（"黑体字"规则） | The settled core rule, as stated in outlines and Restatements |
| cause of action | 诉因；请求权基础 | A set of facts giving a right to sue (e.g., negligence) |
| claim | 请求；诉讼请求 | A demand for relief based on a cause of action |
| plaintiff / defendant | 原告 / 被告 | Parties at trial |
| appellant / appellee (respondent) | 上诉人 / 被上诉人 | Parties on appeal; California often says "respondent" |
| petitioner | 申请人；请求人 | Party seeking a writ or discretionary review |
| burden of proof | 证明责任 | Burden of production (come forward with evidence) + burden of persuasion (convince the fact-finder) |
| preponderance of the evidence | 优势证据标准 | More likely than not; the default civil standard |
| clear and convincing evidence | 清楚且令人信服的证据标准 | Intermediate standard (e.g., punitive damages in California, some will issues) |
| beyond a reasonable doubt | 排除合理怀疑 | Criminal conviction standard |
| trier of fact (fact-finder) | 事实认定者 | Jury, or the judge in a bench trial |
| question of law / question of fact | 法律问题 / 事实问题 | Judge decides law; jury decides facts and applies standards |
| verdict | （陪审团）裁断 | The jury's decision |
| judgment | 判决 | The court's final decision that disposes of the case |
| affirm / reverse / vacate / remand | 维持 / 改判 / 撤销 / 发回 | Appellate dispositions |
| damages (vs damage) | 损害赔偿金（与"损害"区分） | ⚠ "Damages" = money awarded; "damage" = harm suffered |
| remedy | 救济 | What the court gives: damages, injunction, restitution, etc. |
| reasonable person | 理性人；合理人 | Objective standard used in negligence, contracts and Fourth Amendment law |
| fiduciary | 受信人；受托义务人 | Person owing duties of loyalty and care (trustee, agent, partner, director, lawyer) |
| IRAC | 争点—规则—分析—结论 | Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion: the essay structure graders expect |
| call of the question | 题目指令；设问 | The question(s) at the end of the essay facts; your headings must track it |
| issue spotting | 争点识别 | Finding every legal issue the facts raise within the call |
| crossover question | 跨科目题 | An essay combining two or more subjects |
| Performance Test (PT) | 实务操作考试 | 90-minute closed-universe lawyering task using a File and a Library |

## 2. Latin and law-French phrases

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| stare decisis | 遵循先例 | Courts follow precedent |
| ratio decidendi | 判决理由 | The reasoning necessary to the holding (British usage; US says "holding") |
| obiter dictum (dicta) | 附带意见 | Statements not necessary to the decision; not binding |
| res judicata | 既判力（请求排除） | Claim preclusion: a final judgment bars relitigating the same claim |
| res ipsa loquitur | 事实自证 | "The thing speaks for itself": inference of negligence from the type of accident plus defendant's control |
| respondeat superior | 雇主（上级）负责原则 | Employer vicariously liable for an employee's torts within the scope of employment |
| mens rea | 犯罪意图；主观罪过 | The guilty mental state |
| actus reus | 犯罪行为 | The voluntary act (or omission where there is a duty) |
| corpus delicti | 犯罪事实之客观要件 | Proof that a crime occurred, independent of the defendant's confession |
| prima facie | 初步的；表面成立 | Enough evidence to establish a claim unless rebutted |
| per stirpes | 按支分配 | Division by family branches; descendants of a dead child take that child's share |
| per capita | 按人头分配 | Equal shares per person at a given generation (variants: per capita at each generation) |
| inter vivos | 生前的 | During life (inter vivos gift or trust) |
| causa mortis | 死因（赠与） | Gift in contemplation of imminent death; revoked if donor survives |
| quantum meruit | 按劳计酬；合理价值 | Recovery of the reasonable value of services, usually in restitution |
| ultra vires | 越权 | Beyond the entity's powers; rarely a defense today |
| bona fide | 善意的 | In good faith (bona fide purchaser = BFP) |
| caveat emptor | 买者自慎 | Buyer beware; the traditional rule for land sales, now heavily limited |
| cy pres | 近似原则 | Court redirects a failed charitable gift to a similar charitable purpose |
| pro rata | 按比例 | Proportionately |
| pro tanto | 按相应比例 | "To that extent"; used for proportional CP/SP apportionment |
| de novo | 重新审查 | Appellate review with no deference (questions of law) |
| sua sponte | 依职权；法院主动 | The court acts on its own motion |
| voir dire | 陪审员资格审查 | Questioning prospective jurors (also preliminary questioning of a witness) |
| habeas corpus | 人身保护令 | Writ challenging the legality of detention |
| certiorari | 调卷令 | Discretionary writ by which the U.S. Supreme Court takes a case |
| mandamus | 职务执行令 | Writ ordering an official or lower court to perform a duty |
| amicus curiae | 法庭之友 | Non-party who files a brief |
| pro se | 自行诉讼（无律师） | Representing oneself |
| ex parte | 单方的 | Communication or proceeding with only one side present |
| in personam / in rem / quasi in rem | 对人 / 对物 / 准对物 | Bases of jurisdiction over a person, over property itself, or via property for an unrelated claim |
| forum non conveniens | 不方便法院原则 | Dismissal (or federal transfer) because another forum is far more convenient |
| nolo contendere | 不争辩答辩 | Criminal plea that does not admit guilt; treated as a conviction |
| ex post facto | 溯及既往的 | Retroactive criminal law; barred by U.S. Const. art. I, §§ 9–10 |
| ab initio | 自始 | From the beginning (void ab initio) |
| de facto / de jure | 事实上 / 法律上 | In fact vs by law (de facto corporation; de jure segregation) |
| in pari delicto | 双方同等过错 | Equal fault; courts leave the parties where they are |
| lis pendens | 未决诉讼登记 | Recorded notice that title to land is in litigation |
| quid pro quo | 交换条件；对价 | Something for something |
| per curiam | 法庭全体（不署名）意见 | An unsigned opinion of the court |
| inter alia | 除其他外 | Among other things |

## 3. Civil Procedure (CIV)

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| subject-matter jurisdiction | 事物管辖权 | The court's power over the type of case; cannot be waived |
| federal question jurisdiction | 联邦问题管辖 | Claims arising under federal law (28 U.S.C. § 1331) |
| diversity jurisdiction | 异籍管辖 | Complete diversity + amount in controversy over $75,000 (28 U.S.C. § 1332) |
| amount in controversy | 争议金额 | Diversity threshold; aggregation rules apply |
| supplemental jurisdiction | 补充管辖 | Related claims forming the same case or controversy (28 U.S.C. § 1367) |
| removal / remand | 移送 / 发回 | Defendant moves a case from state to federal court / it is sent back |
| personal jurisdiction | 对人管辖权 | Court's power over the defendant; due-process minimum contacts |
| general / specific jurisdiction | 一般管辖 / 特定管辖 | Defendant "at home" (any claim) vs claim arising from or relating to forum contacts |
| minimum contacts | 最低限度联系 | *International Shoe* test plus fair play and substantial justice |
| long-arm statute | 长臂管辖法 | State statute authorizing jurisdiction over non-residents (California: to constitutional limits) |
| venue | 审判地 | Which district or county; statutory (28 U.S.C. § 1391) |
| service of process | 送达 | Formal delivery of summons and complaint |
| pleading | 诉答文书 | Complaint, answer and replies |
| notice pleading / fact pleading | 通知式诉答 / 事实式诉答 | Federal plausibility standard vs California's requirement to plead ultimate facts |
| complaint / answer | 起诉状 / 答辩状 | Opening pleading / defendant's response |
| demurrer | 诉状异议（法律不足抗辩） | California challenge to the legal sufficiency of a pleading (≈ federal Rule 12(b)(6)) |
| motion to dismiss (Rule 12(b)(6)) | 驳回起诉动议 | Failure to state a claim upon which relief can be granted |
| counterclaim / crossclaim | 反诉 / 交叉请求 | Against an opposing party / against a co-party |
| impleader | 第三人引入诉讼 | Defendant brings in a third party who may be liable to it |
| intervention | 诉讼参加 | Non-party joins an existing suit |
| joinder | 合并（当事人或请求） | Adding parties or claims to one action |
| class action | 集团诉讼 | Representative suit; Rule 23 requirements |
| discovery | 证据开示 | Pre-trial exchange of information between parties |
| deposition | 庭外证言录取 | Oral questioning under oath before trial |
| interrogatories | 书面质询 | Written questions to a party, answered under oath |
| request for admission | 自认请求 | Asks a party to admit facts |
| work product | 律师工作成果 | Materials prepared in anticipation of litigation; qualified protection |
| summary judgment | 简易判决 | No genuine dispute of material fact; judgment as a matter of law |
| judgment as a matter of law (JMOL) / directed verdict | 依法判决 / 指示裁断 | No reasonable jury could find for the non-moving party |
| relation back | 修改溯及 | An amended pleading is treated as filed on the original date for limitations purposes |
| claim preclusion | 请求排除（既判力） | Bars a second suit on the same claim (federal: same transaction; California: primary rights) |
| issue preclusion (collateral estoppel) | 争点排除 | Bars relitigating an issue actually litigated and necessarily decided |
| final judgment rule | 终局判决规则 | Appeal generally lies only from a final judgment |
| interlocutory appeal | 中间上诉 | Appeal before final judgment; limited exceptions |
| standard of review | 审查标准 | De novo, clearly erroneous, abuse of discretion |
| *Erie* doctrine | 伊利原则 | Federal court in diversity applies state substantive law and federal procedure |
| anti-SLAPP motion | 反SLAPP动议 | California special motion to strike claims arising from protected speech or petitioning (CCP § 425.16) |

## 4. Constitutional Law (CON)

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| judicial review | 司法审查（违宪审查） | Courts may invalidate unconstitutional laws |
| case or controversy | 案件或争议 | Article III limit on federal judicial power |
| standing | 起诉资格 | Injury in fact, causation, redressability |
| ripeness / mootness | 成熟性 / 诉由消失 | Too early / too late for adjudication |
| political question | 政治问题 | Issue committed to the political branches; non-justiciable |
| Commerce Clause | 商业条款 | Congress regulates interstate commerce (art. I, § 8, cl. 3) |
| Dormant Commerce Clause | 休眠商业条款 | Implied limit on state laws that discriminate against or unduly burden interstate commerce |
| Necessary and Proper Clause | 必要和适当条款 | Congress may use means reasonably related to an enumerated power |
| spending power / taxing power | 开支权 / 征税权 | Congress may tax and spend for the general welfare; conditions on grants are limited |
| anti-commandeering | 反征用原则 | Congress may not compel states to enact or enforce federal programs |
| preemption | 联邦法优先 | Federal law displaces conflicting state law (Supremacy Clause) |
| state action | 国家行为 | Most constitutional rights restrict only government conduct |
| procedural due process | 程序性正当程序 | Notice and a hearing before deprivation of life, liberty or property |
| substantive due process | 实体性正当程序 | Protection of fundamental rights from government infringement |
| equal protection | 平等保护 | Classifications reviewed under rational basis, intermediate or strict scrutiny |
| strict scrutiny | 严格审查 | Necessary to a compelling interest; narrowly tailored; government bears the burden |
| intermediate scrutiny | 中度审查 | Substantially related to an important interest (e.g., sex classifications) |
| rational basis review | 合理依据审查 | Rationally related to a legitimate interest; challenger bears the burden |
| fundamental right | 基本权利 | Rights triggering strict scrutiny (e.g., voting, interstate travel, privacy aspects) |
| suspect classification | 可疑分类 | Race, national origin (and alienage, generally) → strict scrutiny |
| prior restraint | 事前限制 | Government restraint on speech before publication; heavily presumed invalid |
| content-based / content-neutral | 基于内容 / 内容中立 | Content-based → strict scrutiny; neutral time, place and manner rules → intermediate test |
| public forum | 公共论坛 | Traditional, designated, limited and non-public forums get different tests |
| overbreadth / vagueness | 过宽 / 模糊 | A speech law that reaches substantial protected speech / gives no fair notice |
| Establishment Clause | 禁止确立国教条款 | Government may not establish religion |
| Free Exercise Clause | 宗教自由行使条款 | Neutral, generally applicable laws usually valid; targeting religion → strict scrutiny |
| Takings Clause / just compensation | 征收条款 / 公正补偿 | Private property taken for public use requires just compensation |
| Contracts Clause | 契约条款 | States may not substantially impair existing contracts without adequate justification |
| incorporation | 并入（吸收） | Most Bill of Rights guarantees apply to states via the Fourteenth Amendment |
| sovereign immunity (Eleventh Amendment) | 州主权豁免 | Limits private damages suits against states in federal court |

## 5. Contracts and UCC Article 2 (CTR)

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| offer | 要约 | Manifestation of willingness to bargain that invites acceptance |
| acceptance | 承诺 | Assent to the offer's terms in a manner invited |
| counteroffer | 反要约 | A response with different terms; rejects the offer at common law |
| revocation / rejection | 撤回（撤销）要约 / 拒绝要约 | Offeror withdraws / offeree declines; both end the power of acceptance |
| option contract | 选择权合同 | Promise to keep an offer open, supported by consideration (or reliance) |
| firm offer | 商人确定要约 | Merchant's signed written offer, irrevocable without consideration (UCC § 2-205) |
| mailbox rule | 投邮生效规则 | Acceptance generally effective on dispatch ⚠ (PRC generally uses arrival) |
| mirror-image rule | 镜像规则 | Common law: acceptance must match the offer exactly |
| battle of the forms | 格式之争 | UCC § 2-207: an acceptance with different or additional terms can still form a contract |
| merchant | 商人 | One who deals in goods of the kind or holds out special knowledge (UCC) |
| goods | 货物 | Movable things; the trigger for UCC Article 2 |
| consideration | 对价 | Bargained-for exchange ⚠ no PRC equivalent requirement |
| pre-existing duty rule | 既存义务规则 | Promise to do what one is already bound to do is not consideration (common law) |
| illusory promise | 虚幻允诺 | A promise that commits to nothing; not consideration |
| requirements / output contract | 需求合同 / 产量合同 | Quantity measured by buyer's needs or seller's output, in good faith |
| promissory estoppel | 允诺禁反言 | Reasonable, foreseeable reliance on a promise makes it enforceable to prevent injustice |
| statute of frauds | 防止欺诈法 | Certain contracts need a signed writing (land, > 1 year, goods ≥ $500, etc.) |
| parol evidence rule | 口头证据规则 | Limits prior or contemporaneous extrinsic terms that contradict an integrated writing |
| integration / merger clause | 完整性条款 | Clause stating the writing is the complete agreement |
| condition precedent / subsequent | 先决条件 / 解除条件 | Event that must occur before a duty arises / event that cuts off a duty |
| express / constructive condition | 明示条件 / 推定条件 | Stated by parties / implied by law (e.g., order of performance) |
| material breach | 重大违约 | Breach that defeats the essence of the bargain; allows suspension and termination |
| substantial performance | 实质履行 | Common-law: minor deviation still entitles the party to the contract price minus damages |
| anticipatory repudiation | 预期违约 | Clear statement before performance is due that a party will not perform |
| adequate assurance | 充分履约保证 | Demand allowed on reasonable insecurity (UCC § 2-609) |
| perfect tender rule | 完美交付规则 | UCC buyer may reject goods that fail to conform in any respect, subject to cure |
| cure | 补救（瑕疵履行之补正） | Seller's right to fix a non-conforming tender in certain cases |
| warranty of merchantability | 适销性默示担保 | Implied in sales by a merchant: goods fit for ordinary purposes |
| warranty of fitness for a particular purpose | 特定用途适用性担保 | Implied when the seller knows the buyer's purpose and the buyer relies on the seller's skill |
| expectation damages | 期待利益损害赔偿 | Puts the plaintiff where performance would have |
| reliance damages | 信赖利益损害赔偿 | Reimburses expenses incurred in reliance |
| mitigation | 减损义务 | Plaintiff cannot recover avoidable losses |
| liquidated damages | 约定损害赔偿金 | Pre-agreed sum, enforceable if a reasonable forecast ⚠ courts don't adjust it like 违约金 |
| impracticability | 履行不可行 | Unforeseen event makes performance extremely burdensome; discharge |
| frustration of purpose | 合同目的落空 | Unforeseen event destroys the principal purpose; discharge |
| mutual / unilateral mistake | 共同错误 / 单方错误 | Mistake on a basic assumption; mutual is easier to use for avoidance |
| misrepresentation | 虚假陈述 | False assertion inducing assent; may make the contract voidable |
| duress / undue influence | 胁迫 / 不当影响 | Improper threat / unfair persuasion of a dependent party |
| unconscionability | 显失公平 | Procedural + substantive unfairness at formation |
| capacity | 缔约能力 | Minors, mental incapacity, intoxication |
| third-party beneficiary (intended / incidental) | 第三方受益人（意定 / 附带） | Only intended beneficiaries can enforce |
| assignment / delegation | 权利转让 / 义务委托履行 | Transfer of rights / appointment of another to perform duties (delegator stays liable) |
| novation | 合同更新（债务承担） | New contract substituting a party and releasing the original obligor |
| accord and satisfaction | 和解与清偿 | Agreement to accept substitute performance, and its performance |
| waiver | 弃权 | Voluntary relinquishment of a known right (e.g., a condition) |
| risk of loss | 风险负担 | Who bears loss of goods before receipt (UCC § 2-509, § 2-510) |

## 6. Criminal Law and Procedure (CRM)

| English term | 中文 (approx.) | One-line meaning / exam note |
|---|---|---|
| specific intent / general intent | 特定故意 / 一般故意 | Common-law categories; matter for defenses (voluntary intoxication, mistake) |
| purposely / knowingly / recklessly / negligently | 蓄意 / 明知 / 轻率 / 疏忽 | Model Penal Code culpability levels |
| strict liability offense | 严格责任犯罪 | No mens rea required for an element (e.g., statutory rape in many states) |
| malice aforethought | 预谋恶意 | Murder mental state: intent to kill, intent to cause serious bodily harm, depraved heart, or felony murder |
| depraved-heart murder | 极端轻率谋杀 | Extreme indifference to human life |
| felony murder | 重罪谋杀 | Killing during an inherently dangerous felony (California limits it: Pen. Code § 189(e)) |
| premeditation and deliberation | 预谋与深思 | First-degree murder (by statute) |
| voluntary manslaughter | 故意杀人（激情） | Intentional killing in adequately provoked heat of passion |
| involuntary manslaughter | 过失杀人 | Criminal negligence or misdemeanor-manslaughter |
| larceny | 盗窃 | Trespassory taking and carrying away of another's property with intent to permanently deprive |
| embezzlement | 侵占（挪用） | Fraudulent conversion of property lawfully possessed |
| false pretenses | 诈骗（取得所有权） | Obtaining **title** by a knowing false statement of fact |
| larceny by trick | 欺诈性盗窃 | Obtaining **possession** (not title) by deception |
| robbery | 抢劫 | Larceny from the person or presence by force or threat |
| burglary | 入室犯罪（夜盗） | ⚠ Not just theft: breaking and entering a dwelling at night with intent to commit a felony therein (common law) |
| arson | 纵火 | Malicious burning of the dwelling of another (common law) |
| receiving stolen property | 收受赃物 | Receiving property known to be stolen, with intent to deprive the owner |
| solicitation | 教唆（劝诱犯罪） | Asking another to commit a crime |
| conspiracy | 共谋 | Agreement + intent (+ overt act in many jurisdictions); separate crime |
| attempt | 犯罪未遂 | Specific intent + substantial step (MPC) or dangerous proximity (common law) |
| merger | 罪数合并 | Solicitation and attempt merge into the completed crime; conspiracy does not |
| accomplice | 共犯（帮助犯） | One who aids with intent; liable for the crime and foreseeable crimes |
| accessory after the fact | 事后帮助犯 | Helps a felon avoid arrest; separate, lesser offense |
| insanity (M'Naghten / irresistible impulse / MPC) | 精神失常抗辩 | Tests of mental-disease defense |
| intoxication (voluntary / involuntary) | 醉态（自愿 / 非自愿） | Voluntary: defense only to specific-intent crimes |
| self-defense | 正当防卫 | Reasonable belief of imminent unlawful force; proportional force |
| necessity / duress | 紧急避险 / 胁迫 | Choice of lesser evil / threat of death or serious harm (duress is no defense to murder) |
| entrapment | 警察诱陷 | Government induced the crime and the defendant was not predisposed |
| mistake of fact / mistake of law | 事实认识错误 / 法律认识错误 | Fact mistake may negate mens rea; law mistake rarely a defense |
| search / seizure | 搜查 / 扣押 | Fourth Amendment events |
| reasonable expectation of privacy | 合理隐私期待 | Test for whether a "search" occurred (also property-based trespass test) |
| probable cause | 相当理由 | Fair probability of crime or evidence; needed for warrants and arrests |
| reasonable suspicion | 合理嫌疑 | ⚠ Lower standard for a *Terry* stop. Do **not** confuse with "reasonable doubt" (合理怀疑). |
| warrant | 令状（搜查令 / 逮捕令） | Issued by a neutral magistrate on probable cause, with particularity |
| exigent circumstances | 紧急情况 | Warrantless entry or search exception |
| search incident to arrest | 附带于逮捕的搜查 | Search of the arrestee and immediate area |
| automobile exception | 汽车例外 | Probable cause allows a warrantless vehicle search |
