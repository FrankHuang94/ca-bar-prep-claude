## Scope
The State Bar's essay outline has six parts [S-085] [VERIFY V-024]:
1. **Agency**: formation; the principal-agent relationship; termination; contract liability. Tort liability (respondeat superior) is added here because essays test it.
2. **General partnerships**: formation (including partnership by estoppel); partner liability; partner rights; authority; dissociation and dissolution.
3. **Limited partnerships.**
4. **Limited liability partnerships.**
5. **Corporations**: veil piercing; formation (promoters, defective incorporation); directors' and officers' duties (care, BJR, loyalty, interested transactions, corporate opportunities, controlling shareholders); shareholders (voting, inspection, distributions, derivative suits); securities (Rule 10b-5, insider trading, § 16(b)); fundamental changes; close and nonprofit corporations.
6. **Other business structures**: LLCs, joint ventures, sole proprietorships.

The matrix adds one past-exam row: **insider shareholder loans and equitable subordination in bankruptcy** (Feb 2017 Q4).

## How it is tested
- **"Who is liable?"** calls that list debts or injuries one by one: "Who is liable for the Delta loan, the Echo loan, the Big Shoe contract, and Peter's injuries?" (Jul 2025 Q3); "Which defendants … might be held liable for the car accident?" (Jul 2023 Q1); "On what theories might each owner be personally liable to the landlord?" (Feb 2022 Q5).
- **Shareholder-suit** calls: "On what theories can the shareholders sue directors A and B, and director C, and what are the likely results?" (Jul 2024 Q1); dividend decision, inspection demand and a shareholder suit (Oct 2020 Q2); enjoining a change of business and suing the president (Jul 2022 Q4) [S-814].
- **Owner-liability / veil-piercing** calls: Feb 2012 Q4, Feb 2017 Q4, Jul 2026 Q4.
- **Entity-type** calls: "What type of entity is the law firm, and what is the status of its associates and senior attorneys?" (Feb 2020 Q5).
- **Most-tagged issues** ([`../../06-past-exams/frequency-analysis.md`](../../06-past-exams/frequency-analysis.md); tags [VERIFY V-071]): general partnerships 4; director duties (care/loyalty/BJR) 4; veil piercing 3; formation and promoters 3; corporations generally 3; agency 3; close corporations 2; derivative suits 2; LLPs 1; equitable subordination 1.
