# Business Associations — attack outline

One line per rule. Full text: [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md). Default: general law, plus one California sentence where marked **CA** ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md)).

## 0. Set-up (first 10 minutes)
- Headings from the call: one per debt/injury (liability calls), per defendant, or per director (shareholder-suit calls).
- Timeline: when was each obligation incurred, and when were articles filed?
- For every heading: rule → facts both ways → conclusion.

## I. Agency
- **Agency**: principal's manifestation + agent's consent + acting on behalf + principal's right to control (R3d § 1.01). (R-BUS-001)
- **Employee**: right to control manner and means; factors (R2d § 220). (R-BUS-002)
- **Actual authority**: agent reasonably believes, from principal's manifestations to agent, that principal wants the act; express or implied. (R-BUS-008)
- **Apparent authority**: third party reasonably believes, traceable to principal's manifestations to third party. **CA** "ostensible" (Civ. Code § 2317) + good-faith reliance (§ 2334). (R-BUS-009)
- **Ratification**: knowledge of material facts + affirmance (or accepting benefits); relates back; all-or-nothing. (R-BUS-010)
- **Estoppel / undisclosed principal**: careless creation of belief + reliance; undisclosed principal bound for usual acts. (R-BUS-011)
- **Agent on contract**: disclosed → agent not liable; unidentified or undisclosed → agent liable; no authority → implied warranty of authority. (R-BUS-012, R-BUS-013)
- **Termination**: either party's power; operation of law; apparent authority lingers until notice. (R-BUS-006, R-BUS-007)
- **Duties**: agent — loyalty (no secret profits, adverse dealing, competition, misuse of info), care, obedience, disclosure, accounting; principal — pay, indemnify. (R-BUS-003 to R-BUS-005)
- **Respondeat superior**: employer liable for employee's torts within scope (kind of work; time/space; purpose to serve). Detour in, frolic out; commuting out unless exception. **CA** "engendered by the employment" for intentional torts (*Lisa M.*). (R-BUS-014 to R-BUS-016)
- **Independent contractor**: no vicarious liability except nondelegable duty, ultrahazardous work, own negligence. (R-BUS-017)

## II. General partnerships
- **Formation**: two or more persons, carrying on as co-owners, a business for profit — intent irrelevant (§ 16202). Profit share presumes partnership unless debt, wages, rent, annuity, interest, goodwill. (R-BUS-021, R-BUS-022)
- **Estoppel**: represents or consents to representation as partner + reliance (§ 16308). (R-BUS-023)
- **Authority**: each partner binds for acts apparently in ordinary course, unless no authority **and** third party knew (§ 16301(1)); extraordinary acts need all partners. (R-BUS-025, R-BUS-026)
- **Torts**: partnership liable for partner's wrong in ordinary course (§ 16305). (R-BUS-027)
- **Liability**: all partners jointly and severally liable (§ 16306(a)); exhaust partnership assets first (§ 16307(d)); new partner not personally liable for prior debts. (R-BUS-028 to R-BUS-030)
- **Rights**: equal management; majority for ordinary, unanimity for extraordinary; equal profits; losses follow profits (**CA** *Kovacik* argument); no salary. (R-BUS-031, R-BUS-032)
- **Duties**: loyalty — account, no adverse dealing, no competition; care — no gross negligence, recklessness, intentional misconduct, knowing violation of law; good faith (§ 16404). (R-BUS-034, R-BUS-035)
- **Dissociation → buyout or dissolution**; at-will dissolves on notice of will to withdraw; winding up pays creditors first. (R-BUS-036 to R-BUS-039)

## III–IV. LPs and LLPs
- **LP**: filed certificate; GP unlimited liability; LP shielded — **CA** liable as GP only to those with actual knowledge of control who reasonably believe she is a GP (§ 15903.03). (R-BUS-040 to R-BUS-043)
- **LLP**: registration; partners not liable for partnership obligations, only own wrongs (§ 16306(c)); **CA** professions only + security. (R-BUS-044, R-BUS-045)

## V. Corporations
- **Limited liability**: shareholders risk only investment. (R-BUS-046)
- **Veil piercing**: unity of interest (undercapitalization, commingling, domination, no formalities) + fraud or injustice; easier for tort victims; active owners only. **CA** *Associated Vendors* factors; *Automotriz* undercapitalization. (R-BUS-047, R-BUS-048)
- **Own torts**: officer who participates is liable (*Frances T.*). (R-BUS-050)
- **Formation**: articles filed; existence on filing. (R-BUS-051)
- **De facto**: statute + good-faith attempt + use of corporate powers; MBCA § 2.04 liability if knew. **Estoppel**: contract creditor who dealt with "Inc." (R-BUS-052, R-BUS-053)
- **Promoter**: personally liable on pre-incorporation contract until novation; corporation liable only on adoption (express or implied); adoption ≠ release. Promoter owes fiduciary duty (no secret profits). (R-BUS-054 to R-BUS-056)
- **Ultra vires**: valid as to third parties; shareholder may enjoin; officers liable (§ 208). Donations allowed if reasonable (§ 207(e)). (R-BUS-057, R-BUS-058)
- **Board**: acts at meeting with quorum by majority present, or unanimous written consent; officers bind by agency. (R-BUS-059, R-BUS-060)
- **Care**: good faith, best interests, prudent person, reasonable inquiry; may rely on reports (**CA** § 309). (R-BUS-061)
- **BJR**: presumption informed, good faith, disinterested; rebut by fraud, illegality, conflict, bad faith, gross negligence, waste. (R-BUS-062)
- **Oversight**: liability only for no system or conscious disregard of red flags (*Caremark*). (R-BUS-063)
- **Loyalty / self-dealing**: safe harbor by disclosure + disinterested directors or shareholders, or fairness (MBCA §§ 8.60–8.63). **CA** § 310: board route also requires just and reasonable. (R-BUS-064 to R-BUS-066)
- **Corporate opportunity**: line of business / interest or expectancy / how learned; must offer first. (R-BUS-067)
- **Controllers**: no use of control for self alone (**CA** *Jones v. Ahmanson*); entire fairness for self-dealing (*Sinclair*, *Weinberger*). (R-BUS-071, R-BUS-072)
- **Exculpation**: care only; **CA** § 204(a)(10) excludes reckless disregard and pattern of inattention. (R-BUS-070)
- **Shareholders**: notice 10–60 days; quorum majority; **CA** cumulative voting on notice (§ 708). (R-BUS-073 to R-BUS-075)
- **Inspection**: proper purpose (**CA** § 1601); directors absolute (§ 1602). (R-BUS-078)
- **Dividends**: board discretion (BJR); legal limits — **CA** retained earnings or balance sheet + insolvency bar (§§ 500–501); MBCA equity insolvency + balance sheet; directors liable. (R-BUS-079, R-BUS-080)
- **Derivative**: who was harmed? → contemporaneous + continuous ownership (**CA** § 800(b)(1) exception) → demand or futility (MBCA universal demand; *Zuckerberg*) → **CA** bond up to $50,000 → recovery to corporation. (R-BUS-081 to R-BUS-084)
- **Fundamental changes**: board + shareholders (CA/DE: majority of outstanding shares; MBCA: votes for > against, majority quorum); dissenters get fair value; appraisal generally exclusive (CA § 1312: only a vote challenge unless one party controls, or is under common control with, another; *Steinberg*). (R-BUS-085, R-BUS-086)
- **10b-5**: material misstatement, scienter, purchase/sale, reliance, loss, loss causation — any security. (R-BUS-088)
- **Insider trading**: classical (insiders, temporary insiders: disclose or abstain); tippee (tipper's personal benefit + tippee knew); misappropriation (deceive source); Rule 14e-3 (tender offers). (R-BUS-089 to R-BUS-091)
- **§ 16(b)**: public company; officer/director/>10% holder; purchase and sale within < 6 months; strict liability; lowest-in highest-out. (R-BUS-092)
- **Close corporations**: **CA** § 158 (≤ 35), § 300(b) agreements, § 300(e) formalities; *Donahue/Wilkes* duties; § 1800 dissolution, § 2000 buyout. (R-BUS-094 to R-BUS-096)
- **§ 2115**: pseudo-foreign corporation; *VantagePoint* contra. (R-BUS-098)
- **Insider loan in bankruptcy**: recharacterize as equity? → equitable subordination (§ 510(c); inequitable conduct + harm + consistent with Code; *Pepper v. Litton*; Deep Rock) → 1-year insider preference. (R-BUS-099, R-BUS-100)

## VI. Other structures
- **LLC**: filed articles; shield; piercing like corporations (**CA** missed meetings not a factor unless required); **CA** statutory agency of members/managers; duties like partners; no professional LLCs in CA. (R-BUS-101 to R-BUS-105)
- **Joint venture**: partnership for one enterprise; shared profits/losses + joint control; partnership law applies. (R-BUS-106)
- **Sole proprietorship**: no entity; unlimited liability. (R-BUS-107)

## Closing check
Every defendant has a conclusion; every director has a care and a loyalty conclusion; procedure (direct/derivative) is addressed before the merits in shareholder suits.
