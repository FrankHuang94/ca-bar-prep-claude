# Business Associations — issue checklist

**How to use it.** Read the call first and build headings from it (one per debt, injury, defendant or director). Then run this list against the facts in order. Threshold issues come first because they decide everything after them (`00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md` §5: "entity liability before individual liability"). Rule IDs point to [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## A. Threshold: what is the business, and when?
| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Governing law** | Call says "California law"? Out-of-state corporation operating in California? | R-BUS-098; [`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md) |
| 2 | **Timeline** | Dates of contracts, loans and injuries vs. date articles were filed | — |
| 3 | **General partnership by default** | Two or more people "start a business," "pool resources," "share profits," "split management"; nothing filed | R-BUS-021 |
| 4 | Profit-sharing presumption / exceptions | Someone gets "a percentage of profits" as lender, employee, landlord or seller | R-BUS-022 |
| 5 | Partnership by estoppel | Letterhead or website lists someone as "partner"; someone says "we're partners" to a creditor | R-BUS-023 |
| 6 | Joint venture | Combining money and skill for one project; joint control | R-BUS-106 |
| 7 | LP / LLP / LLC status | Certificate, registration or articles filed? Law/accounting firm? | R-BUS-040, R-BUS-044, R-BUS-101 |
| 8 | **De jure corporation** | Articles filed; date of filing | R-BUS-051 |
| 9 | De facto corporation / corporation by estoppel | Articles rejected, lost or never sent; owners believed they had incorporated; creditor dealt with "X, Inc." | R-BUS-052, R-BUS-053 |

## G. Crossover checks
- **PR**: a lawyer representing the business or its owners → who is the client; conflicts; confidentiality; reporting up (R-BUS-109; R-PRF-055, R-PRF-056).
- **Remedies**: injunction (ultra vires; self-dealing), rescission, constructive trust, accounting (R-REM-044, R-REM-054, R-REM-055).
- **Torts**: the underlying tort's elements before vicarious liability (`03-subjects/torts/`).
- **Contracts**: formation and breach before asking who is liable on the contract (R-CTR-003).

**Last pass.** For every heading, did you (1) state the rule, (2) use specific facts on both sides, and (3) conclude for **each** defendant?
