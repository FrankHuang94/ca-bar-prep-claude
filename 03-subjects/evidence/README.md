## Scope
- **MBE** (NCBE outline [S-040]):
  - I Presentation of evidence (≈ 1/4 — about 6 scored questions);
  - II Relevancy and reasons for excluding relevant evidence (≈ 1/3 — about 8);
  - III Privileges and other policy exclusions and IV Writings, recordings and photographs (together ≈ 1/6 — about 4);
  - V Hearsay and circumstances of its admissibility (≈ 1/4 — about 6).
- **California essay outline** [S-083]: presentation of evidence (general provisions; testimonial evidence), relevance (including § 352, public-policy exclusions, character), privileges, hearsay and exceptions, and contents of writings — under both codes. Exact numbering unconfirmed [VERIFY V-024].
- **Current law built in:** FRE 106, 615, 702 (2023); 107, 613(b), 801(d)(2), 804(b)(3), 1006 (2024); **801(d)(1)(A) (Dec. 1, 2026)** [S-101]; CEC § 352.2 (2023); AB 2556 rape-shield extension [VERIFY V-030]. See `00-exam-intel/recent-changes.md` §§ 2 and 5.

**Typical shape.**
- The call lists **items** (statements, exhibits, testimony), so the answer goes **item by item** — one heading per item — and runs, for each: logical relevance → legal relevance (403 / § 352) → specific exclusionary rules (character, policy, privilege) → hearsay (definition → exclusion → exception, each layer) → confrontation if criminal → authentication and best/secondary evidence for writings → limiting instruction [VERIFY V-070]. See [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md).
- **Most-tested issues**: hearsay definition and exceptions (above all party admissions, business records, spontaneous statements), impeachment by prior conviction under California law, privilege (lawyer-client), and writings (authentication + BER / secondary evidence).
- In **California criminal** essays, Truth-in-Evidence (Prop 8) frames every item.

## Study order (Fall 2026 coursework in progress)
1. [`primer.md`](primer.md): the map and vocabulary (1 hour).
2. **Hearsay** (outline V; rules 083–109) — the biggest essay topic and a quarter of the MBE. Drill the definition until automatic, then the exceptions with the FRE/CEC differences.
3. **Relevance and character** (outline II.A, II.C; rules 002–004, 035–047) — a third of the MBE; Prop 8 and §§ 1101–1109 for essays.
4. **Impeachment and witnesses** (outline I.A–I.D; rules 009–033) — heavy on the MBE; prior convictions under California law for essays.
5. **Privileges and policy exclusions** (outline III; rules 062–077) — essays and PR crossovers.
6. **Writings and experts** (outline II.B, II.D, IV; rules 048–061, 078–082).
7. **Confrontation** (outline V.L; rules 110–115), studied with Criminal Procedure.
8. **California overlay** ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md)), then timed essays with [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md); spaced review with [`flashcards.csv`](flashcards.csv).
