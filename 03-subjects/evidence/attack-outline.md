# Evidence — attack outline

**Per item:** Relevance → 403/§ 352 → character/policy/privilege → witness/form → writings → hearsay (each layer) → confrontation → limiting instruction. **CEC criminal:** Prop 8 first.

## Framework
- **Prop 8 (CA criminal):** relevant evidence not excluded except hearsay, privilege, §§ 352, 782, 1103, two-thirds statutes (§ 1101 survives — Ewoldt). (R-EVD-004)
- **Relevance:** any tendency; fact of consequence (CEC: disputed). (R-EVD-002)
- **403 / § 352:** probative value substantially outweighed by unfair prejudice, confusion, misleading, delay/time. (R-EVD-003)
- **Limiting instruction:** on request (105 / § 355). (R-EVD-007)
- **Preserve:** timely specific objection; offer of proof. (R-EVD-008)

## I. Presentation
- **Personal knowledge:** evidence sufficient to support a finding (602 / § 702). (R-EVD-009)
- **Competency:** all competent; CEC § 701: understandable + duty of truth. (R-EVD-010)
- **Jurors:** only extraneous info, outside influence, form error (606(b)); CEC § 1150 overt acts. (R-EVD-011)
- **Refresh:** anything; not evidence; adverse party may introduce; CEC § 771 production mandatory, before or during. (R-EVD-012)
- **Lay opinion:** perception, helpful, not specialized (701 / § 800). (R-EVD-013)
- **Judicial notice:** indisputable; civil conclusive, criminal permissive (201). (R-EVD-014)
- **Presumptions:** FRE 301 production only; CEC § 606 public-policy presumptions shift persuasion. (R-EVD-015)
- **Criminal presumptions:** mandatory burden-shifting unconstitutional (Sandstrom). (R-EVD-016)
- **Leading:** not on direct (except hostile/adverse/preliminary); OK on cross. (R-EVD-019)
- **Scope of cross:** subject of direct + credibility. (R-EVD-018)
- **Exclusion of witnesses:** mandatory on request; exemptions; 2023 no-disclosure orders. (R-EVD-021)

## Impeachment
- **Any party** may impeach (607 / § 785). (R-EVD-022)
- **Bias:** always; extrinsic OK. (R-EVD-024)
- **PIS:** no need to show; extrinsic only after chance to explain (613(b) 2024) / or witness not excused (§ 770); substantive under 801(d)(1)(A) (2026) and § 1235. (R-EVD-023, R-EVD-085)
- **609:** felony — 403 (witness) / probative > prejudicial (accused); crimen falsi automatic; >10 yrs substantially outweighs + notice. (R-EVD-025)
- **CA convictions:** criminal — felony moral turpitude + § 352 (Castro), misdemeanor conduct (Wheeler); civil — felony § 788 + § 352. (R-EVD-026)
- **608(b):** specific untruthful acts on cross only; no extrinsic. CEC: civil barred (§ 787); criminal moral-turpitude conduct admissible (Prop 8). (R-EVD-027, R-EVD-028)
- **608(a):** reputation/opinion for truthfulness; support only after attack. (R-EVD-029)
- **Capacity:** perception, memory, communication. (R-EVD-030)
- **Hearsay declarant:** as if testified (806 / § 1202). (R-EVD-031)
- **Rehab:** prior consistent pre-motive (Tome) or other ground (FRE only). (R-EVD-032, R-EVD-086)
- **Contradiction:** extrinsic only if not collateral. (R-EVD-033)

## II. Relevance, character, authentication, experts
- **Propensity ban:** 404(a)(1) / § 1101(a). (R-EVD-035)
- **In issue:** any method (405(b)). (R-EVD-036)
- **Accused's trait:** D opens; reputation/opinion; prosecution rebuts. (R-EVD-037)
- **Victim's trait:** D opens; prosecution rebuts + D's same trait; homicide peacefulness; CEC § 1103 specific acts. (R-EVD-038)
- **Have you heard:** good faith; no extrinsic. (R-EVD-040)
- **Habit:** specific, regular, semi-automatic (406 / § 1105). (R-EVD-041)
- **404(b) / § 1101(b):** MIMIC; Huddleston; 403; notice; Ewoldt scale. (R-EVD-042)
- **413–415 / § 1108:** sex-offense propensity, 403. (R-EVD-043)
- **§ 1109:** DV propensity, § 352, 10 years. (R-EVD-044)
- **Rape shield:** 412 (source, with D, constitutional); CEC § 1103(c), § 782 procedure. (R-EVD-045, R-EVD-046)
- **§ 352.2:** creative expression — minimal literal-truth value. (R-EVD-047)
- **Authentication:** sufficient to support a finding (901 / §§ 1400–1401); methods; self-authenticating (902). (R-EVD-048 to R-EVD-050)
- **Photos/machines:** fair and accurate or reliable process; machine output not hearsay (Goldsmith; §§ 1552–1553). (R-EVD-051)
- **Chain of custody** for fungibles. (R-EVD-052)
- **Illustrative aids:** FRE 107 — not evidence. (R-EVD-053)
- **702 (2023):** qualified; helpful; sufficient facts; reliable method; reliable application — each by preponderance. (R-EVD-054)
- **703:** reasonably relied on; reverse 403 to disclose. **Sanchez:** no case-specific hearsay as true. (R-EVD-056, R-EVD-057)
- **704:** ultimate issue OK; not criminal mental state (704(b); Pen. Code § 29). (R-EVD-058)
- **Daubert/Kumho** vs. **Sargon + Kelly** (general acceptance for new science). (R-EVD-059, R-EVD-060)

## III. Privileges and policy
- **Source:** FRE 501 common law (state law in diversity) / CEC § 911 statutes. (R-EVD-062)
- **Attorney-client:** confidential + lawyer + legal advice; client holds; Upjohn / Chadbourne; crime-fraud; § 956.5. (R-EVD-063 to R-EVD-065)
- **Work product:** anticipation of litigation; substantial need; opinion absolute (CCP § 2018.030). (R-EVD-066)
- **Waiver:** 502 / § 912. (R-EVD-067)
- **Spousal testimonial:** Trammel (criminal; witness holds) / §§ 970–971 (all cases). (R-EVD-068)
- **Marital communications:** both hold; survives divorce. (R-EVD-069)
- **Physician (CA only):** not in criminal cases (§ 998). **Psychotherapist:** Jaffee / § 1014; § 1024 danger. (R-EVD-070, R-EVD-071)
- **Insurance:** not for fault; OK for bias, ownership, control. (R-EVD-073)
- **Repairs:** 407 incl. product defect / § 1151 not strict liability (Ault). (R-EVD-074)
- **Compromise:** disputed claim; offers + statements (408); CEC § 1152 adds humanitarian. (R-EVD-075)
- **Medical bills:** offer out, admissions in (409); CEC § 1160 sympathy out. (R-EVD-076)
- **Pleas:** withdrawn guilty, nolo, plea talks with prosecutor (410 / § 1153). (R-EVD-077)

## IV. Writings
- **BER:** original to prove content (1002); duplicates (1003); excuses (1004). (R-EVD-078, R-EVD-079)
- **CA secondary evidence rule:** copies OK unless genuine dispute + justice, or unfair (§ 1521); oral testimony limited (§ 1523). (R-EVD-080)
- **Summaries:** 1006 — underlying need not be admitted. (R-EVD-081)
- **Completeness:** 106 oral too, over hearsay / § 356. (R-EVD-082)

## V. Hearsay
- **Definition:** out-of-court statement offered for its truth (801(c) / § 1200). (R-EVD-083)
- **Not for truth:** verbal acts, effect on listener, state of mind circumstantially, impeachment. (R-EVD-084)
- **Prior statements (D testifies, subject to cross):** inconsistent (substantive, 2026 / § 1235); consistent (Tome / § 791); identification (§ 1238). (R-EVD-085 to R-EVD-087)
- **Party:** own; adopted (silence if reasonable to deny); authorized; employee within scope (FRE only; CA § 1222/§ 1224); coconspirator during + furtherance. (R-EVD-088 to R-EVD-091)
- **Multiple hearsay:** each layer. (R-EVD-092)
- **PSI** (FRE only; CA § 1241 own conduct). **Excited / spontaneous** (§ 1240). (R-EVD-093, R-EVD-094)
- **State of mind:** then-existing; Hillmon; not memory (Shepard); CA §§ 1251–1252. (R-EVD-095, R-EVD-096)
- **Medical:** 803(4) history, symptoms, cause, not fault; CA § 1253 child abuse <12 only. (R-EVD-097)
- **Recorded recollection:** once knew, now can't, made when fresh, accurate; read not received. (R-EVD-098)
- **Business records:** regular course + practice, near time, person with knowledge, custodian/cert; CA no opinions, proponent shows trustworthiness. (R-EVD-099)
- **Public records:** activities, duty-observed (not police vs. accused), civil findings. (R-EVD-100)
- **Treatise:** FRE substantive via expert; CA impeachment only (§ 721(b)). (R-EVD-101)
- **Unavailable:** privilege, refusal, memory, death/illness, absence. (R-EVD-102)
- **Former testimony:** opportunity + similar motive; CA § 1292 civil broader. (R-EVD-103)
- **Against interest:** when made; only self-inculpatory part; corroboration (criminal); CA social interest. (R-EVD-104)
- **Dying declaration:** FRE homicide/civil; CA any case, declarant died. (R-EVD-105)
- **Forfeiture:** wrongdoing intended to prevent testimony (Giles). (R-EVD-106)
- **Residual:** 807; CA none. **CA-only:** § 1370 threats; § 1360; § 1261. (R-EVD-107 to R-EVD-109)

## Confrontation (criminal)
- **Crawford:** testimonial + declarant absent → barred unless unavailable + prior cross. (R-EVD-110)
- **Primary purpose:** emergency = nontestimonial; past events to police = testimonial. (R-EVD-111)
- **Lab reports:** analyst must testify (Melendez-Diaz, Bullcoming); expert basis for truth (Smith; Sanchez). (R-EVD-112)
- **Bruton:** facially incriminating codefendant confession; redaction (Richardson, Gray, Samia). (R-EVD-113)
- **Face-to-face:** case-specific necessity (Craig; Pitts). (R-EVD-114)
- **Defense rights:** Chambers, Holmes, Rock. (R-EVD-115)
