# Evidence — issue checklist

Run this list **once per item** (statement, exhibit, question, ruling). California selected answers go item by item [VERIFY V-070]. Trigger facts are in *italics*.

## 0. Threshold (once per question)
- [ ] **Governing law** — the call: "Federal Rules of Evidence" vs. "California Evidence Code" / "California law" (R-EVD-001). *"Answer according to…"*
- [ ] **Civil or criminal?** Criminal + CEC → **Truth-in-Evidence** paragraph (R-EVD-004). Criminal (either code) → **Confrontation** for prosecution hearsay. *"People v.", "prosecution", "charged with"*
- [ ] **Forum and stage** — trial (rules apply) vs. sentencing, grand jury, preliminary hearing (R-EVD-034). *"At the sentencing hearing…"*
- [ ] **Who offers it, for what, against whom?** Write it down for each item before analysing.

## 1. Relevance
- [ ] **Logical relevance** — any tendency; fact of consequence; CEC: disputed fact (R-EVD-002). *Evidence about something conceded or remote.*
- [ ] **Legal relevance** — 403 / § 352 (R-EVD-003). *Gruesome photos, emotional testimony, wealth, gang membership, mini-trials, cumulative witnesses.*
- [ ] **§ 352.2** — *rap lyrics, poems, videos by the defendant* (R-EVD-047).

## 2. Character and other acts
- [ ] **Purpose?** In issue / propensity / impeachment / non-propensity (R-EVD-035, R-EVD-036). *"He's always been violent"; "she has a reputation for…"*
- [ ] **Mercy rule** — accused's trait; victim's trait; door opened to defendant's same trait; homicide first aggressor (R-EVD-037, R-EVD-038). *Defense character witnesses; self-defense.*
- [ ] **Method** — reputation/opinion vs. specific acts; CEC § 1103 specific acts (R-EVD-039). *"I know he once…"*
- [ ] **Cross of character witness** — "have you heard" (R-EVD-040).
- [ ] **Other acts** — 404(b) / § 1101(b) MIMIC; Huddleston; Ewoldt; notice (R-EVD-042). *Prior burglaries, prior frauds, similar method.*
- [ ] **Sex offense / DV propensity** — 413–415; §§ 1108, 1109 (R-EVD-043, R-EVD-044). *Prior assaults on partners; prior molestation.*
- [ ] **Habit / routine practice** (R-EVD-041). *"Every morning she…"; office mailing practice.*
- [ ] **Rape shield** — 412; §§ 1103(c), 782, 1106 (R-EVD-045, R-EVD-046). *Complainant's sexual history.*

## 3. Policy exclusions
- [ ] **Subsequent remedial measures** — 407 / § 1151; Ault in CA strict liability (R-EVD-074). *Repairs, new warnings, firing the employee after the accident.*
- [ ] **Compromise** — 408 / §§ 1152, 1154 (R-EVD-075). *"Let's settle this"; demand letters; negotiation emails.*
- [ ] **Medical expenses / humanitarian / sympathy** — 409 / §§ 1152, 1160 (R-EVD-076). *"I'll pay your bills"; "I'm so sorry".*
- [ ] **Pleas** — 410 / § 1153 (R-EVD-077). *Withdrawn guilty plea; plea talks with a DA.*
- [ ] **Liability insurance** — 411 / § 1155 (R-EVD-073). *"Don't worry, I'm insured"; insurer's investigator testifies.*

## 4. Privileges
- [ ] **Which law** — FRE 501 / CEC § 911 (R-EVD-062).
- [ ] **Attorney-client** — confidential, legal advice, holder, exceptions (crime-fraud; § 956.5), waiver (R-EVD-063 to R-EVD-065, R-EVD-067). *Lawyer called as witness; client's letters; third parties present; physical evidence given to lawyer.*
- [ ] **Work product** (R-EVD-066). *Investigator's report; lawyer's memo.*
- [ ] **Spousal** — testimonial and communications (R-EVD-068, R-EVD-069). *Spouse called by prosecution; divorce since.*
- [ ] **Physician / psychotherapist** — CA criminal exception; patient-litigant; dangerous patient (R-EVD-070, R-EVD-071). *Doctor or therapist subpoenaed.*
- [ ] **Other** — Fifth Amendment, clergy, informer, journalist, mediation (R-EVD-072).

## 5. Witness and form issues
- [ ] **Competency / oath** (R-EVD-010). *Child, mentally ill, intoxicated witness.*
- [ ] **Personal knowledge** (R-EVD-009). *"I heard that…"; "I assume…"*
- [ ] **Lay opinion** (R-EVD-013). *"He was drunk"; "the painting was worth $5,000"; "she was lying".*
- [ ] **Expert** — qualification, helpfulness, reliability (702 2023 / Sargon / Kelly), basis (703 / Sanchez), ultimate issue (704(b) / Pen. Code § 29) (R-EVD-054 to R-EVD-061). *New technique; expert relies on reports; "in my opinion, he intended…"*
- [ ] **Form of question** — leading, compound, argumentative, assumes facts, speculation, narrative, nonresponsive (R-EVD-019, R-EVD-020). *"Isn't it true…" on direct.*
- [ ] **Scope of cross** (R-EVD-018). **Exclusion of witnesses** (R-EVD-021). *Witness sat through testimony.*
- [ ] **Refreshing** vs. **recorded recollection** (R-EVD-012, R-EVD-098). *Witness reads notes; "I can't remember".*
- [ ] **Judicial notice** (R-EVD-014). *Calendar dates, geography.*

## 6. Impeachment and rehabilitation (for each witness)
- [ ] **Bias / interest** (R-EVD-024). *Plea deal, relationship, payment.*
- [ ] **Prior inconsistent statement** — foundation (613(b) / § 770) and substantive use (801(d)(1)(A) / § 1235) (R-EVD-023, R-EVD-085). *"But you told the officer…"*
- [ ] **Convictions** — 609 vs. California moral turpitude (R-EVD-025, R-EVD-026). *Prior forgery, assault, drug possession; age of conviction.*
- [ ] **Specific acts** — 608(b) vs. § 787 / Prop 8 (R-EVD-027, R-EVD-028). *Lied on a job application; insurance fraud.*
- [ ] **Character for truthfulness** (R-EVD-029). **Capacity** (R-EVD-030). **Contradiction / collateral** (R-EVD-033).
- [ ] **Hearsay declarant** (R-EVD-031).
- [ ] **Rehabilitation** — prior consistent statement timing (R-EVD-032, R-EVD-086).

## 7. Writings, recordings, photographs, objects
- [ ] **Authentication** — method fits item (R-EVD-048 to R-EVD-051). *Email, text, photo, signature, phone call, printout.*
- [ ] **Self-authentication** (R-EVD-050). *Certified copy, newspaper, label.*
- [ ] **Chain of custody / real evidence** (R-EVD-052). *Drugs, blood sample.*
- [ ] **Demonstrative / illustrative / experiment** (R-EVD-053). *Animations, models, re-enactments.*
- [ ] **BER (FRE) or secondary evidence rule (CEC)** — is content being proved? original/duplicate/excuse; § 1521, § 1523 (R-EVD-078 to R-EVD-080). *Photocopy; "the contract said…"; lost original.*
- [ ] **Summaries** (R-EVD-081). **Completeness** (R-EVD-082). *Party plays half a recording.*

## 8. Hearsay (for each statement and each layer)
- [ ] **Statement? Out of court? For its truth?** — or non-hearsay purpose (R-EVD-083, R-EVD-084). *Warnings, contracts, threats, "I am the King of Mars".*
- [ ] **Multiple hearsay** (R-EVD-092). *Reports quoting others.*
- [ ] **Prior statements of a testifying witness** (R-EVD-085 to R-EVD-087). *Lineup ID; earlier accounts.*
- [ ] **Party / adoptive / authorized / employee / coconspirator** (R-EVD-088 to R-EVD-091). *Driver's statement; silence when accused; conspirator's planning talk.*
- [ ] **803 (availability immaterial)** — PSI (CEC gap), excited/spontaneous, state of mind (+ § 1251), medical (CEC § 1253 only), recorded recollection, business, public, treatise (CEC gap) (R-EVD-093 to R-EVD-101). *911 calls, diaries, hospital and ATM records, police reports.*
- [ ] **804 (unavailability first)** — former testimony, against interest (+ social interest in CA), dying declaration (CA any case), forfeiture (R-EVD-102 to R-EVD-106). *Dead or absent declarant; invoked Fifth.*
- [ ] **807 (FRE) / California-only exceptions** — § 1370 threats, § 1360 child abuse, § 1261 decedent (R-EVD-107 to R-EVD-109). *Domestic-violence victim who died or vanished.*

## 9. Constitutional overlay (criminal)
- [ ] **Confrontation** — testimonial? declarant testifies? unavailable + prior cross? forfeiture? (R-EVD-110, R-EVD-111). *Statements to police, lab reports, affidavits.*
- [ ] **Forensic / expert basis** (R-EVD-112). **Bruton** in joint trials (R-EVD-113). **Face-to-face** with child witnesses (R-EVD-114).
- [ ] **Right to present a defense** (R-EVD-115). *Exclusion of reliable third-party confession.*

## 10. Close the item
- [ ] **Limiting instruction** on request (R-EVD-007).
- [ ] **Conclusion** for the item ("likely admissible for X only"); in a ruling question, say whether the court **erred** and whether an objection was **preserved** (R-EVD-008).
