# Wills and Succession — attack outline

One line per rule. Write the heading, then the one-liner, then apply. Full rules: [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## 0. Set-up (first 2 minutes)
- **Call:** claimant-by-claimant headings; threshold issues first.
- **Law:** California Probate Code.
- **Married?** Characterize every asset before distributing.

## I. Property at death
- **CP/QCP at death (§§ 100, 101, 6101):** survivor owns half of CP and half of decedent's QCP; decedent can will only SP + half CP + half own QCP (R-WIL-001).
- **QCP recapture (§ 102):** gift of QCP without consideration or written consent, with retained interest or survivorship → survivor recovers half (R-WIL-002).
- **Out-of-state land:** not QCP at death; situs law (§ 66).
- **Nonprobate first:** JT, POD/Totten, insurance, TOD deed, trust pass outside the will (§ 5000) (R-WIL-099).
- **Survival:** clear and convincing evidence (§§ 220–224); 120 hours only in intestacy (§ 6403) (R-WIL-007, R-WIL-020).
- **Slayer / abuser / disclaimant:** treated as predeceased (§§ 250, 259, 282) (R-WIL-012 to R-WIL-014).

## II. Validity
- **Who:** 18+, sound mind (§ 6100) (R-WIL-032).
- **Capacity (§ 6100.5):** understand act, property, relations at execution; lucid interval OK (R-WIL-033).
- **Insane delusion:** disorder + delusion + caused the gift (R-WIL-034).
- **Burden:** proponent proves execution; contestant proves defects (§ 8252) (R-WIL-035).
- **Conservatee:** keeps right to make a will (§ 1871(c)); conservator's will only by § 2580 order (R-WIL-036).
- **§ 6104:** duress, menace, fraud, undue influence → tainted parts fail (R-WIL-038).
- **Undue influence:** free will overborne; § 86 / W&I § 15610.70 factors (R-WIL-039).
- **Presumption:** confidential relationship + active procurement + undue benefit → burden shifts (*Rice v. Clark*) (R-WIL-040).
- **Disqualified donee (§ 21380):** drafter, fiduciary-transcriber, care custodian (incl. one who married/cohabited with the dependent adult) → presumed fraud/UI; clear and convincing to rebut; drafter conclusive (R-WIL-095).
- **Fraud:** execution vs inducement; reliance; constructive trust for prevented will (R-WIL-041).
- **Intent / conditional (§ 6105):** motive vs condition (R-WIL-043).

## III. Execution
- **Attested (§ 6110):** writing; testator signs; two witnesses present at same time; understand it's a will; sign in testator's lifetime (R-WIL-047).
- **Harmless error (§ 6110(c)(2)):** witnessing defect cured by clear and convincing evidence of intent at signing; not for unsigned wills (R-WIL-048).
- **Holographic (§ 6111):** signature + material provisions handwritten; form OK for intent (R-WIL-050).
- **Undated holograph:** fails only if inconsistent with another will (not proved later), or if incapacity was possible and execution while competent is not proved (R-WIL-051).
- **Interested witness (§ 6112):** presumption unless two disinterested witnesses; capped at intestate share (R-WIL-049).
- **Choice of law (§ 6113):** valid if valid where executed, domicile/abode/nationality, or California (R-WIL-053).
- **Extrinsic evidence (§ 6111.5):** on whether a document is a will and its meaning (R-WIL-052).

## IV. Contents
- **Integration:** pages present + intended (R-WIL-056).
- **Codicil:** executed as a will; republishes will as of its date (R-WIL-057).
- **Incorporation (§ 6130):** existing writing + intent + identification (R-WIL-058).
- **§ 6132 list:** tangible personal property; dated; handwritten or signed; $25,000/$5,000 (R-WIL-059).
- **Independent significance (§ 6131):** acts with non-testamentary purpose (R-WIL-060).
- **Pour-over (§ 6300):** identified trust; instrument before, with, or within 60 days after will; amendments count; revoked trust → lapse (R-WIL-061).
- **Contracts (§ 21700):** five proof methods; mutual wills no presumption (R-WIL-005, R-WIL-006).

## V. Revocation
- **Later will (§ 6120(a)):** express or inconsistency (R-WIL-077).
- **Physical act (§ 6120(b)):** burn, tear, cancel, obliterate, destroy + intent; by testator or another in presence and at direction (R-WIL-078).
- **Partial revocation:** allowed; new words need holograph or execution (R-WIL-079).
- **Duplicate (§ 6121):** act on one revokes (R-WIL-080).
- **Lost will (§ 6124):** last in possession + competent + not found → presumed revoked (burden of production) (R-WIL-081).
- **Divorce (§ 6122):** gifts and nominations to ex-spouse revoked; read as predeceased; § 5040 for nonprobate (R-WIL-082, R-WIL-083).
- **Revival (§ 6123):** act → circumstances/declarations; third will → its terms (R-WIL-084).
- **DRR:** revocation based on mistaken belief disregarded if closer to intent (*Kaufman*) (R-WIL-085).

## VI. Distribution under the will
- **Ambiguity:** provisional extrinsic evidence; resolve (*Russell*; § 21102) (R-WIL-044).
- **Reformation:** clear and convincing mistake in expression + actual intent (*Duke*) (R-WIL-046).
- **Lapse (§ 21109):** must survive (R-WIL-062).
- **Anti-lapse (§ 21110):** kindred of testator or spouse/ex-spouse → issue take; survivorship words defeat (R-WIL-063, R-WIL-064).
- **Failed gift (§ 21111):** alternative → residue → intestacy; residuary share to co-residuary takers (R-WIL-065).
- **Class gift / terms (§ 21115):** survivors share; adoptees by intestacy rules; third-party adoptee lived in household as minor (R-WIL-066, R-WIL-067).
- **Classification (§ 21117):** specific, general, demonstrative, residuary (R-WIL-068).
- **Ademption:** specific gift gone → adeemed unless traceable change in form (*Mason*), § 21133 unpaid balances, § 21134 conservator/agent/trustee sale (conservator: not if conservatorship ended and T survived 1 year) (R-WIL-069, R-WIL-070, R-WIL-074).
- **Securities (§ 21132):** splits, mergers and reinvestment shares follow the gift; pre-death cash dividends do not (R-WIL-071).
- **Satisfaction (§ 21135):** writing needed (R-WIL-072).
- **Exoneration (§ 21131):** none (R-WIL-073).
- **Abatement (§ 21402):** intestate → residue → general non-relatives → general relatives → specific non-relatives → specific relatives; pro rata (R-WIL-075).
- **No-contest (§ 21311):** direct contest without probable cause only (R-WIL-093).

## VII. Omitted heirs
- **Omitted spouse (§ 21610):** married after all instruments → decedent's half CP + half QCP + SP intestate share ≤ 1/2 (R-WIL-086).
- **Spouse exceptions (§ 21611):** intent on face; transfer in lieu; waiver; care-custodian spouse, decedent died within 6 months of marriage, unless clear and convincing rebuttal (R-WIL-087).
- **Omitted child (§ 21620):** born/adopted after all instruments → intestate share (R-WIL-090).
- **Child exceptions (§ 21621):** intent on face; substantially all to other parent; transfer in lieu (R-WIL-091).
- **Believed dead / unknown (§ 21622):** intestate share (R-WIL-092).
- **Funding (§§ 21612, 21623):** undisposed property, then pro rata (R-WIL-088).
- **Trusts count (§ 21601)** (R-WIL-089).

## VIII. Intestacy
- **Spouse (§ 6401):** all CP; decedent's half QCP; SP all / 1/2 / 1/3 (R-WIL-016). Not a spouse if divorced, or party to an order ending all marital property rights (legal-separation judgment) (§ 78; R-WIL-017).
- **Others (§ 6402):** issue → parents → parents' issue → grandparents' line → predeceased spouse's issue → next of kin (R-WIL-018).
- **Representation (§ 240):** divide at first generation with a living member (R-WIL-011).
- **Half-blood (§ 6406):** equal (R-WIL-022).
- **Adoption (§ 6451):** severs natural parent unless lived together + stepparent/after-death adoption (R-WIL-027).
- **Stepchild (§ 6454):** minority + joint lifetimes + legal barrier to death, clear and convincing (R-WIL-029).
- **Equitable adoption (§ 6455):** intent to adopt, clear and convincing (*Ford*) (R-WIL-030).
- **Parent barred (§ 6452):** termination, non-acknowledgment, seven-year abandonment (R-WIL-028).
- **Advancement (§ 6409):** writing required (R-WIL-024).
- **Escheat (§ 6404)** (R-WIL-021).
