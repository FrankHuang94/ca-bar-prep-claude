## Scope

Effect of death on community and quasi-community property (cross-referenced to Community Property); spousal waivers and contracts concerning wills; simultaneous death and survival; intestate succession (spouse's share, other heirs, representation, half-bloods, adopted, step- and foster children, equitable adoption, advancements, escheat); capacity, undue influence, fraud, duress and the disqualified-donee statute; execution of attested, holographic and statutory wills, harmless error, interested witnesses and choice of law; integration, codicils and republication, incorporation by reference, the § 6132 list, acts of independent significance and pour-overs; lapse and anti-lapse, class gifts, classification, ademption, satisfaction, exoneration, conservator sales and abatement; revocation, revival and dependent relative revocation; omitted spouses and children; no-contest clauses; will substitutes and nonprobate transfers.

## How it is tested

- **Format.** A one-hour essay, usually "What rights do A, B and C have in T's estate? Answer according to California law." Answers are organized **claimant by claimant**, after a threshold section on the validity of the will and (if married) characterization of property [`grader-expectations.md` §5](../../00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md).
- **Most-tested issues** (Phase 3 tags; indicative only [VERIFY V-071]): execution — attested, holographic, choice of law (5); omitted spouse (3); omitted children (3); adopted and stepchildren (2); CP/QCP at death (2 plus CP crossovers); revocation and revival (2).
- **Past Wills essays** (by administration): 2012-02 Q1 · 2012-07 Q5 · 2013-07 Q5 · 2015-02 Q6 · 2016-02 Q1 · 2017-02 Q1 · 2018-02 Q5 · 2019-02 Q1 · 2020-10 Q3 · 2021-07 Q5 · 2022-07 Q5 · 2025-02 Q2 · 2026-07 Q2. See [`../../06-past-exams/index.csv`](../../06-past-exams/index.csv).

**PRC bridge note.** Your PRC training helps with the structure (statutory succession vs. testamentary succession; holographic and attested wills). The big differences: California has **no first-order sharing** among spouse, children and parents; it has **no necessary share** for dependent heirs; and it relies heavily on **salvage doctrines** (harmless error, anti-lapse, DRR, reformation) to carry out presumed intent. Glossary terms are in [`../../02-foundations/legal-english-glossary.md`](../../02-foundations/legal-english-glossary.md).
