# Wills and Succession — essay templates

Seven skeletons for the question types California has asked (Phase 3 index, indicative only [VERIFY V-071]). Each gives the headings, the paragraph pattern, and the timing. The paragraph pattern for every issue is the repo's IRAC: **rule (1–3 sentences from [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md)) → facts applied, both sides → "likely" conclusion** ([`../../00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md`](../../00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md) §4).

**Timing for a one-hour essay.** 10–15 minutes reading and outlining, 45–50 minutes writing. Spend the most words on the issue the facts develop most (usually execution, an omitted heir, or revocation/DRR).

---

## Template 1 — "What rights do A, B and C have in T's estate?" (the classic)

Used in: Jul 2013 Q5, Feb 2017 Q1, Feb 2018 Q5, Jul 2021 Q5, Jul 2022 Q5.

```
Preliminary: Governing law and characterization
  - California law; T domiciled in CA at death.
  - [If married] CP/QCP/SP characterization; survivor's half (§§ 100, 101, 6101).
Validity of the will
  - Attested will (§ 6110) → harmless error (§ 6110(c)(2)) → holograph (§ 6111) → § 6113.
  - Capacity / undue influence / fraud (only if facts raise them).
Contents and later changes
  - Codicil and republication; handwritten changes; incorporation; § 6132.
Revocation (if any)
A's rights
  - Under the will (lapse/anti-lapse, ademption, abatement as needed).
  - Statutory claim (omitted spouse / child) with exceptions.
  - Share in numbers.
B's rights (same pattern)
C's rights (same pattern)
Conclusion: table of who takes what
```

**Tips.** Put validity once, before the claimants, then refer back ("as discussed, the will is a valid holograph"). End with a short distribution table.

## Template 2 — Community property + Wills crossover (married decedent, moved from State X)

Used in: Feb 2019 Q1, Jul 2022 Q5, Jul 2026 Q2 (with CP).

```
I. Characterization of each asset at death (asset by asset)
   - General presumption; QCP definition at death (§ 66): personal property anywhere + CA land only.
   - Out-of-state land → situs law.
   - Title, JT form, transmutation (§ 852) where relevant.
II. Effect of death
   - Survivor owns half of CP and half of decedent's QCP (§§ 100, 101).
   - Recapture of QCP transferred without consent (§ 102).
III. Validity and terms of the will (or intestacy)
IV. Distribution
   - Under the will: only decedent's SP + half CP + half QCP (§ 6101).
   - Intestate: § 6401 spouse's share; § 6402 others.
   - Omitted spouse if married after the will (§ 21610).
V. Each claimant's total (survivor: own half + inheritance)
```

**Tips.** Do the CP work asset by asset (see [`../community-property/essay-templates.md`](../community-property/essay-templates.md)), then switch to Wills. Cite R-CMP-091 to R-CMP-097 for the death rules.

## Template 3 — Altered, torn or replaced will (revocation, revival, DRR)

Used in: Jul 2012 Q5, Feb 2025 Q2.

```
1. Was the original will validly executed? (short)
2. The alteration / new document
   a. Valid holographic codicil? (signature + material provisions handwritten)
   b. Valid attested codicil / harmless error?
3. Revocation
   a. By later instrument (§ 6120(a)) – express or inconsistency
   b. By physical act (§ 6120(b)) – act + intent + who/presence
   c. Partial revocation of the struck gift
   d. Missing will presumption (§ 6124) if applicable
4. Revival (§ 6123) – act vs third will
5. Dependent relative revocation – mistaken belief; closer to intent?
6. Result: what each beneficiary takes
```

**Tips.** Always run DRR after a failed new gift. Say who made the marks; marks by a beneficiary are ineffective.

## Template 4 — Omitted spouse and omitted child

Used in: Feb 2012 Q1 (trust), Jul 2013 Q5, Feb 2018 Q5, Jul 2021 Q5.

```
Spouse S
  1. Timing: married after ALL testamentary instruments (will, codicils, trust)? Republication?
  2. Not provided for?
  3. Exceptions (§ 21611): intent on face; transfer in lieu; waiver (§§ 140-147)
  4. Share (§ 21610): decedent's 1/2 CP + 1/2 QCP + SP intestate share ≤ 1/2 – compute
  5. Funding (§ 21612)
Child C
  1. Born/adopted after ALL instruments? (or § 21622 believed dead/unknown)
  2. Exceptions (§ 21621): intent on face; substantially all to other parent; transfer in lieu
  3. Share (§ 21620): intestate share of whole estate – compute (spouse takes all CP in intestacy)
  4. Funding (§ 21623)
Other children (alive at execution): no omitted-child claim; take under will only
Adopted / step / foster children: §§ 6450-6455 status first
```

**Tips.** Show the arithmetic. A child alive at execution and simply left out has no claim (except § 21622).

## Template 5 — Will contest (capacity, undue influence, disqualified donee, no-contest clause)

Used in: Feb 2019 Q1 (codicil benefiting a conservator).

```
1. Burden of proof (§ 8252)
2. Capacity (§ 6100.5) – three prongs at execution; insane delusion
   [Conservatee: § 1871(c); conservator's will needs § 2580 order]
3. Undue influence
   a. Definition (Rice; § 86 / W&I § 15610.70 factors)
   b. Common-law presumption: confidential relationship + active procurement + undue benefit
   c. Statutory presumption (§ 21380): drafter / fiduciary-transcriber / care custodian
      - exceptions (§ 21382), certificate (§ 21384), effect (§ 21386)
   d. Interested witness (§ 6112) if the beneficiary witnessed
4. Fraud / duress / menace (§ 6104)
5. Remedy: only tainted gifts fail; who takes instead (residue; prior will via DRR)
6. No-contest clause (§ 21311): direct contest? probable cause? forfeiture?
```

## Template 6 — Distribution problems (beneficiary died, property gone, estate short)

Used in: Feb 2015 Q6 (conservatorship), Feb 2018 Q5 (gift to employees).

```
For each gift:
  1. Classify (§ 21117): specific / general / demonstrative / residuary
  2. Beneficiary survived? (§ 21109; §§ 220-224 if simultaneous)
     - Anti-lapse (§ 21110): kindred? issue? contrary intent?
     - Failed gift (§ 21111): alternative → residue → intestacy
     - Class gift: survivors share; § 21115 for adoptees/stepchildren
  3. Property still there? (specific gifts only)
     - § 21132 securities; § 21133 unpaid balances; § 21134 conservator/agent sale
     - Mason: traceable change in form, intent
     - Satisfaction (§ 21135)
  4. Enough money? Abatement (§ 21402) with numbers
  5. Liens: no exoneration (§ 21131)
```

## Template 7 — Wills + Trusts crossover

Used in: Feb 2012 Q1, Jul 2013 Q5, Feb 2015 Q6, Feb 2016 Q1, Feb 2019 Q1, Feb 2025 Q2.

```
Part A – Wills issues
  - Validity of will; pour-over to the trust (§ 6300: instrument before/with/within 60 days;
    amendments; revoked trust → lapse)
  - Omitted spouse/child from will AND trust (§ 21601)
Part B – Trusts issues (see Trusts outline)
  - Valid trust; revocation/modification (Haggerty); trustee duties; charitable trust / cy pres
  - Creditors' rights in revocable trust property
Part C – Interaction
  - Does property pass by will (probate) or by trust (nonprobate)?
  - Disqualified donee / undue influence apply to both instruments
```

**Tips.** Label which instrument controls each asset. The Trusts subject has its own templates; cross-reference rather than repeat ([`../trusts/README.md`](../trusts/README.md)).
