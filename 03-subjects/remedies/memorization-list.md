## Mnemonics (original)
- **Order of attack:** **L-R-E-D-B** — Legal, Restitution, Equitable, Defenses, Best remedy.
- **Damages limits:** **C-F-C-M** — Causation, Foreseeability, Certainty, Mitigation.
- **Permanent injunction:** **I-P-F-B-D** — "I Prefer Fair, Balanced Decrees": Inadequate remedy, Protectable interest, Feasibility, Balancing, Defenses.
- **Specific performance:** **CCIFD** — Contract certain, Conditions met, Inadequate remedy, Feasibility, Defenses (same as Contracts).
- **Preliminary injunction (federal):** **S-H-E-P** — Success likely, Harm irreparable and likely, Equities balance, Public interest.
- **Constructive trust vs. lien:** "**Up → trust, down → lien.**"
- **Equitable defenses:** **"Lazy Unclean Partners Eat Wasted Hard Candy"** — Laches, Unclean hands, in Pari delicto, Estoppel, Waiver, Hardship, Changed circumstances (+ unconscionability).
