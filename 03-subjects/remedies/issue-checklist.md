# Remedies — issue checklist

Run this list **claim by claim** (Π v. Δ1, Π v. Δ2, cross-claims) in the order below. Threshold items come first. Rule IDs point to [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## 0. Threshold (2 minutes, before writing)
| Check | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Read the call.** Does it name specific remedies (TRO, PI, SP, damages, declaratory relief)? Then answer only those, in that order | "Can Sam obtain a TRO, a preliminary injunction and specific performance?" | R-REM-006 |
| **Identify each claim** and its underlying liability (contract breach, fraud, trespass, conversion, nuisance, constitutional violation) | Several parties; a Torts or Con Law liability call before the remedies call | R-REM-001 |
| **Governing law.** General principles unless the call says California; UCC for goods | "Under California law…"; sale of a painting, mower, chess piece | R-REM-002; R-CTR-001 |
| **What does Π actually want?** Money, the thing itself, Δ's profits, an order, a ruling | Π "wants the painting back," "wants the fence removed," "wants to stop the auction" | R-REM-001 |

## 1. Legal remedies — damages (outline II)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Compensatory damages: general vs. special | Dollar figures, repair bills, lost profits, medical costs | R-REM-007 |
| Contract measure (expectation, reliance, restitution) | Breach of a contract with a price and a market value | R-REM-014; R-CTR-089 to R-CTR-109 |
| Land-sale damages | Seller refuses to close; buyer backs out; market value moved | R-REM-015, R-REM-016 |
| Tort measures: personal injury, harm to land, conversion, trespass to chattels | Injury; damaged garden or trees; taken or damaged chattel | R-REM-018 to R-REM-022 |
| Fraud: out-of-pocket vs. benefit of the bargain (CA § 3343) | Lies about authenticity, condition, zoning, flooding | R-REM-023 |
| Nuisance: permanent vs. continuing | Noise, smoke, odor, ongoing interference | R-REM-024 |
| Emotional distress | Humiliation, anguish; emotionally sensitive contract | R-REM-025 |
| **Limits:** causation, foreseeability (*Hadley* / proximate cause), certainty (new business, speculative profits), mitigation | Lost profits; "new" business; Π waited or did nothing; Δ did not know of the special use | R-REM-008 to R-REM-011 |
| Collateral source; present value | Insurance paid; permanent injury | R-REM-012, R-REM-013 |
| Liquidated damages clause | "Seller keeps the deposit"; "$500 per day of delay" | R-REM-017 |
| Punitive damages: tort + aggravated conduct; CA § 3294; due process | Deliberate lies, threats, knowing destruction, vulnerable victim; large punitive figure | R-REM-026 to R-REM-031 |
| Economic loss rule (tort vs. contract) | Contracting parties; fraud during performance | R-REM-032 |
| Nominal damages | Trespass or breach with no proven loss | R-REM-094 |
| Fees, interest, statutory multiples (CA § 3346 trees) | Fee clause; liquidated sum overdue; trees cut | R-REM-020, R-REM-033, R-REM-034 |

## 2. Restitution (outline III)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose and measure: Δ's gain (innocent vs. conscious wrongdoer) | Δ profited more than Π lost; Δ resold at a higher price | R-REM-035, R-REM-036 |
| Disgorgement ("waiver of tort") | Δ used Π's property and saved money or made profits | R-REM-037 |
| Quasi-contract / quantum meruit | Services or goods with no enforceable contract; contract unenforceable; Π in breach | R-REM-038, R-REM-039 |
| Volunteer / officious intermeddler | Π helped Δ without being asked | R-REM-040 |
| Replevin (claim and delivery) | A specific chattel still in Δ's hands | R-REM-041 |
| Ejectment | Δ occupying Π's land | R-REM-042 |
| Rescission + restitution (legal; CA §§ 1689–1692) | Fraud, mistake, duress; Π "wants out" | R-REM-043 |
| **Constructive trust** | Δ bought an asset with money or property obtained by fraud, theft, embezzlement, breach of fiduciary duty; asset rose in value; Δ insolvent | R-REM-044 |
| **Equitable lien** | Asset fell in value; Π's money improved Δ's house or paid part of the price | R-REM-045, R-REM-046 |
| **Tracing**; commingled account | "Deposited the money in her checking account," later withdrawals and deposits | R-REM-047, R-REM-048 |
| **BFP / donee / change of position** | Δ gave the asset to a relative; sold it to a stranger; recipient spent the money | R-REM-049 |
| Resulting trust | One person paid, title in another's name | R-REM-050 |
| Subrogation | Stolen money paid off Δ's mortgage or debt | R-REM-051 |
| Indemnity / contribution | Employer paid for employee's tort; joint tortfeasors | R-REM-052, R-REM-053 |
| Accounting | Partner, trustee or agent diverted profits | R-REM-054 |

## 3. Equitable remedies (outline IV)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **TRO** (ex parte, ≤ 14 days federal; CA 15/22-day OSC) | Imminent sale, auction, demolition, tree cutting "tomorrow" | R-REM-065, R-REM-067 |
| **Preliminary injunction** (*Winter*; CA *IT Corp.*) | Need to hold things in place until trial; status quo | R-REM-066, R-REM-067, R-REM-069 |
| Bond / undertaking | Any TRO or PI | R-REM-068 |
| **Permanent injunction** (I-P-F-B-D) | Ongoing trespass, encroachment, nuisance, threatened destruction | R-REM-055 to R-REM-060 |
| Encroachment: relative hardship / equitable easement | Fence, wall or garage over the line; innocent mistake; costly removal | R-REM-061 |
| Nuisance injunction alternatives | Theater noise, factory dust; Π came to the nuisance | R-REM-062 |
| Speech | Δ's statements about Π; protest | R-REM-063 |
| Personal services | Artist, performer, employee refuses to work | R-REM-064 |
| **Specific performance** (CCIFD) | Land; unique goods (art, chess piece, heirloom); Π ready to pay | R-REM-070 to R-REM-072 |
| CA § 3387 presumption | Single-family home the buyer will live in | R-REM-071 |
| Abatement; lis pendens | Acreage shortfall; seller may sell to someone else | R-REM-073, R-REM-074 |
| **Reformation** | Writing omits or misstates an agreed term (painting, mineral rights, road, coverage limit) | R-REM-075 |
| Rescission (equitable); insurance rescission (Ins. Code §§ 331, 359); cancellation | Misstatement in an insurance application; fraudulently obtained deed | R-REM-043, R-REM-076, R-REM-077 |
| Contempt; who is bound | Δ ignores an order; third parties act for Δ | R-REM-078 to R-REM-080 |

## 4. Equitable defenses (outline V)
| Defense | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Laches | Π waited months or years after learning of the problem; Δ built or spent meanwhile | R-REM-081, R-REM-090 |
| Unclean hands | Π lied, bribed, threatened or used self-help in the same deal | R-REM-082 |
| In pari delicto | Both parties in an illegal scheme | R-REM-083 |
| Estoppel / waiver | Π said "fine," accepted premiums or late performance | R-REM-084, R-REM-085 |
| Undue hardship | Removal or performance would cost Δ far more than it benefits Π; Δ acted in good faith | R-REM-086 |
| Unconscionability / inadequate consideration (CA § 3391) | Very low price; elderly or confused seller | R-REM-087 |
| Changed circumstances | Rezoning, new statute, years after decree | R-REM-088 |
| Contract defenses to SP | No writing, mistake, misrepresentation, impossibility, sale to BFP | R-REM-089 |

## 5. Declaratory remedies (outline VI)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Declaratory judgment; actual controversy | Constitutional challenge to a law; dispute over a contract term before breach | R-REM-091, R-REM-092 |
| Quiet title / cancellation | Forged or fraudulent deed; disputed boundary | R-REM-077, R-REM-093 |

## 6. Wrap-up (every answer)
- **Election and best remedy:** which combination maximizes recovery without double counting (R-REM-005)?
- **Conclusion** for each remedy, hedged ("likely").
