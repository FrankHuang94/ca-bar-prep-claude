# Remedies — attack outline

**Order for each claim:** LEGAL (damages) → RESTITUTION (legal, then equitable) → EQUITY (TRO → PI → permanent injunction / SP; reformation; rescission) → DEFENSES → BEST REMEDY. If the call names remedies, follow the call. Rule IDs in brackets.

## I. Legal remedies (damages)
- **Compensatory** [R-REM-007]: money to put Π where it would have been absent the wrong (tort) or with performance (contract). General vs. special (consequential).
- **Limits** [R-REM-008 to R-REM-011]:
  - *Causation:* but-for / substantial factor; proximate cause in tort.
  - *Foreseeability:* contract — natural consequences or special circumstances known at contracting (*Hadley*); tort — proximate cause (CA § 3333 "whether it could have been anticipated or not").
  - *Certainty:* fact of loss certain, amount reasonable; new-business profits need reliable proof; fallback to reliance.
  - *Mitigation:* no recovery for avoidable loss; Δ's burden; mitigation costs recoverable.
- **Contract measures** [R-REM-014; R-CTR-089 ff.]: expectation = loss in value + incidental + consequential − cost avoided. Land [R-REM-015/016]: market − contract (buyer); contract − market (seller).
- **Tort measures:**
  - Personal injury [R-REM-018]: economic + non-economic; eggshell plaintiff.
  - Land [R-REM-019]: lesser of diminution or restoration; personal-reason exception (*Heninger*).
  - Trespass [R-REM-020]: nominal + harm + rental value; CA § 3346 trees ×3 (×2 if casual/involuntary).
  - Conversion [R-REM-021]: FMV at conversion + interest (forced sale).
  - Fraud [R-REM-023]: out-of-pocket vs. benefit of bargain; CA § 3343 out-of-pocket in property deals.
  - Nuisance [R-REM-024]: permanent (diminution) vs. continuing (past harm + injunction).
- **Liquidated damages** [R-REM-017]: hard to estimate + reasonable forecast; CA § 1671(b) presumed valid.
- **Punitive** [R-REM-026 to R-REM-031]: tort only; malice, oppression, fraud; CA § 3294 clear and convincing; employer needs officer/director/managing agent; actual damages + Δ's financial condition (*Adams*); due process — reprehensibility, ratio (single digit), comparable penalties (*Gore*, *State Farm*).
- **Nominal** [R-REM-094]: right violated, no loss.

## II. Restitution
- **Purpose** [R-REM-035]: prevent unjust enrichment; measure Δ's gain. Innocent Δ → value of benefit; wrongdoer → all profits [R-REM-036, R-REM-037].
- **Legal:**
  - Quasi-contract [R-REM-038]: benefit + knowledge/acceptance + unjust retention; no contract.
  - Quantum meruit [R-REM-039]: reasonable value of services.
  - Replevin [R-REM-041]: get the chattel back (CA claim and delivery, writ of possession on probable validity).
  - Ejectment [R-REM-042]: get the land back + mesne profits.
  - Rescission + restitution [R-REM-043]: ground + notice + restore; CA § 1692 damages too.
- **Equitable:**
  - Constructive trust [R-REM-044]: Δ holds title + wrongful acquisition + traceable + inadequate legal remedy → Π gets asset and gains; priority over unsecured creditors.
  - Equitable lien [R-REM-045]: security interest + deficiency judgment; use when asset dropped in value or Π's money improved Δ's property [R-REM-046].
  - Tracing [R-REM-047/048]: follow exchanges; commingled → lowest intermediate balance; Δ spends own money first.
  - BFP cuts off; donee does not; change of position for innocent recipients [R-REM-049].
  - Resulting trust; subrogation; indemnity; contribution; accounting [R-REM-050 to R-REM-054].

## III. Equitable remedies
- **TRO** [R-REM-065, R-REM-067]: immediate irreparable harm before a hearing; notice or certified reasons; federal ≤ 14 days; CA OSC within 15 (22) days; bond.
- **Preliminary injunction** [R-REM-066, R-REM-067]: *Winter* — likely success, likely irreparable harm, balance of equities, public interest; CA *IT Corp.* — likelihood of prevailing + relative interim harm, sliding scale; bond [R-REM-068]; status quo; mandatory PIs disfavored [R-REM-069].
- **Permanent injunction** [R-REM-055 to R-REM-060] — **I-P-F-B-D:**
  - *Inadequate legal remedy:* unique, irreparable, speculative, multiplicity, insolvency.
  - *Protectable interest:* property or other legal right.
  - *Feasibility:* negative easy; mandatory/supervision hard; personal services never.
  - *Balancing:* Π's harm vs. Δ's and public's; no balancing for willful Δ.
  - *Defenses:* see V.
  - Federal: *eBay* four factors.
- **Settings:** encroachment → equitable easement for innocent encroacher (*Hirshfield*) [R-REM-061]; nuisance → partial injunction / *Boomer* damages / *Spur* compensated injunction [R-REM-062]; speech → no prior restraint, CA post-trial exception (*Balboa Island*) [R-REM-063]; personal services → no SP, negative injunction (CA § 3423(e)) [R-REM-064].
- **Specific performance** [R-REM-070 to R-REM-074] — **CCIFD:** contract with certain terms; conditions met (ready, willing, able); inadequate remedy (land; unique goods § 2-716; CA § 3387 presumption, conclusive for owner-occupied single-family home); feasibility; defenses. Abatement for shortfall; lis pendens.
- **Reformation** [R-REM-075]: prior agreement + writing departs + mutual mistake or fraud; clear and convincing; PER and SOF no bar; BFP bars.
- **Rescission / cancellation** [R-REM-043, R-REM-076, R-REM-077]: insurance — Ins. Code §§ 331, 359 (material misrepresentation or concealment, even innocent).
- **Contempt** [R-REM-078 to R-REM-080]: civil coercive / compensatory vs. criminal; collateral bar (*Walker*; CA *In re Berry*); FRCP 65(d) persons bound.

## IV. Equitable defenses [R-REM-081 to R-REM-090]
- **Laches:** knew + unreasonable delay + prejudice; not a bar to damages within the SOL.
- **Unclean hands:** Π's misconduct in the same transaction.
- **In pari delicto:** equally guilty in an illegal deal.
- **Estoppel:** representation + reasonable detrimental reliance. **Waiver:** knowing relinquishment.
- **Undue hardship:** disproportionate burden; not for willful Δ.
- **Unconscionability / inadequate consideration:** unfair at formation (CA § 3391).
- **Changed circumstances:** modify or dissolve decree.
- **Contract defenses:** SOF (part performance), mistake, misrepresentation, impossibility, BFP.

## V. Declaratory remedies [R-REM-091 to R-REM-094]
- **Declaratory judgment:** actual controversy; adverse parties; ripe; discretionary (28 U.S.C. § 2201; CCP §§ 1060–1061); available against threatened enforcement (*Steffel*).
- **Quiet title / cancellation;** reformation as declaration; nominal damages.

## VI. Close
- **Election** [R-REM-005]: no double recovery; CA § 1692 allows rescission + damages.
- **Best remedy:** one or two sentences recommending the combination that serves Π best.
