## Scope
Remedies answers one question: **once the plaintiff has a claim, what will the court give?** The State Bar's essay outline has six parts [S-091] [VERIFY V-024]:
1. **Basic principles:** legal vs. equitable remedies; classification.
2. **Legal remedies:** damages, including contract, real-property and tort measures, their limits, and punitive damages.
3. **Restitution:** legal (quasi-contract, quantum meruit, replevin, ejectment, legal rescission) and equitable (constructive and resulting trusts, equitable lien, equitable rescission, subrogation, indemnity, contribution, accounting).
4. **Equitable remedies:** injunctions (TRO, preliminary, permanent), specific performance, reformation, rescission, contempt.
5. **Equitable defenses:** laches, unclean hands, in pari delicto, estoppel, undue hardship, waiver, changed circumstances, unconscionability.
6. **Declaratory remedies:** declaratory judgments, reformation, nominal damages.

## How it is tested
- **Open call:** "What remedies can Π obtain, and what defenses can Δ raise?" (Feb 2013 Q3, Feb 2014 Q6, Feb 2017 Q2, Oct 2020 Q5) [VERIFY V-071].
- **Named remedies:** "TRO, preliminary injunction and specific performance" (Jul 2017 Q3, chess queen); "TRO, preliminary injunction and declaratory relief" (Jul 2019 Q2, church candle ban); "preliminary injunction, permanent injunction and damages" (Mar 2025 Q5, fence).
- **Restitution by asset:** "Who prevails as to each of four assets?" (Jul 2026 Q3); "What can Acme recover from Barbara's checking account?" (Jul 2023 Q5).
- **Crossover add-on:** liability in another subject, then remedies.
- **Most-tagged issues:** equitable remedies (8), damages (5), basic principles / open call (4), equitable restitution, defenses and provisional relief (3 each), declaratory relief (1) ([`../../06-past-exams/frequency-analysis.md`](../../06-past-exams/frequency-analysis.md); tags [VERIFY V-071]).
