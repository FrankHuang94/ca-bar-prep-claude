## Scope

The State Bar's Trusts outline [S-093] has twelve parts:
- I. Express trusts: creation, revocability, beneficiaries, charitable trusts, modification and termination.
- II. Statute of frauds and rule against perpetuities.
- III. Failure of express trust: resulting and constructive trusts, gifts, intestacy.
- IV. Restrictions on the beneficial interest: spendthrift, discretionary and support trusts.
- V. Duties of the trustee.
- VI. Discretionary powers.
- VII. Rights of beneficiaries.
- VIII. Remedying breach.
- IX. Trustee liability to beneficiaries.
- X. Beneficiary liability.
- XI. Trust or trustee liability to third parties.
- XII. Third-party liability to the trust.

The repo adds three topics:
- revocability and *Haggerty* (TRS-SUP-001);
- pour-over wills (TRS-SUP-002);
- charitable trusts and cy pres (TRS-SUP-003).

## How it is tested

Trusts is the **least frequent** labelled subject, but it is the **most common crossover partner of Wills**. A Wills + Trusts question appeared 6 times (Feb 2012, Jul 2013, Feb 2015, Feb 2016, Feb 2019, Feb 2025), once with Community Property as well (Feb 2019). Jul 2025 Q1 was a stand-alone Trusts essay.

Recurring structure of a Trusts essay:
1. **Was a valid trust created?** Intent, property, beneficiary, purpose; testamentary trust validity; pour-over.
2. **Was it revoked or modified?** § 15400 default; § 15401 methods; *Haggerty* (2024).
3. **Can creditors reach it?** Spendthrift, discretionary or support provisions; the settlor's creditors and revocable trusts.
4. **Did the trustee breach duties?** Take them one at a time: loyalty and self-dealing, prudent investing and diversification, impartiality, duty to inform and account.
5. **What remedies, and against whom?** Surcharge, rescission, tracing, removal, co-trustee and successor liability, third parties.
6. **Can it be modified or terminated?** Consent, material purpose, changed circumstances, uneconomic trusts, cy pres.
7. **If the trust fails, where does the property go?** Resulting trust, then will residue or intestacy.

## Study order

1. Study **after Wills** and alongside Community Property; the plan pairs them (W10 first pass, W22 second pass).
2. Read [`primer.md`](primer.md) (20 minutes), then [`outline.md`](outline.md) parts I and I.J. Revocation, modification and termination give the most essay points.
3. Read outline V–IX (duties, breach, liability), then IV and XI (creditors).
4. Memorize the must-know rules in [`memorization-list.md`](memorization-list.md) from [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md). Drill [`flashcards.csv`](flashcards.csv).
5. Work the [`worked-examples.md`](worked-examples.md), then write the past essays in timed conditions:
   - Feb 2016 Q1 (revocation and duties);
   - Feb 2012 Q1 (termination and successor trustee);
   - Feb 2025 Q2 (cy pres);
   - Jul 2025 Q1 (termination and trustee liability).
6. Before the exam, re-read [`attack-outline.md`](attack-outline.md), [`traps.md`](traps.md) and the ★ rows of [`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md).
