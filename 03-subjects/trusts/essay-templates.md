# Trusts — essay templates

These skeletons come from the issue patterns in the seven Trusts essays of 2012–2026 (see the appendix of [`outline.md`](outline.md)). The State Bar rewards **headings that follow the call**, a short rule, specific facts, two-sided analysis and a conclusion on every issue (`00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md` §5). Adapt the headings to the call; never paste a template in full when the call asks for less.

**Opening line (every Trusts essay).** If the call says California law: "California's Probate Code governs." If not: "General trust principles apply; where California's Probate Code differs, both are noted."

---

## Template 1 — Was a valid trust created? (creation + failure)

*Use when the facts give a will or instrument with trust language, odd beneficiaries or purposes, or missing property (Feb 2015 pattern).*

```
I. Validity of the [Name] Trust
   A. Type of trust (inter vivos / testamentary / pour-over)          R-TRS-009, R-TRS-010
   B. Settlor's capacity [and undue influence if facts]                R-TRS-003
   C. Intent to create a trust (precatory words?)                      R-TRS-004
   D. Trust property (funding; expectancy?)                            R-TRS-005
   E. Lawful purpose                                                   R-TRS-006
   F. Ascertainable beneficiaries / charitable purpose                 R-TRS-018, R-TRS-019
   G. Formalities (§ 15206 land; § 15207 oral personalty; will)        R-TRS-030, R-TRS-031
   H. Trustee (vacancy does not defeat trust)                          R-TRS-007
   Conclusion: valid / fails in part
II. If the trust fails: resulting trust → residue → intestacy          R-TRS-036, R-TRS-040
```

**Paragraph pattern (each element):** "A trust requires [element] (Prob. Code § ___). Here, [specific fact] shows [element is / is not met], because [reason]. [Other side: however, …]. Therefore, [conclusion]."

## Template 2 — Was the trust revoked or amended? (SUP-001; Feb 2016 pattern; *Haggerty*)

```
I. Was the trust revocable?
   Rule: CA § 15400 (revocable unless expressly irrevocable) vs. traditional rule  R-TRS-011
   Application: what the instrument says; where created
II. Was it validly revoked [or amended]?
   A. Method stated in the trust: complied?                          R-TRS-012
   B. Exclusive? (must be explicit)
   C. Statutory method: signed writing, not a will, delivered to trustee in life
      – Revocation by later will                                     R-TRS-013
      – Delivery where settlor is (co-)trustee
   D. Amendment: § 15402 + Haggerty (2024)                           R-TRS-014
   E. CP in trust: one spouse revokes; both modify                   R-TRS-015
   F. Agent / conservator acting?                                    R-TRS-016
   Conclusion (state the alternative result under the other view)
III. Consequence: which instrument governs; pour-over to amended trust; if revoked, property returns to settlor's estate → will
```

**Tip.** Two selected answers in Feb 2016 reached opposite conclusions on revocation by will. Present both sides fully; the conclusion matters less than the reasoning.

## Template 3 — Trustee breaches, duty by duty, then remedies (Feb 2016; Jul 2025)

```
I. Duties owed (and to whom: settlor while revocable; beneficiaries after)   R-TRS-065
II. Breaches by [Trustee]
   A. Duty of loyalty — self-dealing [the house purchase]           R-TRS-052, R-TRS-053
      (fair price no defense; authorization? consent? court?)
   B. Duty of loyalty — conflict [investment in own business]       R-TRS-055
   C. Duty to invest prudently / diversify                          R-TRS-057, R-TRS-058
   D. Duty of impartiality                                          R-TRS-056
   E. Duty to earmark / not commingle                               R-TRS-060
   F. Duty to inform and account                                    R-TRS-062, R-TRS-063
   G. [Co-trustee's] duty to monitor and prevent                    R-TRS-061, R-TRS-083
   H. Abuse of discretion (if "sole discretion" facts)              R-TRS-068, R-TRS-069
III. Remedies for [Beneficiary]
   A. Rescind or take profit (self-dealing election)                R-TRS-079
   B. Surcharge: loss + interest / profit / lost profit             R-TRS-081
   C. Tracing; constructive trust or equitable lien                 R-TRS-080
   D. Removal; reduce compensation                                  R-TRS-073, R-TRS-078
   E. Defenses: exculpatory clause, consent, limitations, excuse    R-TRS-082, R-TRS-084 to R-TRS-086
```

**Paragraph pattern (each duty):** rule with section → facts that show the breach → the trustee's best argument → conclusion. One heading per duty; graders count them.

## Template 4 — Can the trust be modified or terminated? (Feb 2012; Jul 2025)

```
I. Is the trust revocable? (if yes and settlor alive: settlor decides)   R-TRS-011
II. Termination or modification by consent
   A. Settlor + all beneficiaries (§ 15404)                             R-TRS-024
   B. All beneficiaries without settlor (§ 15403)                       R-TRS-025
      1. Do all beneficiaries consent? (minors, unborn, contingent)
      2. Material purpose? (spendthrift, age delay, support, keep farm)
      3. Claflin bar vs. California balancing
III. Court modification
   A. Changed circumstances (§ 15409)                                   R-TRS-026
   B. Uneconomic trust (§ 15408; ≤ $100,000 trustee route)              R-TRS-027
   C. Combine/divide (§§ 15411–15412)                                   R-TRS-029
   D. Charitable: cy pres                                               R-TRS-021
IV. Result and distribution                                             R-TRS-028
```

## Template 5 — Charitable trust and cy pres (Feb 2015; Feb 2025)

```
I. Is it a valid charitable trust? (purpose; public benefit; indefinite beneficiaries)   R-TRS-019
II. Who may enforce / petition (AG; trustees; special interest)                          R-TRS-020
III. Cy pres
   A. Original purpose impossible, impracticable, illegal (or wasteful)?
   B. General charitable intent? (breadth, donor history, gift over?)
   C. Nearest purpose: is the proposed purpose close enough?
   Conclusion
IV. If cy pres fails: resulting trust → estate (residue/heirs)                           R-TRS-036, R-TRS-040
```

## Template 6 — Creditors and the trust (Feb 2019 pattern)

```
I. Creditors of the settlor
   A. Revocable trust — during life (§ 18200)                         R-TRS-092
   B. Revocable trust — after death (§ 19001)                         R-TRS-093
   C. Self-settled irrevocable trust (§ 15304)                        R-TRS-047
   D. [CP crossover: character of the trust property; community debts]
II. Creditors of a beneficiary
   A. Type of interest: mandatory / support / discretionary           R-TRS-048, R-TRS-049
   B. Spendthrift clause valid?                                       R-TRS-041
   C. Exceptions: support; restitution; public support               R-TRS-043 to R-TRS-045
   D. General creditors: 25% and excess over support (contrast general rule)  R-TRS-046
   E. Amounts paid or principal due                                   R-TRS-042
```

## Template 7 — Wills + Trusts crossover: "What rights does X have in the trust/estate?"

```
I. Inventory: what passes outside probate (funded trust, Totten, JT) vs. by will
II. The will: validity, pour-over (§ 6300), interpretation        R-WIL-047 ff.; R-TRS-010
III. The trust: validity; revocation/amendment; which version     Templates 1–2
IV. Gifts within the trust: § 21380 disqualified donees; no-contest clause   R-TRS-075, R-TRS-076
V. Family protection reaching the trust: omitted spouse/child (§ 21601); former spouse (§ 5040)   R-TRS-077
VI. Distribution: party by party, as the call lists them
```

**Tip.** When the call names parties ("What rights do A, B and C have?"), make each party a heading and run the relevant trust and will issues under each.

## Template 8 — Third parties (purchaser, lender, tort victim)

```
I. Trustee's liability to the third party (contract § 18000; tort § 18002)   R-TRS-090, R-TRS-091
II. Trust's liability (sue trustee in representative capacity, § 18004)
III. Third party's liability to the trust
   A. Knowing participation → liability + constructive trust              R-TRS-094
   B. Purchaser: good faith + value + no actual knowledge (§ 18100)       R-TRS-095
   C. Donee or knowing transferee → restore; § 850 petition; § 859        R-TRS-096
```
