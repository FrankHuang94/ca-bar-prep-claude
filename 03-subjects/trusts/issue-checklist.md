# Trusts — issue checklist

Run this list **in order** while reading the facts. The threshold issues come first, because later issues depend on them. Each line gives the issue, the **trigger facts** to look for, and the rule ID.

## 0. Threshold

- [ ] **Read the call.** Does it say "California law"? If not, give the general rule and add California where it differs ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md) §1).
- [ ] **Crossover?** A will, a death, a marriage, a new child, or a lawyer-drafter → Wills, CP or PR issues travel with the trust.
- [ ] **Which instrument governs?** Living trust, testamentary trust, amendments, pour-over will. Build a timeline: dates of trust, will, amendments, deaths, marriages, births.

## 1. Creation (outline I, II)

| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Method of creation | "I hold … as trustee"; deed to "X as trustee"; will "in trust" | R-TRS-002 |
| Capacity / undue influence | Elderly or ill settlor, caregiver or child arranges the amendment, isolation | R-TRS-003; R-WIL-039, R-WIL-040 |
| Intent vs. precatory words | "hope," "wish," "request," "it is my desire" | R-TRS-004, R-TRS-039 |
| Trust property / funding | Asset never deeded, listed only on a schedule; "future profits"; expected inheritance | R-TRS-005 |
| Lawful purpose | Condition against marriage, encouraging divorce, illegal aim | R-TRS-006 |
| Ascertainable beneficiary | "my friends," "worthy students," pets, purposes | R-TRS-018 |
| Charitable trust | Research, education, poor, religion, health, public benefit | R-TRS-019, R-TRS-020 |
| Honorary / pet trust | Care of an animal; maintain a car, grave or garden | R-TRS-022, R-TRS-023 |
| Trustee missing or declining | Named trustee dead, refuses, "doesn't want the job" | R-TRS-007 |
| Merger | Same person sole trustee and sole beneficiary | R-TRS-008 |
| Statute of frauds | Oral trust of land; oral trust of personal property | R-TRS-030, R-TRS-031 |
| Secret / semi-secret trust | Will gift to a friend who "promised" to pass it on; "in trust for purposes I have told him" | R-TRS-032, R-TRS-033 |
| Pour-over | Will leaves residue "to the trustee of my trust"; trust signed after the will | R-TRS-010; R-WIL-061 |
| Totten trust | Bank account "in trust for" | R-TRS-017; R-WIL-101 |
| Perpetuities | Gift to grandchildren "who reach 25"; remote contingencies | R-TRS-034, R-TRS-035 |

## 2. Revocation and modification (I.G; SUP-001)

| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Revocable at all? | Instrument silent; "irrevocable" appears or not | R-TRS-011 |
| Method of revocation | Letter, email, note, will, torn document, notarized writing; delivered or not | R-TRS-012 |
| Revocation by will | Later will "revokes my trust" or gives away trust assets | R-TRS-013 |
| Amendment method (*Haggerty*) | Unsigned or undelivered amendment; clause "may be amended by…"; settlor is trustee | R-TRS-014 |
| CP in trust / joint settlors | Spouses' joint trust; one spouse acts alone | R-TRS-015 |
| Agent or conservator acting | POA holder or conservator amends or revokes | R-TRS-016 |

## 3. Termination and modification of an irrevocable trust (I.J)

| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Settlor + all beneficiaries consent | Settlor alive, family agreement | R-TRS-024 |
| Beneficiaries alone; material purpose | Beneficiaries want the money now; spendthrift clause; "until age 35" | R-TRS-025 |
| Changed circumstances | Unforeseen event: cure found, farm unprofitable, beneficiary disabled | R-TRS-026 |
| Uneconomic trust | Small trust, high fees | R-TRS-027 |
| Termination by terms | Age reached, purpose done | R-TRS-028 |
| Combine/divide | Two similar trusts | R-TRS-029 |
| Cy pres | Charitable purpose impossible or accomplished; trustee wants a new purpose | R-TRS-021 |

## 4. Failure of the trust (III)

- [ ] Resulting trust (failed purpose, surplus, purchase money) → R-TRS-036, R-TRS-037.
- [ ] Constructive trust (fraud, undue influence, secret trust, trustee's wrongful purchase) → R-TRS-038.
- [ ] Gift instead (precatory) → R-TRS-039.
- [ ] Destination: residue, other residuary takers, intestacy → R-TRS-040.

## 5. Creditors (IV, XI.D)

| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Spendthrift clause | "Not assignable or subject to creditors" | R-TRS-041 |
| Distributed / due amounts | Money already paid; principal due at a stated age | R-TRS-042 |
| Exception creditors | Ex-spouse or child support; crime victim; county | R-TRS-043 to R-TRS-045 |
| General creditor | Bank or contract judgment | R-TRS-046 |
| Self-settled | Settlor also a beneficiary | R-TRS-047 |
| Discretionary / support | "in trustee's discretion"; "for support and education" | R-TRS-048, R-TRS-049 |
| Settlor's creditors, revocable trust | Settlor alive or dead with debts | R-TRS-092, R-TRS-093 |

## 6. Trustee duties: one heading each (V, VI)

| Duty | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Follow the terms | Trustee ignores instructions (sells the home the widow was to keep) | R-TRS-050 |
| Standard of care | Careless management, missed deadlines, professional trustee | R-TRS-051 |
| Loyalty | Trustee benefits personally or helps relatives | R-TRS-052 |
| Self-dealing | Trustee buys or rents trust property, borrows trust money, sells own property to trust (even at fair value) | R-TRS-053 |
| Trustee–beneficiary deal | Trustee buys a beneficiary's interest | R-TRS-054 |
| Conflict | Trust invests in trustee's business; hires trustee's firm or family | R-TRS-055 |
| Impartiality | Income vs. remainder; favoring one sibling; two trusts with one trustee | R-TRS-056 |
| Prudent investing | Speculative investment; trust's needs ignored | R-TRS-057 |
| Diversification | Concentrated stock, single asset | R-TRS-058 |
| Preserve / productive | Vacant property; uninsured; idle cash | R-TRS-059 |
| Earmark / commingling | Trust money in trustee's personal account | R-TRS-060 |
| Delegation / co-trustee | Passive co-trustee; unsupervised agent | R-TRS-061 |
| Inform / account | No reports; refusal to give terms; secret transactions | R-TRS-062, R-TRS-063 |
| Notice after death; 120-day contest | Settlor died; contest filed months later | R-TRS-064 |
| Revocable-trust period | Trustee's acts while the settlor was alive | R-TRS-065 |
| Compensation; lawyer-trustee | Trustee bills legal fees and trustee fees | R-TRS-066 |
| Discretion | "sole and absolute discretion"; refusal to pay; payments to self | R-TRS-067 to R-TRS-070 |

## 7. Beneficiary rights, remedies and liability (VII–IX)

- [ ] Standing: revocable trust? Settlor alive? → R-TRS-065, R-TRS-071.
- [ ] Petition, removal, drafter-trustee → R-TRS-072 to R-TRS-074.
- [ ] Contest; no-contest clause; disqualified donee; omitted heirs → R-TRS-075 to R-TRS-077.
- [ ] Remedies menu; election for self-dealing; tracing → R-TRS-078 to R-TRS-080.
- [ ] Measure of liability; excuse → R-TRS-081, R-TRS-082.
- [ ] Co-trustee, successor, agent → R-TRS-083.
- [ ] Defenses: exculpatory clause, consent/release, limitations → R-TRS-084 to R-TRS-086.
- [ ] Trustee dead → claim against the estate → R-TRS-087.

## 8. Outsiders (X–XII)

- [ ] Beneficiary who joined the breach; overpayments → R-TRS-088.
- [ ] Third-party claims against beneficiary → R-TRS-089.
- [ ] Trustee's contracts and torts → R-TRS-090, R-TRS-091.
- [ ] Third party who knowingly took part → R-TRS-094.
- [ ] Buyer of trust property: good faith, value, actual knowledge? → R-TRS-095.
- [ ] Someone grabbed trust assets → § 850 petition, § 859 double damages → R-TRS-096.

## 9. Finish

- [ ] Conclusion on **every** issue ("likely," "probably").
- [ ] **Distribution**: who takes what, after any failure, lapse, omitted-heir share or creditor claim.
