# Trusts — attack outline

**Before writing.** Read the call: does it ask for "California law"? Draw a timeline of the instruments and events. One heading per call; inside it, one heading per issue (and **one per trustee duty**). Use Prob. Code section numbers.

## I. Valid express trust
- **Elements:** settlor with capacity + intent + property + lawful purpose + ascertainable beneficiary (or charity); trustee not essential (§§ 15200–15205). R-TRS-001
- **Methods:** declaration, transfer, will, power of appointment, enforceable promise; no consideration (§§ 15200, 15208). R-TRS-002
- **Capacity:** §§ 810–812; § 6100.5 test for will-like amendments (*Andersen*); undue influence. R-TRS-003
- **Intent:** present, enforceable duties; precatory words → gift. R-TRS-004
- **Res:** existing, identifiable; no expectancies; *Heggstad* schedule funding. R-TRS-005
- **Purpose:** not illegal or against policy (no restraint on marriage). R-TRS-006
- **Trustee:** a trust never fails for want of a trustee; acceptance and rejection. R-TRS-007
- **Merger:** sole trustee = sole beneficiary → no trust (remainder beneficiaries prevent it). R-TRS-008
- **Living vs. testamentary:** testamentary depends on a valid will. R-TRS-009
- **Pour-over (§ 6300):** trust signed before, with, or within 60 days after the will; unfunded is fine; amendments apply; revoked trust → lapse. R-TRS-010
- **Totten trust:** revocable; balance to beneficiary at death. R-TRS-017

## I.G Revocation / modification (*Haggerty*)
- **Default:** CA revocable unless expressly irrevocable (§ 15400); traditional rule = irrevocable. R-TRS-011
- **Methods (§ 15401(a)):** instrument method, or signed writing (not a will) delivered to trustee during life; instrument method blocks the statute only if **explicitly exclusive**. R-TRS-012
- **Will:** not a § 15401(a)(2) writing; UTC contra. R-TRS-013
- **Amend (§ 15402; *Haggerty* 2024):** revocation method works unless the amendment method is explicitly exclusive. R-TRS-014
- **CP (Fam. Code § 761):** one spouse revokes; both modify. R-TRS-015
- **Agent/conservator:** express POA authority / court approval. R-TRS-016

## I.x2 Beneficiaries; charity
- **Ascertainable** class or power of selection. R-TRS-018
- **Charitable:** public purpose, indefinite beneficiaries, perpetual, AG enforces (Gov. Code § 12598). R-TRS-019, R-TRS-020
- **Cy pres:** purpose impossible/impracticable/illegal (wasteful) + general charitable intent → nearest purpose; else resulting trust. R-TRS-021
- **Honorary** 21 years (§ 15211); **pet** trusts (§ 15212). R-TRS-022, R-TRS-023

## I.J Modification and termination
- **Settlor + all beneficiaries:** yes, even with a material purpose (§ 15404). R-TRS-024
- **Beneficiaries alone:** material purpose (spendthrift, age, support) → *Claflin* bar; CA balancing (§ 15403). R-TRS-025
- **Changed circumstances (§ 15409):** unforeseen; defeats purposes; court may deviate. R-TRS-026
- **Uneconomic (§ 15408):** court any size; trustee ≤ $100,000. R-TRS-027
- **By terms; distribution; combine/divide.** R-TRS-028, R-TRS-029

## II. SOF and RAP
- **Land:** writing signed by trustee or conveyance by settlor, or operation of law (§ 15206). R-TRS-030
- **Personal property:** oral OK; clear and convincing; settlor's declaration alone insufficient (§ 15207). R-TRS-031
- **Secret trust** → constructive trust; **semi-secret** → resulting trust (majority) / constructive (modern). R-TRS-032, R-TRS-033
- **USRAP:** lives + 21 or 90-year wait-and-see; reform (§§ 21205, 21220). Charity exempt. R-TRS-034, R-TRS-035

## III. Failure
- **Resulting trust:** failed or surplus express trust; purchase money (gift presumed for family). R-TRS-036, R-TRS-037
- **Constructive trust:** wrongful gain (Civ. Code § 2224). R-TRS-038
- **Gift:** no duty intended. R-TRS-039
- **Destination:** residue → other residuary takers → intestacy. R-TRS-040

## IV. Restrictions on the beneficial interest
- **Spendthrift valid** (§§ 15300–15301); paid money reachable; due principal (§ 15301(b)). R-TRS-041, R-TRS-042
- **Exceptions:** support (§ 15305); felony restitution (§ 15305.5); public support (§ 15306); general creditors 25% (§ 15306.5) and excess over support (§ 15307). R-TRS-043 to R-TRS-046
- **Self-settled:** creditors reach max trustee could pay (§ 15304). R-TRS-047
- **Discretionary:** creditor can't compel (§ 15303). **Support:** protected to extent needed (§ 15302). R-TRS-048, R-TRS-049

## V. Duties (one heading each)
- **Follow terms** (§ 16000). R-TRS-050
- **Care:** prudent person in like capacity; special skills (§§ 16040, 16014). R-TRS-051
- **Loyalty:** solely in beneficiaries' interest (§ 16002). R-TRS-052
- **Self-dealing:** no further inquiry; fair price no defense (§ 16004(a)). R-TRS-053
- **Trustee–beneficiary deal:** presumed breach (§ 16004(c)). R-TRS-054
- **Conflicts:** trustee's business, firm, relatives. R-TRS-055
- **Impartiality:** income vs. remainder (§ 16003). R-TRS-056
- **Prudent investor:** portfolio, risk/return, purposes (§ 16047). R-TRS-057
- **Diversify; review; no hindsight** (§§ 16048, 16049, 16051). R-TRS-058
- **Control, preserve, productive, enforce claims** (§§ 16006–16011). R-TRS-059
- **Earmark, no commingling** (§ 16009). R-TRS-060
- **Delegation; co-trustees** (§§ 16052, 15620, 16013). R-TRS-061
- **Inform / account:** annually, termination, change of trustee; not while revocable (§§ 16060–16069). R-TRS-062, R-TRS-063
- **Death notice 60 days; contest 120 days / 60 days after terms, whichever later** (§§ 16061.7–16061.8). R-TRS-064
- **Revocable period:** duties to settlor; *Giraldin* standing after death (§ 15800). R-TRS-065
- **Compensation; lawyer-trustee no dual fees** (§ 15687). R-TRS-066

## VI. Discretion
- **Powers:** instrument + statute + implied; all fiduciary. R-TRS-067
- **Reasonable** (§ 16080); **"sole/absolute"** → good faith, purposes; trustee-beneficiary HEMS (§ 16081). R-TRS-068, R-TRS-069
- **Abuse review; other resources.** R-TRS-070

## VII. Beneficiary rights
- Transferable unless spendthrift; standing. R-TRS-071
- **§ 17200 petitions.** R-TRS-072
- **Removal (§ 15642)**, incl. drafter or care custodian as sole trustee (§ 15642(b)(6)). R-TRS-073, R-TRS-074
- **No-contest** (direct contest, no probable cause); **§ 21380** donees; omitted heirs and former spouse. R-TRS-075 to R-TRS-077

## VIII–IX. Breach, remedies and liability
- **Remedies (§ 16420):** compel, enjoin, surcharge, receiver, remove, cut fees, set aside, lien/constructive trust, trace (subject to § 18100). R-TRS-078
- **Self-dealing:** rescind or take profit. R-TRS-079
- **Tracing:** constructive trust (up) / lien (down). R-TRS-080
- **Measure (§ 16440):** loss + interest, or trustee's profit, or lost profit; court may excuse good-faith trustee. R-TRS-081, R-TRS-082
- **Co-trustee / successor / agent** (§§ 16401–16403). R-TRS-083
- **Defenses:** exculpation (not intentional, gross, bad faith, reckless, or profit) (§ 16461); consent/release (§§ 16463–16465); 3-year limitations (§ 16460). R-TRS-084 to R-TRS-086
- **Dead trustee:** claim against estate. R-TRS-087

## X. Beneficiary liability
- Consenting/participating beneficiary barred; interest impounded; repay overpayments. R-TRS-088
- Not liable to third parties as beneficiary. R-TRS-089

## XI. Trust/trustee liability to third parties
- **Contracts:** no personal liability if capacity revealed and trust identified (§ 18000). R-TRS-090
- **Torts/ownership:** personal liability only if at fault; sue trust via trustee (§§ 18001–18004). R-TRS-091
- **Settlor's creditors:** revocable trust reachable during life (§ 18200) and after death if probate estate short (§ 19001). R-TRS-092, R-TRS-093

## XII. Third-party liability to trust
- **Knowing participant** liable; constructive trust. R-TRS-094
- **Good faith + value + no actual knowledge** → protected (§ 18100); donees and knowers must restore. R-TRS-095
- **§ 850 petition; § 859 double damages** for bad-faith taking. R-TRS-096

**Finish:** conclusion on every issue; then **who takes what**.
