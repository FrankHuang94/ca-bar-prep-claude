# Community Property — issue checklist

Run this list in order while reading the facts. Then build **one heading per asset** in the call's order and drop each issue under the asset it affects. Rule IDs point to [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## A. Threshold issues (before any asset)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What event?** Dissolution, legal separation, death, or a creditor's claim during marriage | "seeks a divorce," "died," "creditor obtained a judgment" | R-CMP-075, R-CMP-091, R-CMP-084 |
| 2 | **Valid marriage?** Putative spouse; registered domestic partner; cohabitants | Bigamy, defective license, "believed they were married," "registered partners," "lived together" | R-CMP-006, R-CMP-007 |
| 3 | **Domicile history** → QCP; choice of law; both spouses domiciled in California at dissolution? | "lived in State X, a non-community-property state," "moved to California in 2015" | R-CMP-003, R-CMP-005 |
| 4 | **Date of marriage and date of separation** | Moving out, "told H she wanted a divorce," separate bedrooms, reconciliation attempts, joint vacation after the fight | R-CMP-070 |
| 5 | **Premarital agreement** (enforceability first, then its effect) | "signed a prenup the night before the wedding," "H's lawyer drafted it," "W did not have a lawyer," support waiver | R-CMP-018 to R-CMP-021 |
| 6 | **Federal benefit** | Military retired pay, Social Security, ERISA 401(k), SGLI/FEGLI, savings bonds | R-CMP-100 to R-CMP-103 |

## B. For each asset (the core loop)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Source and time** of acquisition | "before they married," "inherited," "gift from her aunt," "bought with his salary," "after they separated" | R-CMP-001, R-CMP-002, R-CMP-008 |
| 8 | **General CP presumption** and who must rebut | Acquired during marriage; no records | R-CMP-011 |
| 9 | **Title presumptions**: one name (Valli/Brace); joint title at dissolution (§ 2581); joint tenancy at death; pre-1975 married woman | "titled in H's name," "as joint tenants," "community property with right of survivorship," 1970s deed | R-CMP-012 to R-CMP-015 |
| 10 | **Gift or inheritance** to one or both spouses | "her mother deeded the house to both of them," wedding gifts | R-CMP-017 |
| 11 | **Transmutation**: writing? express declaration? personal-gift exception? pre-1985? | Deed adding a spouse to title; "told her the car was hers"; jewelry, watch, jersey given between spouses | R-CMP-022 to R-CMP-025 |
| 12 | **Undue-influence presumption** for any interspousal advantage | Interspousal deed; quitclaim; postnuptial agreement; one spouse drafted | R-CMP-016, R-CMP-026, R-CMP-027 |
| 13 | **Commingling and tracing** (direct; exhaustion; family expense presumption) | Inheritance deposited in joint account; purchase from mixed account; bank records available or not | R-CMP-028 to R-CMP-032 |
| 14 | **Credit acquisition**: lender's intent | Loan, mortgage, "pledged his inherited stock," "both signed the note" | R-CMP-058, R-CMP-059 |
| 15 | **Community labor on SP business** → Pereira / Van Camp (apply both) | Premarital or inherited business grew while spouse worked in it; salary drawn; market boom | R-CMP-033 to R-CMP-036 |
| 16 | **Community payments on SP real property** → Moore/Marsden | House bought before marriage; mortgage paid from salary during marriage | R-CMP-037, R-CMP-038, R-CMP-040 |
| 17 | **Community funds improving SP** → reimbursement | Remodel, garage, pool on one spouse's SP land paid with CP | R-CMP-039 |
| 18 | **SP contributions** → § 2640 reimbursement (CP asset or other spouse's SP); written waiver? | SP down payment; SP paid principal; transmutation silent on reimbursement | R-CMP-042 to R-CMP-045 |
| 19 | **Education** → § 2641 reimbursement; loans assigned | Tuition paid with CP; student loans; "now earns far more as a doctor" | R-CMP-046, R-CMP-047, R-CMP-080 |
| 20 | **Pensions and deferred compensation** → time rule; division method | Pension, 401(k), years of service before/during/after marriage | R-CMP-048 to R-CMP-050, R-CMP-082 |
| 21 | **Disability, severance, workers' comp** → what does it replace? | Benefits starting after DOS; layoff package | R-CMP-051, R-CMP-052 |
| 22 | **Special assets**: stock options, life insurance, goodwill, lottery, IP | Options vesting after DOS; term policy; professional practice; lottery ticket | R-CMP-053 to R-CMP-057 |
| 23 | **Personal-injury recovery**: when did the claim arise; against whom; commingled? | Car accident, settlement, spouse caused the injury | R-CMP-056, R-CMP-078, R-CMP-079 |

## C. Conduct of the spouses (management)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | **Gift of CP** without written consent | "gave the antique mirror to a friend," donations, gifts to a lover | R-CMP-064, R-CMP-099 |
| 25 | **Household goods / clothing** sold or pledged without consent | Pawning the furniture, selling the home furnishings, the children's clothing | R-CMP-065 |
| 26 | **Business**: primary management; notice for sale of substantially all assets | Spouse runs the community business; sells equipment | R-CMP-066 |
| 27 | **Real property joinder** (sale, encumbrance, lease > 1 year); BFP; one-year limit | Deed or lien signed by one spouse; forged signature | R-CMP-067, R-CMP-059 |
| 28 | **Fiduciary duty and § 1101 remedies** (50% / 100%) | Concealment, secret transfers, self-dealing | R-CMP-063, R-CMP-068 |
| 29 | **SP management** (no consent needed) | Spouse sells inherited stock alone | R-CMP-069 |

## E. Division and debts

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 33 | **Equal division**; valuation date; in-kind vs. offset | Any dissolution | R-CMP-075 |

| 37 | **Allocation of debts** at dissolution; liability after division; reimbursement | "Who must pay the loan?" | R-CMP-089, R-CMP-090, R-CMP-061 |

## F. Death (Wills crossover)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 38 | **CP at death**: survivor's half; decedent's testamentary power; item theory; election | Will gives away "my house" or "all our property" | R-CMP-091, R-CMP-095 |
| 39 | **QCP at death**: decedent's QCP only; out-of-state land excluded | Moved from State X; farm in State X | R-CMP-092 |
| 40 | **QCP recapture** (§ 102) | Decedent deeded QCP into joint tenancy with a child or into a revocable trust without spouse's consent | R-CMP-093 |
| 41 | **Intestacy shares** (CP, QCP, SP) | No will, or a will that fails | R-CMP-094 |
| 42 | **Nonprobate transfers** of CP; omitted spouse; revocation by dissolution | Beneficiary designations; will predates marriage; divorce before death | R-CMP-096, R-CMP-097 |
| 43 | **Survivorship forms** and passage without administration | Joint tenancy; CP with right of survivorship | R-CMP-014, R-CMP-098 |

## G. Before you finish

- Did every asset get a **conclusion** (character + who gets what)?
- Did you address **reimbursement** after every presumption that favors the community?
- Did you separate the **creditor** question from the **allocation** question?
- In a crossover, did you characterize **before** applying Wills/Trusts/PR rules?
