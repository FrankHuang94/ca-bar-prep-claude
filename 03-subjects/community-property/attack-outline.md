# Community Property — attack outline

**Structure:** one heading per asset (call order) → Source/Time → Presumption → Agreements/Transmutation → Tracing/Apportionment → Reimbursement → Conclusion & division. Separate headings for debts and fiduciary claims. California law throughout.

## Opening paragraph (write every time)
California is a community property state. Property acquired during marriage by a spouse domiciled in California is presumed CP (Fam. Code § 760); property acquired before marriage, after separation, or by gift, bequest, devise or descent, and its rents and profits, is SP (§§ 770, 771). Character is fixed at acquisition by source and time, unless the spouses changed it by a valid agreement. At dissolution the community estate is divided equally (§ 2550).

## I Definitions and threshold
- **CP:** acquired during marriage while domiciled in CA; present equal interests (§§ 760, 751). (R-CMP-001)
- **SP:** before marriage; gift/inheritance; rents and profits; post-DOS earnings (§§ 770, 771). (R-CMP-002)
- **QCP:** acquired while domiciled elsewhere that would have been CP; divided like CP at dissolution if both spouses domiciled in CA (Roesch); at death, decedent's QCP half to survivor, CA land and personal property only (§ 125; Prob. §§ 66, 101). (R-CMP-003)
- **Putative spouse:** good-faith (subjective) belief in a void/voidable marriage → quasi-marital property divided equally (§ 2251; Ceja). (R-CMP-006)
- **Domestic partners = spouses** (§ 297.5); cohabitants → Marvin contract only. (R-CMP-007)

## II.A Presumptions
- **General:** acquired during marriage → CP; SP claimant must trace (§ 760). (R-CMP-011)
- **One name:** CP funds + one spouse's title ≠ SP without § 852 writing; Evid. § 662 yields (Valli; Brace). (R-CMP-012)
- **Joint title at dissolution:** CP; rebut only by SP statement in title document or written agreement; SP contributor gets § 2640 (§ 2581). (R-CMP-013)
- **Joint tenancy at death:** survivorship controls; Brace makes CP-funded JT property presumptively CP but did not disturb survivorship, so a will cannot pass "half." (R-CMP-014)
- **Undue influence:** interspousal advantage → presumption; advantaged spouse proves free, informed act (§ 721; Haines). (R-CMP-016)
- **Pre-1975 married woman:** written-instrument property presumed her SP; with another → tenant in common; "husband and wife" → CP (§ 803). (R-CMP-015)

## II.B Agreements
- **Prenup:** writing signed by both; no consideration; effective at marriage (§§ 1611, 1613). (R-CMP-018)
- **Voluntariness § 1615(c):** counsel or separate written waiver; 7 days with final agreement; written explanation if unrepresented; no duress/fraud/undue influence/incapacity. (R-CMP-019)
- **Unconscionable + no disclosure + no written waiver + no knowledge** → unenforceable (§ 1615(a)(2)). (R-CMP-020)
- **Support waiver:** needs independent counsel at signing; not unconscionable at enforcement (§ 1612(c)). (R-CMP-021)
- **Transmutation:** writing + express declaration of change of ownership, by adversely affected spouse (§ 852(a); MacDonald; Benson). (R-CMP-022, R-CMP-023)
- **Personal-gift exception:** clothing/jewelry/personal items used by donee, not substantial in value (§ 852(c)). (R-CMP-024)
- **Pre-1985:** oral OK (§ 852(e)). (R-CMP-025)

## II.C Commingling and tracing
- **Untraceable commingled funds → CP;** SP claimant traces (See). (R-CMP-028)
- **Direct tracing:** SP funds available + intent to use them, with records (Mix). (R-CMP-029)
- **Exhaustion:** CP exhausted by family expenses at time of purchase (See). (R-CMP-030)
- **Family expense presumption:** expenses paid from CP first; SP used for them = gift, no reimbursement (See). (R-CMP-031)

## II.D Community effort or funds on SP
- **Pereira** (effort-driven): SP = capital + fair return; rest CP. (R-CMP-034)
- **Van Camp** (capital-driven): CP = reasonable salary − amounts drawn; rest SP. (R-CMP-035)
- **Choose for substantial justice; apply both** (Beam; Dekker); neither formula mandatory. (R-CMP-036)
- **Moore/Marsden:** CP% = CP principal ÷ price; CP share = CP principal + CP% × marital appreciation; premarital appreciation SP; interest/taxes/insurance excluded. (R-CMP-037, R-CMP-038)
- **CP improves SP:** reimbursement (cost or value added); consent ≠ gift (Wolfe; Allen). (R-CMP-039)

## II.E–J Reimbursement and special assets
- **§ 2640(b):** SP down payment/improvement/principal into CP → reimbursed, no interest, capped at net value, unless written waiver. (R-CMP-042)
- **§ 2640(c):** SP into other spouse's SP → reimbursed unless written transmutation or waiver. (R-CMP-043)
- **Education:** degree not property; CP reimbursed with interest; loans to student; 10-year presumptions (§ 2641; Sullivan). (R-CMP-046, R-CMP-047)
- **Pensions:** CP to extent earned during marriage, vested or not; time rule; cash-out or in-kind/QDRO; Gillmore election (Brown; § 2610). (R-CMP-048 to R-CMP-050)
- **Disability/severance:** what does it replace? post-DOS earnings → SP; retirement substitute → CP (Stenquist; Saslow). (R-CMP-051, R-CMP-052)
- **Stock options:** Hug (past services; from hire) or Nelson (future services; from grant). (R-CMP-053)
- **Life insurance:** whole life pro rata; term follows last premium; title irrelevant (Valli; Burwell). (R-CMP-054)
- **Goodwill:** CP if developed during marriage; exclude post-DOS effort. (R-CMP-055)
- **PI damages:** CP if claim arose during marriage; SP if after DOS/judgment or from spouse (§§ 780, 781). (R-CMP-056)
- **Credit acquisitions:** lender relied primarily on SP? else CP (Gudelj; Grinius). (R-CMP-058)

## III–IV Management
- **Equal management** of CP personal property (§ 1100(a)). (R-CMP-062)
- **Fiduciary duty:** highest good faith; full disclosure; accounting; until division (§§ 721, 1100(e)). (R-CMP-063)
- **Gifts need written consent;** voidable entirely during life, one-half after death (§ 1100(b); Spreckels; Fields). (R-CMP-064)
- **Home furnishings/clothing:** written consent to sell or encumber (§ 1100(c)). (R-CMP-065)
- **Business:** managing spouse acts alone; prior written notice to sell substantially all (§ 1100(d)). (R-CMP-066)
- **Real property:** both must join (sale, encumbrance, lease > 1 yr); BFP presumption if title in one name; 1-year limit (§ 1102). (R-CMP-067)
- **§ 1101 remedies:** accounting; title; 50% + fees; 100% if § 3294 fraud/malice/oppression (Rossi). (R-CMP-068)
- **SP:** owner manages alone (§ 770(b)). (R-CMP-069)

## V Separation
- **DOS:** complete and final break = expressed intent to end marriage + consistent conduct; all evidence; same roof possible (§ 70; abrogating Davis). (R-CMP-070)
- **Post-DOS earnings/accumulations = SP** (§ 771). (R-CMP-071)
- **Post-DOS business growth:** apportion labor vs. capital (Imperato). (R-CMP-072)
- **Epstein:** SP pays community debt post-DOS → reimbursement (unless support/gift/offset). (R-CMP-073)
- **Watts:** exclusive use of CP asset post-DOS → charge for fair rental value. (R-CMP-074)

## VI Division and debts
- **Equal division** of net community estate; value near trial (§§ 2550, 2552). (R-CMP-075)
- **Misappropriation:** award/offset (§ 2602). (R-CMP-076)
- **Nondisclosure:** 50% / 100% (§ 1101(g)–(h)); sanctions (§ 2107). (R-CMP-077)
- **PI from third party:** all to injured spouse unless justice requires; at least half; commingled → equal (§ 2603). (R-CMP-078)
- **PI from spouse:** injured spouse's SP; tortfeasor's SP first (§§ 781(c), 782). (R-CMP-079)
- **Educational loans:** to the student (§§ 2627, 2641). (R-CMP-080)
- **Tort liabilities not for community benefit:** to tortfeasor without offset; debts > assets as just (§§ 2627, 2622(b)). (R-CMP-081)
- **Out-of-state land:** divide without changing title; else conveyance or money value (§ 2660). (R-CMP-083)
- **Creditors:** community estate liable for either spouse's pre-DOS debts (§ 910); earnings exempt from spouse's premarital debts if kept separate (§ 911); debtor's SP liable, non-debtor's SP not (§ 913); necessaries (§ 914); QCP = CP (§ 912). (R-CMP-084 to R-CMP-087)
- **Torts:** community-benefit activity → CP then tortfeasor's SP; otherwise tortfeasor's SP then CP (§ 1000). (R-CMP-088)
- **Allocation:** premarital → incurring spouse; marital → equal; post-DOS → incurring spouse unless necessaries (§§ 2620–2627). (R-CMP-089)
- **After division:** own debts follow you; spouse's debts only if assigned (§ 916). (R-CMP-090)

## VII Death
- **CP:** half to survivor; decedent wills SP + half CP + half own QCP; item by item (Prob. §§ 100, 101, 6101). (R-CMP-091)
- **QCP at death:** decedent's QCP only; personal property anywhere + CA land; not out-of-state land (Prob. §§ 66, 101). (R-CMP-092)
- **Recapture (§ 102):** QCP given away without consideration/consent with retained control or survivorship → transferee restores half to the estate, for the survivor; not insurance, annuities or pensions. (R-CMP-093)
- **Intestacy:** survivor takes all CP, decedent's half of QCP, and 1, 1/2 or 1/3 of SP (Prob. § 6401). (R-CMP-094)
- **Election:** will disposes of survivor's half + gives benefits → survivor elects. (R-CMP-095)
- **Nonprobate CP transfers** need written consent; consent ≠ transmutation (Prob. §§ 5020–5022). (R-CMP-096)
- **Omitted spouse:** half CP, half QCP, SP intestate share ≤ 1/2 (Prob. § 21610). (R-CMP-097)

## VIII Federal preemption
- **Supremacy Clause:** no division or offset of Social Security, Railroad Retirement, waived military pay (Hisquierdo; Mansell; Howell); military retired pay divisible only under USFSPA; ERISA by QDRO (Boggs; Egelhoff); federal insurance and bonds follow federal designations. (R-CMP-100 to R-CMP-103)
