## Scope

Characterization of property as community, separate or quasi-community; presumptions and title; premarital agreements and transmutation; commingling and tracing; apportionment of businesses (Pereira/Van Camp) and homes (Moore/Marsden); reimbursement (§§ 2640, 2641; Epstein/Watts); pensions, disability, stock options, life insurance and goodwill; management and control and the spousal fiduciary duty (§§ 721, 1100–1102); date of separation (§ 70); equal division and its statutory deviations; liability for debts; community and quasi-community property at death; federal preemption; putative spouses.

## How it is tested

- **Format.** A one-hour essay. The call almost always lists assets ("What are H's and W's rights in the house, the stock, the car and the loan?") and says to answer under California law. Selected answers work **asset by asset**: characterize at acquisition, apply presumptions, test transmutation, trace or apportion, reimburse, divide [`grader-expectations.md` §5](../../00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md).
- **Most-tested issues** (Phase 3 tags; indicative only [VERIFY V-071]): general and title presumptions (5), management/fiduciary duty (4), quasi-community property (4), transmutation and premarital agreements (3), separate property and characterization generally (6), date of separation and post-separation earnings (4), debts (3).
