# Community Property — essay templates

**Built from the past essays.** The State Bar's CP calls (Feb 2012 – Jul 2026) fall into six recurring shapes [S-079]. Selected answers analyze **asset by asset** and end each asset with a conclusion about who gets what ([grader-expectations §5](../../00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md)). Use these skeletons; fill in rules from [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md). Facts in the illustrations are invented.

**Timing for a one-hour essay:** 10–15 minutes reading and outlining, 45–50 minutes writing. Spend time in proportion to how much the facts develop each asset.

## Template 1 — Dissolution: "What are H's and W's rights in [assets 1–4]?" (most common)

Seen in: Jul 2017 Q1; Feb 2022 Q2; Feb 2024 Q1; Feb 2026 Q5; Jul 2013 Q3; Feb 2014 Q2.

```
Introduction (3–4 sentences)
  CA is a CP state; § 760 presumption; § 770 SP; source/time/actions of parties; equal division § 2550.
  [If disputed] Date of separation (§ 70): two candidate dates → conclusion.

1. [Asset 1]
   a. Source and time of acquisition → general presumption (CP or SP)
   b. Title presumptions (one name: Valli; joint: § 2581)
   c. Actions of the parties: transmutation (§ 852 writing? express? § 852(c)?) → undue influence (§ 721)
   d. Commingling/tracing (Mix; See) OR apportionment (Pereira/Van Camp; Moore/Marsden)
   e. Reimbursement (§ 2640; § 2641; improvements; Epstein/Watts)
   f. Conclusion: character + division (e.g., "CP; equal division after W's $40,000 § 2640 reimbursement")
2. [Asset 2] … same sub-steps (skip those the facts do not raise)
3. [Asset 3] …
4. [Asset 4 — often a debt] → see Template 4 sub-headings
Conclusion (1–2 lines)
```

**Paragraph pattern under each sub-step:** Rule (1–2 sentences) → "Here, …" with the specific facts → "However, [other side] …" → "Therefore, likely …"

## Template 2 — Separate business or premarital house grown with community effort or funds

Seen in: Feb 2021 Q3 (inherited business); Jul 2013 Q3 (restaurant; rental property); Feb 2026 Q5 (restaurant; house).

```
[Business]
  Character at acquisition: SP (premarital / inheritance) → § 770
  Community labor during marriage → apportionment required
    Pereira: rule → numbers (SP capital + fair return; rest CP)
    Van Camp: rule → numbers (salary value − draws = CP; rest SP)
    Which method? (personal effort vs. capital/market) → conclusion with figures
  Post-separation growth (Imperato) if valued at trial
  Goodwill (if a practice)
  Division of the CP share

[House bought before marriage]
  Character: SP at acquisition (inception of title)
  CP payments on principal → Moore/Marsden: CP% → CP share → SP share (show formula)
  CP-funded improvements → reimbursement
  Transmutation? (added spouse to title → § 852 + undue influence)
  Conclusion and division
```

## Template 3 — Agreements: premarital agreement and/or transmutation

Seen in: Mar 2025 Q2 (premarital agreement; support waiver; funds, stock, watch); Feb 2024 Q1 (house from aunt added to title); Feb 2014 Q2.

```
I. Is the premarital agreement enforceable?
   A. Formation (§ 1611: writing, signed, no consideration)
   B. Voluntariness (§ 1615(c)): counsel or separate written waiver; seven days with final agreement;
      written explanation if unrepresented; duress/fraud/undue influence/capacity; other factors
   C. Unconscionability + disclosure (§ 1615(a)(2))
   D. Spousal-support waiver (§ 1612(c): independent counsel; unconscionable at enforcement)
   E. [PR crossover] Lawyer-spouse drafting: conflicts, communication with an unrepresented person
   Conclusion: enforceable in whole / in part / not at all
II. Characterization of each asset — under the agreement if enforceable, under default law if not
   [Asset] → (agreement term) → default rules → transmutation (§ 852; § 852(c) for the watch/jewelry) → conclusion
```

## Template 4 — Debts and creditors ("Can the creditor reach…?" / "Who is liable for the loan?")

Seen in: Jul 2012 Q2 (tort judgment and lawyer's lien against the home); Jul 2013 Q3 (tort judgment); Feb 2022 Q2 and Feb 2024 Q1 (loans).

```
[Debt]
  1. When incurred and by whom (before marriage / during marriage before DOS / after DOS)
  2. Liability to the creditor
     - Community estate (§ 910) — includes QCP (§ 912)
     - § 911 earnings exception (premarital debts; separate uncommingled account)
     - Debtor's SP (§ 913(a)); non-debtor's SP not liable (§ 913(b)) — necessaries exception (§ 914)
     - Tort: community-benefit test and order of satisfaction (§ 1000)
     - Forged/unauthorized encumbrance of CP land: joinder (§ 1102)
  3. Allocation between spouses at dissolution (§§ 2620–2627) and reimbursement (§§ 920, 2626)
  4. Conclusion: which property the creditor can reach; who bears it in the end
```

## Template 5 — Management and fiduciary duty ("What are W's rights regarding H's [gift / sale / loan]?")

Seen in: Jul 2012 Q2; Feb 2014 Q2 (mirror given away; office building sold); Feb 2024 Q1 (forged signature).

```
[Transaction]
  1. Character of the property involved (CP? SP? QCP?)
  2. Management rule
     - Personal property: equal management (§ 1100(a)); gifts need written consent (§ 1100(b));
       household goods (§ 1100(c)); business notice (§ 1100(d))
     - Real property: joinder (§ 1102); BFP presumption; one-year limit
  3. Fiduciary duty (§§ 721, 1100(e)) — breach?
  4. Remedies: set aside (whole during life / half after death); § 1101 accounting, 50%, 100%;
     § 2602 misappropriation at division
  5. Conclusion
```

## Template 6 — Death and quasi-community property (Wills crossover)

Seen in: Jul 2014 Q5; Feb 2018 Q5; Feb 2019 Q1; Jul 2022 Q5; Jul 2026 Q2.

```
Introduction: CA CP state; at death survivor owns half of CP (Prob. § 100) and half of decedent's QCP (§ 101);
  decedent may will SP + half CP + half own QCP (§ 6101).
I. [WILLS] Validity of the will / codicil; revocation; omitted spouse (§ 21610) — per the call
II. Characterize each asset
   [Asset]
     Domicile at acquisition → CP, SP or QCP?
     At death: QCP = personal property anywhere + CA land (Prob. § 66); out-of-state land → situs law
     Title/joint tenancy (survivorship controls at death; Brace did not disturb it); transmutation
     Transfers during life: unconsented gift of CP (§ 1100(b); half after death); QCP recapture (§ 102);
       nonprobate transfer without consent (Prob. §§ 5020–5021)
III. Distribution
     Testate: decedent's half passes by will; survivor's half stays; election if will reaches survivor's half
     Intestate: survivor takes all CP + decedent's half of QCP + 1, 1/2 or 1/3 of SP (§ 6401); rest to other heirs (§ 6402)
IV. Conclusion per claimant (spouse; children; siblings; others)
```

## Writing reminders

- **Call order controls headings**; if the call names parties, conclude for each party.
- **Two-sided analysis** on the contestable points: date of separation; § 852(c) "substantial in value"; Pereira vs. Van Camp; undue influence rebuttal; lender intent; community benefit for a tort.
- **Show formulas** (Moore/Marsden, Pereira, Van Camp, time rule) even when numbers are missing.
- **Never stop at "it is CP."** Finish with reimbursement and division.
