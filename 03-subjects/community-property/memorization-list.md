## Numbers and formulas to memorize

- **Moore/Marsden:** CP% = CP principal ÷ purchase price; CP share = CP principal + CP% × marital appreciation; premarital appreciation → SP.
- **Pereira:** SP = capital + (capital × rate × years); CP = rest.
- **Van Camp:** CP = (reasonable salary − salary drawn) × years; SP = rest.
- **Time rule:** marital months of service before DOS ÷ total months of service.
- **Hug/Nelson:** (start of employment or grant → DOS) ÷ (same start → exercisable date).
- **§ 2640:** no interest; no appreciation; capped at net value. **§ 2641:** legal-rate interest; 10-year presumptions.
- **§ 1101:** 50% (+ fees) / 100% (Civ. Code § 3294).
- **§ 1615(c):** 7 calendar days with the final agreement.
- **§ 1102:** lease > 1 year needs joinder; 1 year to sue after recording.
- **§ 2603:** at least one-half of PI damages to the injured spouse.
- **§ 6401(c):** SP share 1 (no close relatives) / 1/2 (one child, or parents or their issue) / 1/3 (two or more children).
