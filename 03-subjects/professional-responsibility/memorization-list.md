**Must-know numbers and words:**
- "at every peril" (§ 6068(e)(1));
- **criminal act** → death/SBH (§ 6068(e)(2));
- informed **written** consent;
- **$1,000** (§ 6148);
- "not set by law and is negotiable" (§ 6147);
- **14 days** notice / **45 days** distribution (rule 1.15);
- **30 days** and **3 suits in 12 months** (§ 6068(o));
- rule 8.3 effective **Aug. 1, 2023**, "credible evidence";
- rule 7.3(f) effective **June 1, 2026**;
- MPRE passing score **86**.
