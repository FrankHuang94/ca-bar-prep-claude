# Professional Responsibility — attack outline

**Format for every issue:** **Heading** → ABA rule → "California's rule is the same / differs: …" → facts → conclusion ("likely violated both" / "violated the ABA rule but not California's"). Lawyer by lawyer. Cite B&P sections where they exist.

## Lawyer 1: [Name]

### Duty of loyalty (conflicts)
- **Current client (1.7):** direct adversity or significant risk of material limitation (other client, former client, third person, own interest) → reasonable belief + not prohibited + not same-litigation adversity + consent. **CA:** informed written consent; **ABA:** informed consent confirmed in writing. (R-PRF-021)
- **CA 1.7(c):** written disclosure of relationships with a party/witness or with opposing counsel (relative, cohabitant, client, intimate). No ABA twin. (R-PRF-022)
- **Business transaction (1.8.1 / 1.8(a)):** fair terms in writing + written advice to get independent counsel + written consent to the terms and the lawyer's role (ABA: incl. whether lawyer represents client in the deal); CA: charging lien counts. (R-PRF-026)
- **Gifts (1.8.3 / 1.8(c)):** no soliciting/drafting substantial gifts unless related; CA certificate of independent review. (R-PRF-027)
- **Financial assistance (1.8.5 / 1.8(e)):** costs only (ABA); CA allows post-retention loans with written promise to repay. (R-PRF-028)
- **Third-party payor (1.8.6 / 1.8(f)):** no interference + confidentiality + consent (CA written). (R-PRF-029)
- **Malpractice limits (1.8.8 / 1.8(h)):** CA never prospectively; ABA only if independently represented; settlement needs written advice to get counsel; B&P § 6090.5 no State Bar gag. (R-PRF-031)
- **Sex with client (1.8.10 / 1.8(j)):** no, unless pre-existing; CA § 6106.9. (R-PRF-032)
- **Former client (1.9):** same/substantially related + materially adverse + no written consent. (R-PRF-034)
- **Imputation (1.10):** firm-wide; ABA screens any lateral; CA screens only if no substantial participation. (R-PRF-036, R-PRF-037)
- **Organization (1.13):** client = entity; report up; ABA may report out, CA may not. (R-PRF-055, R-PRF-056)

### Duty of confidentiality
- **Rule:** ABA 1.6(a) — no revealing information relating to the representation without consent. CA § 6068(e)(1) — "maintain inviolate … at every peril … preserve the secrets"; rule 1.6. (R-PRF-013)
- **Exceptions:** CA only criminal act → death/SBH, permissive, dissuade first (R-PRF-014). ABA adds financial crime/fraud using lawyer's services, self-defense, ethics advice, court order, conflicts checks (R-PRF-015).
- **vs. privilege:** confidentiality = all information, all settings; privilege = communications, compelled testimony; Evid. Code §§ 954, 956, 956.5, 958, 962. (R-PRF-016, R-PRF-017)
- **Physical evidence:** examine briefly, turn over; never hide; *Meredith*. (R-PRF-020)

### Competence / diligence / communication
- **Competence:** ABA knowledge, skill, thoroughness, preparation; CA intentional/reckless/grossly negligent/repeated; cure by associating, learning, referring. (R-PRF-008)
- **Diligence:** reasonable diligence and promptness; CA same threshold. (R-PRF-009)
- **Communication:** inform, consult, keep informed, respond; § 6068(m); settlement offers 1.4.1 / § 6103.5; CA insurance disclosure 1.4.2. (R-PRF-010 to R-PRF-012)
- **Authority:** client decides objectives, settlement, plea, jury, testify. (R-PRF-005)

### Fees and trust account
- **Standard:** ABA unreasonable; CA unconscionable. (R-PRF-041)
- **Writing:** § 6148 (> $1,000), § 6147 (contingency, "negotiable" statement) — voidable → reasonable fee. (R-PRF-042, R-PRF-043)
- **No contingency** in criminal defense or divorce/support. (R-PRF-044)
- **Nonrefundable** only for a true retainer, in writing. (R-PRF-045)
- **Splits:** ABA proportion or joint responsibility + client written agreement; CA written lawyer agreement + client written consent after written disclosure + no increase; referral fee OK in CA. (R-PRF-046)
- **Trust:** no commingling; CA notice ≤ 14 days; distribute undisputed ≤ 45 days (presumption); disputed portion stays. (R-PRF-047 to R-PRF-049)

### Withdrawal
- **Mandatory:** violation; impairment; fired; CA harassing suit; ABA 2023 crime/fraud persistence and inquiry duty. (R-PRF-050)
- **Permissive:** CA ten grounds; ABA adds "no material adverse effect" and "repugnant". (R-PRF-051)
- **On exit:** court permission; notice; file returned even if unpaid (CA); refund. (R-PRF-052, R-PRF-053)

### Advocate
- **Merit (3.1):** no frivolous claims; § 6068(c). **Delay (3.2).** (R-PRF-058, R-PRF-059)
- **Candor (3.3):** no false statements; correct; adverse controlling authority; ex parte all material facts; § 6068(d). (R-PRF-060)
- **False evidence:** remonstrate → withdraw → ABA disclose / CA no disclosure; criminal defendant narrative (CA). (R-PRF-061)
- **Fairness (3.4):** no obstruction/destruction/suppression; witness pay only expenses + lost time + expert fee, nothing contingent; ABA 3.4(f) no asking non-clients to stay silent (no CA twin). (R-PRF-062 to R-PRF-064)
- **Closing:** ABA bars personal opinion on cause, credibility, civil culpability, guilt; CA 3.4(g) only guilt/innocence + personal knowledge. (R-PRF-066)
- **Judges/jurors (3.5); publicity (3.6); lawyer-witness (3.7):** ABA hardship vs. CA informed written consent (prosecutor: head of office consents). (R-PRF-067 to R-PRF-069)
- **Prosecutor (3.8):** probable cause; timely disclosure of exculpatory and mitigating information. (R-PRF-070)
- **CA 3.10:** no threats of criminal, administrative or disciplinary charges in a civil dispute; ABA Op. 92-363. (R-PRF-071)

### Third persons
- **4.1** no false material statements; **4.2** no contact with represented person without counsel's consent; **4.3** no neutrality claims or advice to the unrepresented (CA 4.3(b) no fishing for privileged information); **4.4** inadvertent documents: CA stop reading + notify, ABA notify. (R-PRF-072 to R-PRF-075)

### Firm, staff, UPL
- **5.1** managers and supervisors; **5.2** subordinate excused only for a reasonable resolution of an arguable question; **5.3** nonlawyers; **CA 5.3.1** disbarred and suspended lawyers + notice. (R-PRF-076 to R-PRF-079)
- **5.4** no fee sharing or ownership with nonlawyers (CA 5.4(a)(6): settlement fees with a 501(c)(3) nonprofit); AB 931. **5.6** no practice restrictions; CA 5.6(b) no agreement barring reports of violations. (R-PRF-080, R-PRF-081)
- **5.5 / § 6125:** no UPL or assisting it; MJP: CA Rules of Court 9.40–9.48, *Birbrower*; ABA 5.5(c) safe harbors. (R-PRF-082, R-PRF-083)

### Advertising
- **7.1** not false or misleading; **7.2** no paying for recommendations (gift exceptions differ); **7.3** no live solicitation for gain (CA: "Advertisement" label; DVRO 2026); **7.4/7.5** specialist and firm names. (R-PRF-085 to R-PRF-088)

### Integrity
- **8.3 report:** CA credible evidence of crime/dishonesty/misappropriation → State Bar; ABA knows of a violation raising a substantial question. (R-PRF-091)
- **8.4 misconduct;** **§ 6106** moral turpitude; **§ 6068(o)** self-report in 30 days; **8.4.1** vs. ABA 8.4(g). (R-PRF-092 to R-PRF-095)
