## Scope
The State Bar's PR outline follows the ABA Model Rules chapters [S-082]:
- I. client-lawyer relationship;
- II. counselor;
- III. advocate;
- IV. transactions with persons other than clients;
- V. law firms and associations;
- VI. public service;
- VII. information about legal services;
- VIII. integrity of the profession.

This repo adds seven California-specific rows:
- CA vs. ABA compare and contrast;
- State Bar Act duties, especially § 6068(e) confidentiality;
- confidentiality vs. privilege and work product;
- loyalty and conflicts;
- competence, diligence and communication;
- the 2023 duty to report (rule 8.3);
- UPL and multijurisdictional practice.

Current law for July 2027 is reflected from `00-exam-intel/recent-changes.md` §6:
- rule 8.3 (2023);
- rule 7.3(f) (June 2026);
- the denied rule 8.2/8.4 amendments (April 2026);
- the pending AI amendments (not adopted);
- CTAPP;
- AB 931;
- ABA MR 1.16(a) (2023) and MR 1.14 (2026).

## Study order (fits your Fall 2026 course and the March MPRE)
1. [`primer.md`](primer.md) — map of the subject and the eight ideas that matter.
2. [`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md) — learn the CA-vs-ABA table **early**; it is the scoring difference.
3. [`outline.md`](outline.md) I.D (confidentiality) and I.E (conflicts) — the two most-tested duties.
4. The rest of [`outline.md`](outline.md) with [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md) open (100 rules, each with a CA vs. ABA line).
5. [`flowcharts.md`](flowcharts.md) and [`worked-examples.md`](worked-examples.md).
6. [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md) + [`issue-checklist.md`](issue-checklist.md) → write a timed past essay (Feb. 2024 Q3 or Jul. 2018 Q3).
7. Before the MPRE (Mar. 2027): outline section X "MPRE notes" — switch to ABA-only answers.
8. Final weeks: [`attack-outline.md`](attack-outline.md), [`memorization-list.md`](memorization-list.md), [`flashcards.csv`](flashcards.csv), [`traps.md`](traps.md).
