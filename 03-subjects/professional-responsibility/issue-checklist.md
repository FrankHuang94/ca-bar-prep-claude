# Professional Responsibility — issue checklist

**Use:** run this list **once per lawyer** in the facts. For each box ticked, write a heading, the ABA rule, the California rule, application and a conclusion under each. Rule IDs point to [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## 0. Threshold (always first)
- [ ] **Read the call.** Does it name lawyers ("Tom and Alan")? Specific conduct ("regarding five listed matters")? Ask for "California and ABA authorities"? Ask about civil liability or disqualification as well as discipline? *Structure the answer on the call.*
- [ ] **List every lawyer** — the obvious one, co-counsel, the supervising partner, opposing counsel, the in-house lawyer, the out-of-state lawyer, the disbarred "paralegal". The 2024 and 2026 essays both had a second lawyer.
- [ ] **Who is the client?** Organization vs. officer/employee; insured vs. insurer; child vs. paying parent; prospective client. (R-PRF-002, R-PRF-003, R-PRF-055) *Trigger:* "the company's lawyer", "her mother paid", "came in for a consultation".
- [ ] **When did the relationship start or end?** Current vs. former vs. prospective client changes the conflict rule. (R-PRF-034)

## 1. Duty of loyalty — conflicts (most tested)
- [ ] **Direct adversity** to a current client. *Trigger:* firm sues or negotiates against an existing client; cross-examining a client. (R-PRF-021)
- [ ] **Material limitation**: another client, former client, third person, or the **lawyer's own interest**. *Triggers:* job offer from opposing firm; membership/funding of an activist group; dating opposing counsel; lawyer's investment in the deal; lawyer's own malpractice exposure. (R-PRF-021, R-PRF-023)
- [ ] **California written disclosure** (rule 1.7(c)) — relationship with a party or witness; opposing counsel is a relative, cohabitant, client or intimate partner. (R-PRF-022)
- [ ] **Joint representation** — co-defendants, buyer/seller, spouses, entity + officer. (R-PRF-024)
- [ ] **Rule 1.8 specific conflicts.** (R-PRF-026 to R-PRF-033)
  - business deal or investment with a client; a charging lien (CA);
  - gift or will naming the lawyer;
  - loan to a client;
  - third party paying the fee;
  - aggregate settlement;
  - malpractice release or refund-for-release;
  - sexual or romantic relationship;
  - media rights.
- [ ] **Former client** — same or substantially related, materially adverse. (R-PRF-034, R-PRF-035)
- [ ] **Imputation / screening** — firm-wide effect; lateral hires; office-sharers holding out as a firm; of counsel. (R-PRF-036, R-PRF-037)
- [ ] **Government lawyers, judges, neutrals.** (R-PRF-038)
- [ ] **Insurance defense / Cumis.** (R-PRF-039)
- [ ] **Consent**: valid? informed **written** (CA) vs. confirmed in writing (ABA)? consentable at all? (R-PRF-021)

## 2. Duty of confidentiality (second most tested)
- [ ] **Disclosure** to anyone (family member, payor, press, government, the other lawyer in a shared office). (R-PRF-013)
- [ ] **Safeguards** — shared printers, receptionists, email, cloud/AI tools. (R-PRF-013)
- [ ] **Exception?** CA: criminal act → death/SBH only (R-PRF-014). ABA: 1.6(b)(1)–(7), incl. financial fraud, self-defense, court order (R-PRF-015).
- [ ] **Organization**: report up (R-PRF-055); report out — ABA yes, CA no (R-PRF-056).
- [ ] **Use** of client information to the client's disadvantage. (R-PRF-019)
- [ ] **Privilege / work product** if the facts involve testimony or subpoenas (Evidence crossover). (R-PRF-016 to R-PRF-018)
- [ ] **Physical evidence** — took, moved, hid, returned or turned over an item. (R-PRF-020)

## 3. Competence, diligence, communication
- [ ] New area of law; no association; missed deadline; no investigation; AI-drafted brief with fake citations. (R-PRF-008, R-PRF-009)
- [ ] Unanswered calls; client not told of settlement offer, conflict, bad news, the lawyer's mistake. (R-PRF-010, R-PRF-011)
- [ ] Uninsured lawyer — rule 1.4.2 (CA). (R-PRF-012)
- [ ] Scope: who decides? Settled without consent; plea or testimony decision overridden; limited-scope agreement. (R-PRF-005, R-PRF-006)
- [ ] Assisting client crime, fraud or (CA) violation of law; advice skewed by the lawyer's views. (R-PRF-007, R-PRF-057)

## 4. Fees and client property
- [ ] Fee amount: unreasonable (ABA) / unconscionable (CA). (R-PRF-041)
- [ ] No written agreement (§ 6148 > $1,000); contingency agreement missing terms (§ 6147). (R-PRF-042, R-PRF-043)
- [ ] Contingent fee in a criminal or divorce case — including non-cash rewards. (R-PRF-044)
- [ ] "Nonrefundable" retainer; flat fee; advance fee not refunded. (R-PRF-045)
- [ ] Fee split with another lawyer or referral fee. (R-PRF-046)
- [ ] Fee sharing with a nonlawyer; paying for referrals. (R-PRF-080, R-PRF-086)
- [ ] Trust account: commingling, late notice (> 14 days), late distribution (> 45 days), disputed funds, liens, taking fees early. (R-PRF-047 to R-PRF-049)

## 5. Ending the relationship
- [ ] Must withdraw (violation; impairment; fired; CA harassing suit; ABA crime/fraud persistence). (R-PRF-050)
- [ ] May withdraw (non-payment after warning; client conduct; ABA "repugnant"). (R-PRF-051)
- [ ] Court permission; notice; file return (CA: even if unpaid); refund. (R-PRF-052, R-PRF-053)

## 6. Advocate duties
- [ ] Frivolous motion or claim; delay tactics. (R-PRF-058, R-PRF-059)
- [ ] False statement to a court; adverse authority; ex parte TRO without the bad facts. (R-PRF-060)
- [ ] Client or witness lies; expert "should have known" false; perjury plans. (R-PRF-061)
- [ ] Hiding, destroying or withholding evidence; discovery games. (R-PRF-062, R-PRF-065)
- [ ] Paying witnesses; inducements; telling witnesses not to talk or to leave. (R-PRF-063, R-PRF-064)
- [ ] Closing argument: personal opinion or personal knowledge. (R-PRF-066)
- [ ] Ex parte contact with judge or jurors; gifts to court staff. (R-PRF-067)
- [ ] Press statements. (R-PRF-068)
- [ ] Lawyer likely to be a witness. (R-PRF-069)
- [ ] Prosecutor: probable cause, *Brady*/3.8(d), plea waivers from unrepresented accused. (R-PRF-070)
- [ ] **Threat** to report crime, agency complaint or State Bar complaint in a civil dispute (CA 3.10). (R-PRF-071)

## 7. Third persons
- [ ] Lies in negotiation; failure to correct a client's fraud. (R-PRF-072)
- [ ] Contact with a represented party or its employees. (R-PRF-073)
- [ ] Advice to an unrepresented person; implying neutrality. (R-PRF-074)
- [ ] Inadvertently received privileged email. (R-PRF-075)

## 8. Firms, supervision, UPL
- [ ] Partner orders, associate obeys; supervisor fails to stop. (R-PRF-076, R-PRF-077)
- [ ] Untrained staff; son as receptionist. (R-PRF-078)
- [ ] Disbarred or suspended person working on cases. (R-PRF-079)
- [ ] Nonlawyer partner, investor, or "marketing" fee share. (R-PRF-080)
- [ ] Out-of-state lawyer in California; lawyer practising in another state; nonlawyer giving advice. (R-PRF-082, R-PRF-083)
- [ ] Non-compete or practice-restriction settlement term. (R-PRF-081)

## 9. Advertising and solicitation
- [ ] Misleading website, guarantees, testimonials, "specialist" claims, fake firm name. (R-PRF-085, R-PRF-088)
- [ ] Gifts or payments to referral sources (chiropractor, tow-truck driver, hospital worker). (R-PRF-086)
- [ ] In-person, phone or real-time contact with strangers; targeted letters without "Advertisement" (CA); DVRO respondents (CA 2026). (R-PRF-087)

## 10. Integrity of the profession
- [ ] Another lawyer's misconduct the actor knows about — duty to report (CA credible evidence / ABA knows). (R-PRF-091)
- [ ] Lawyer's own crime, dishonesty, moral turpitude; DUI; private fraud. (R-PRF-092, R-PRF-093)
- [ ] Self-reporting: three malpractice suits in 12 months; sanctions ≥ $1,000; felony charge; certain convictions. (R-PRF-094)
- [ ] Harassment or discrimination (CA 8.4.1 vs. ABA 8.4(g)). (R-PRF-095)
- [ ] Bar-application candor; statements about judges. (R-PRF-089, R-PRF-090)

## 11. Consequences (only if the call asks)
- [ ] Discipline; disqualification; malpractice; breach of fiduciary duty; fee forfeiture; unenforceable fee agreement. (R-PRF-040, R-PRF-097 to R-PRF-099)
