# Torts — memorization list

**How the tiers were set.** They combine MBE weight (negligence is about half the Torts questions [S-040]) with California essay frequency (Phase 3 tags [VERIFY V-071]: property torts, negligence, intentional torts, misrepresentation, independent contractors, defamation, nuisance and products appear most).

**How to learn each tier.**
- **Must-know:** learn word for word from [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).
- **Should-know:** learn as element lists.
- **Nice-to-know:** be able to recognize them.

Flashcard tags in [`flashcards.csv`](flashcards.csv) follow these tiers.

## Five-minute daily recall set (the essay core)
1. Negligence prima facie case and *Rowland* (R-TRT-018, R-TRT-020).
2. Land entrants and *Rowland* (R-TRT-047 to R-TRT-051).
3. *Thing* bystander test (R-TRT-055).
4. *Knight* primary and secondary assumption of risk (R-TRT-073).
5. Products: three defects and the *Barker* burden shift (R-TRT-082 to R-TRT-084).
6. Pure comparative fault and Prop 51 (R-TRT-042, R-TRT-070).
7. The defamation fault grid (R-TRT-098, R-TRT-099).
8. Private nuisance balancing (R-TRT-092).
