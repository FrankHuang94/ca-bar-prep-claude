## Scope
- **MBE** (NCBE outline [S-040]):
  - I Intentional torts (harms to the person and property; defenses and privileges);
  - II Negligence (duty; standard of care; proof of fault; causation and apportionment; limitations on liability: proximate cause, land, emotional distress, economic loss; liability for others; defenses);
  - III Strict liability and products liability;
  - IV Other torts (nuisance; defamation and privacy; misrepresentation; interference with business relations, whose MBE status is unconfirmed [VERIFY V-023]).
  - **About half** of the questions come from II; the other half from I, III and IV together.
- **California essay outline** [S-092]: intentional torts (to the person; to reputation and similar interests; to property); negligence; wrongful death and survival; misrepresentation; economic torts; products liability; nuisance; strict liability; liability for acts of others; immunities. The numbering is provisional [VERIFY V-024].

**Typical shape.**
- The call names parties ("What claims can P bring against A and B, and what defenses?").
- Organize by **Π v. Δ**, then by tort, with defenses and damages for each.
- Products essays go theory by theory: strict liability (by defect), negligence, warranty, misrepresentation, intent, then defenses.
- Land-use essays pair nuisance and trespass with an injunction analysis.

## Study order (coursework done: refresh, then drill)
1. **Primer** ([`primer.md`](primer.md)): 30 minutes to rebuild the map.
2. **Negligence first** (outline II): duty → standard → breach → causation → limits → vicarious liability → defenses. It is half the MBE and the core of most essays. Memorize R-TRT-018 to R-TRT-073 (must-know tier).
3. **Products and strict liability** (III): the essay order and the California design-defect tests.
4. **Intentional torts and privileges** (I).
5. **Other torts** (IV): nuisance, defamation and privacy, misrepresentation, interference.
6. **California overlay** ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md)): one sentence per difference.
7. **Practice:** 20–25 MBE questions per session; one timed essay per week in the Torts block (the plan's W11 block), using [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md).
