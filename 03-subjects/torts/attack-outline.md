# Torts — attack outline

Numbers in parentheses are rule IDs (043 = R-TRT-043).

**Organize:** call → Π v. Δ pairs → torts → defenses → damages. Every issue gets a conclusion. Majority rule first; "In California, …" second. Full rules: [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md).

## A. Intentional torts
- **Intent:** purpose or substantial certainty; transfers among the five trespass torts and between victims (001).
- **Battery:** intentional harmful or offensive contact with Π's person or anything connected to it (002).
- **Assault:** intentional act causing reasonable apprehension of an imminent harmful or offensive contact (003).
- **False imprisonment:** intentional confinement within fixed boundaries, no reasonable known escape, Π aware or harmed (004).
- **IIED:** extreme and outrageous conduct, intent or recklessness, causing severe distress; third parties under § 46(2); CA *Christensen* (005).
- **Trespass to land:** intentional physical entry onto land in Π's possession; mistake no defense; CA: intangibles are nuisance (*Wilson*) (006).
- **Trespass to chattels:** intentional intermeddling or dispossession causing actual harm (007).
- **Conversion:** serious interference justifying a forced sale at full value (008).

## B. Defenses to intentional torts
- **Consent:** express, apparent, customary, implied by law; capacity; scope; fraud as to essence (009).
- **Self-defense:** reasonable belief, proportional force; deadly only for deadly threats (010).
- **Defense of others:** reasonable belief that the third person may defend (majority) (011).
- **Defense of property:** reasonable nondeadly force after a request; no deadly devices (012).
- **Recapture and shopkeeper:** fresh pursuit, wrongful taking; reasonable detention on reasonable belief (013).
- **Discipline and arrest:** reasonable force for child training (014). Private-citizen felony arrest requires an actual felony (015).
- **Necessity:** public = complete privilege (016); private = incomplete, pay actual damage (017).

## C. Negligence
- **Prima facie case:** duty, breach, actual cause, proximate cause, damages (018).
- **Duty:** reasonable care to foreseeable plaintiffs (*Palsgraf*); CA: § 1714 and *Rowland*; *Kuciemba* no take-home COVID duty (019, 020).
- **Affirmative duties:** special relationship, peril created, undertaking (021); third parties: special relationship; CA: *Brown* two-step (022); *Tarasoff* and § 43.92 (023); rescuers and the firefighter's rule (024).
- **Standard:** reasonable person (objective; physical disability counted) (025); child of like age except adult activities (026); professionals (027); informed consent: material risks (028); carriers: utmost care (029); emergency (030).
- **Breach:** B < P × L (031); custom is evidence (032).
- **Negligence per se:** protected class + type of harm + unexcused violation; CA presumption (Evid. Code § 669) (033); excuses (034).
- **Res ipsa:** not without negligence + Δ's control → inference; CA § 646 (035). **Notice** for hazards (036).
- **Actual cause:** but-for (037); substantial factor (038); *Summers*, concert, market share (039); loss of chance (040).
- **Apportionment:** joint and several for indivisible harm (041); CA Prop 51 (042); contribution, indemnity, CCP § 877 (043).
- **Proximate cause:** foreseeable type of harm (044); eggshell (045); foreseeable intervening acts don't cut off; unforeseeable crimes supersede (046).
- **Land:** trespasser (047); attractive nuisance § 339 (048); licensee: known dangers (049); invitee: inspect (050); **CA:** *Rowland* no categories; § 846 recreational use (051); lessors (052); crime: *Ann M.* and *Delgado* (053).
- **Emotional distress:** zone of danger (054); bystander *Thing*: close relative, present and aware, serious distress (055); CA direct victim *Burgess* (056); fear of cancer *Potter* (057).
- **Other intangible harms:** consortium (058); wrongful death: heirs' losses (059); survival: decedent's pre-death losses, no pain and suffering in actions filed from 2026 (060); pure economic loss: special relationship only (061); wrongful birth and life (062).
- **Vicarious:** respondeat superior, scope; frolic vs. detour (063); independent contractor exceptions: nondelegable duty, inherently dangerous, own negligence; CA *Privette* (064); partners and owners (065); parents (066); negligent hiring and entrustment (067); alcohol: CA § 1714(b)–(d) (068).
- **Defenses:** contributory negligence and last clear chance (069); pure comparative fault (MBE and CA) (070); releases: *Tunkl*, no gross negligence (071); implied assumption of risk (072); **CA *Knight***: primary/secondary; co-participant intent or recklessness; *Kahn* coaches (073); immunities (074, 075); limitations periods (076).

## D. Strict liability and products
- **Abnormally dangerous activity:** § 520 factors; strict liability for the kind of harm that makes it dangerous (077).
- **Animals:** wild: strict (078); domestic: known propensity; CA § 3342 dog bites (079); defenses (080).
- **Strict products liability:** commercial seller, defect when it left Δ, causation, physical harm; CA: no "unreasonably dangerous" (*Cronin*) (081).
  - manufacturing: deviation from design (082);
  - design: consumer expectations or risk-utility; CA *Barker* burden shift; *Soule* (083);
  - warning: knowable risk, learned intermediary (084).
- **Who and whom:** retailers, lessors, components, successors (085); bystanders; no pure economic loss (086).
- **Defenses:** comparative fault, assumption of risk, unforeseeable misuse, alteration, state of the art, preemption (087).
- **Other product theories:** negligence (088); warranty §§ 2-313, 2-314, 2-315 (089); § 402B misrepresentation (090); intent rarely (091).

## E. Other torts
- **Private nuisance:** substantial and unreasonable interference with use and enjoyment (092). **Public nuisance:** special injury (093). **Defenses:** coming to the nuisance is a factor; statutory authorization (094). **Remedies:** damages, injunction, abatement (095).
- **Defamation:** defamatory fact, of and concerning Π, published, damages (096); libel vs. slander, per se, CA § 45a (097); public figure: actual malice (098); private figure: *Gertz* and *Dun & Bradstreet* (099); truth, opinion, privileges, CA § 47, § 48a, anti-SLAPP (100).
- **Privacy:** appropriation, CA § 3344 (101); intrusion (102); disclosure (103); false light (104).
- **Process:** malicious prosecution (105); abuse of process (106).
- **Misrepresentation:** fraud: false fact, scienter, intent, justifiable reliance, damages (107); concealment and duty to disclose; CA *Rattagan* (108); negligent misrepresentation § 552 (109).
- **Economic torts:** interference with contract (110); prospective advantage; CA independently wrongful act (111); trade libel (112).

## F. Damages
- **Compensatory:** economic and non-economic; collateral source; *Howell* (113).
- **Punitive:** malice, oppression or fraud; CA § 3294, clear and convincing evidence (114).
- **MICRA:** $510,000 / $700,000 caps in 2027 [VERIFY V-601] (115).
