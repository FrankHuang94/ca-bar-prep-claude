## Scope

The subject has four parts, following the NCBE outline [S-040]:
1. **I. Judicial review.** Federal courts, Congress's control of jurisdiction, state sovereign immunity, and justiciability: standing, ripeness, mootness, political questions, and adequate and independent state grounds.
2. **II. Separation of powers.** Congress's powers (commerce, tax, spending, war, § 5 enforcement), the President's powers (Youngstown, foreign affairs, appointment and removal), and interbranch checks (legislative veto, presentment, nondelegation, immunities).
3. **III. Federalism.** Intergovernmental immunities, the Tenth Amendment and anti-commandeering, the dormant Commerce Clause, preemption, and congressional authorization of state action.
4. **IV. Individual rights.** State action; due process; equal protection; takings; Article IV and Fourteenth Amendment privileges and immunities; the Contracts Clause; bills of attainder; ex post facto laws; unconstitutional conditions; the First Amendment (religion, speech, press, association); and the Second Amendment.

**What the MBE stresses.** About half of the MBE Con Law items come from category IV, and half from I–III together [S-040]. See [mbe-rules.md](mbe-rules.md).

**What California essays stress.** Individual rights and justiciability. Every Con Law essay since 2012 that was fully indexed raised either a First Amendment issue or an equal-protection or due-process issue, and justiciability opened at least four of them.

## Study order (no prior coursework)

1. **[primer.md](primer.md):** the map and vocabulary. Read it twice.
2. **Justiciability** (outline I.C), because it is the opening issue on essays.
3. **Individual rights core:** the levels of scrutiny, equal protection, due process, then the First Amendment (speech before religion). This is half the MBE and most essays.
4. **Federalism:** dormant Commerce Clause, P&I, preemption, Tenth Amendment.
5. **Federal powers and separation of powers:** commerce, spending, Youngstown, appointments and removal (note the 2026 change, *Trump v. Slaughter*).
6. **Remainder:** takings, Contracts Clause, ex post facto, Second Amendment, the Eleventh and Thirteenth Amendments.

After each block, drill its rules (`/drill`), do 20–30 MBE questions (`/quiz`), and write one essay under time (`/essay`).
