# Constitutional Law — issue checklist

**How to use.** Run this list against every Con Law fact pattern in the order shown. Threshold issues come first, as in California's selected answers (`00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md` §5). Build your headings from the **call**: one heading per plaintiff or per challenged law, then these issues underneath.

## A. Threshold (always first, for each plaintiff)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Which court? Who is the defendant?** | "sued in federal court"; a state, a state agency, a city, an official | R-CON-002, R-CON-005 |
| 2 | **Eleventh Amendment** | State or state agency named as defendant; damages sought | R-CON-005, R-CON-006 (sue the officer for prospective relief) |
| 3 | **Standing: injury** | Lost sales, fines, suspension, denied permit, chilled speech, "offended," "taxpayer," "citizen" | R-CON-008, R-CON-009 |
| 4 | **Causation and redressability** | A third party actually causes the harm; relief would not help | R-CON-010 |
| 5 | **Special standing** | Organization suing for members; vendor or doctor asserting others' rights; taxpayer; state; legislator; would-be intervenor | R-CON-011 to R-CON-014 |
| 6 | **Ripeness** | Law not yet enforced; plaintiff plans to act; pre-enforcement challenge; TRO before an event | R-CON-015 |
| 7 | **Mootness** | Plaintiff graduated, moved, was released; law repealed or suspended; election over; event passed | R-CON-016 (capable of repetition; voluntary cessation; damages; class action) |
| 8 | **Political question / abstention** | Impeachment, Guarantee Clause, partisan districting; pending state prosecution | R-CON-017, R-CON-019 |

## B. Government power (is there authority to act?)

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Federal statute — which power?** | "Congress enacted"; regulation of local activity; conditions on money; tax or penalty | R-CON-020 to R-CON-024 |
| 10 | **§ 5 / Thirteenth / Fifteenth enforcement** | Federal civil-rights statute; suits against states; private discrimination | R-CON-027 to R-CON-029 |
| 11 | **Tenth Amendment / commandeering** | Federal law orders states or state officers to act | R-CON-045, R-CON-046 |
| 12 | **Spending conditions** | Federal funds conditioned on state policy | R-CON-024 (all five Dole/NFIB factors) |
| 13 | **Presidential action** | Executive order; seizure; emergency powers; tariffs; troop deployment | R-CON-031 to R-CON-034 |
| 14 | **Appointment / removal** | Who appoints an officer; firing a commissioner; for-cause protection | R-CON-035, R-CON-036 |
| 15 | **Interbranch** | One-House veto; line-item cancellation; vague delegation; immunity claims | R-CON-038 to R-CON-043 |

## C. Federalism limits on state laws

| # | Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | **Preemption** | A federal statute or regulation in the same field | R-CON-051 |
| 17 | **Dormant Commerce Clause — discrimination** | In-state preference; local processing; out-of-state goods banned or taxed more | R-CON-047 |
| 18 | **DCC — exceptions** | State-owned business, state purchasing, public facility, congressional consent | R-CON-049, R-CON-052 |
| 19 | **DCC — Pike** | Neutral law, heavy burden on interstate trucking or sales | R-CON-048 |

## Sanity checks before writing

- Did I give **each plaintiff** its own standing analysis?
- For each right: did I state the **tier**, the **burden**, and apply **ends and means** with specific facts?
- Did I argue **both sides** where the facts cut both ways, and reach a **conclusion** for every issue?
- Did I avoid California law unless the call asked for it?
