## Scope
- **MBE** (NCBE outline [S-040]):
  - I Formation;
  - II Defenses;
  - III Content and meaning;
  - IV Performance, breach and discharge;
  - V Remedies;
  - VI Third-party rights.
  - About **half** of the questions come from I and IV; about **one-quarter** are UCC Articles 1–2 questions [S-040].
- **California essay outline** [S-088]: introductory principles (applicable law: UCC vs. common law), formation, consideration, third-party beneficiaries, assignment and delegation, performance, breach, remedies, discharge, and contract defenses. The exact numbering is unconfirmed [VERIFY V-024].

**Typical shape.**
- Governing law → formation (if disputed) → defenses → breach (party by party, in date order) → damages for each party → equitable remedies.
- Calls are often two-sided: "Did either party breach, and what can each recover?"

## Study order (no prior coursework)
1. [`primer.md`](primer.md): the map and vocabulary (1 hour).
2. **Formation and consideration** (outline I; rules 001–029), then MBE sets.
3. **Performance and breach**, including UCC Article 2 performance and risk of loss (outline IV; rules 056–088). This is the highest-yield block for both the MBE and essays.
4. **Remedies** (outline V; rules 089–109). Study together with the Remedies folder.
5. **Defenses and content** (outline II–III; rules 030–055).
6. **Third-party rights** (outline VI; rules 110–116) and the California overlay ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md)).
7. Timed essays with [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md); spaced review with [`flashcards.csv`](flashcards.csv).
