# Contracts — attack outline

> One-line rules for exam day. Full statements are in [`rule-statements.md`](rule-statements.md). Use general principles on essays unless the call asks for California law [S-077].

## 1. Governing law
- **UCC Art. 2** — sale of goods (movable things); predominant purpose for mixed deals; otherwise common law (R-CTR-001).
- **Merchant** — deals in goods of the kind or holds itself out as having expertise (R-CTR-002).

## 2. Formation
- **Mutual assent** — objective test: what a reasonable person would understand (R-CTR-003).
- **Offer** — commitment + definite terms + communication; ads are usually invitations (R-CTR-004, R-CTR-005).
- **Termination** — rejection or counteroffer, lapse, revocation (on receipt), death or incapacity (R-CTR-006, R-CTR-007).
- **Irrevocable** — option (consideration); merchant's signed firm offer (≤ 3 months); unilateral performance begun (§ 45); reliance (§ 87(2), *Drennan*) (R-CTR-008 to R-CTR-011).
- **Acceptance** — offeree, knowing of the offer, in the invited manner; mirror image at common law (R-CTR-012).
- **§ 2-207** — acceptance despite new terms unless expressly conditional; between merchants additional terms are in unless limited, material, or objected to; different terms knock out; (3) contract by conduct (R-CTR-013, R-CTR-014).
- **Mailbox** — acceptance on dispatch; everything else on receipt; option acceptance on receipt (R-CTR-015).
- **Performance / shipment** — any reasonable manner; nonconforming shipment = acceptance + breach unless an accommodation (R-CTR-016).
- **Silence** — not acceptance unless benefit taken, invited, or prior dealings (R-CTR-017).
- **Quasi-contract** — reasonable value of a benefit unjustly retained (R-CTR-018).
- **Definiteness** — reasonably certain; UCC gap fillers (price, place, time, payment); quantity required (R-CTR-019, R-CTR-020).
- **Output / requirements** — good faith, not unreasonably disproportionate (R-CTR-021).

## 3. Consideration and substitutes
- **Consideration** — bargained-for legal detriment; adequacy irrelevant (R-CTR-022).
- **Illusory** — no commitment = no consideration; good faith or notice cures it (R-CTR-023).
- **Pre-existing duty** — no consideration; exceptions: new duty, dispute, unforeseen circumstances, UCC (R-CTR-024).
- **Settlement** — forbearance on a valid or good-faith claim counts (R-CTR-025).
- **Past consideration** — none; § 86 material benefit; time-barred debts (R-CTR-026).
- **Promissory estoppel** — promise + foreseeable, actual, reasonable reliance + injustice; remedy as justice requires (R-CTR-027).
- **Modification** — common law needs consideration (§ 89 exceptions); UCC needs only good faith; CA Civ. Code § 1698 (written contracts), § 1697 (written modification of an oral contract: no consideration) (R-CTR-028, R-CTR-029).

## 4. Defenses
- **Minors** — voidable; disaffirm within a reasonable time after 18; necessaries at reasonable value (R-CTR-030).
- **Mental / intoxication** — cognitive or volitional test (volitional requires the other party's reason to know) (R-CTR-031).
- **Duress** — improper threat + no reasonable alternative = voidable (R-CTR-032).
- **Undue influence** — unfair persuasion of a dominated or trusting party (R-CTR-033).
- **Mutual mistake** — basic assumption + material effect + risk not borne (R-CTR-034).
- **Unilateral mistake** — the same, plus unconscionability or the other party's knowledge (R-CTR-035).
- **Misunderstanding** — different meanings, neither knew (or each knew): no contract; only one knew: the innocent party's meaning (R-CTR-036).
- **Misrepresentation** — fraudulent or material + inducement + justified reliance = voidable (R-CTR-037).
- **Nondisclosure** — concealment = assertion; silence only in § 161 cases (R-CTR-038).
- **Illegality** — void; *in pari delicto*; licensing statutes (R-CTR-039).
- **Unconscionability** — procedural + substantive, at formation (R-CTR-040).
- **Public policy** — exculpatory clauses; noncompetes (CA void, B&P § 16600) (R-CTR-041).
- **SOF** — MY LEGS; signed writing with essential terms (R-CTR-042, R-CTR-043).
- **One year** — impossible to perform within a year of making (R-CTR-044).
- **Surety** — collateral promise to the creditor; main purpose exception (R-CTR-045).
- **Land** — part performance (two of possession, payment, improvements) (R-CTR-046).
- **Goods ≥ $500** — quantity; merchant confirmation (10 days); special manufacture; admission; paid or received and accepted (R-CTR-047).
- **Other SOF** — full performance; § 139 estoppel; equal dignities; modifications (R-CTR-048).

## 5. Content and meaning
- **PER** — final writing bars prior or contemporaneous contradicting terms; complete writing bars additions (R-CTR-049).
- **PER exceptions** — integration, ambiguity, fraud, mistake, duress, condition precedent, reformation, separate consideration, later agreements (R-CTR-050).
- **UCC § 2-202** — course of dealing, usage and performance always in; consistent terms unless they would "certainly" have been included (R-CTR-051).
- **California PER** — *Masterson*, *PG&E* (reasonably susceptible), *Riverisland* (fraud) (R-CTR-052).
- **Interpretation** — whole, ordinary meaning, specific over general, typed over printed, against the drafter (R-CTR-053).
- **Hierarchy** — express > course of performance > course of dealing > usage of trade (R-CTR-054).
- **Omitted terms** — court supplies a reasonable term (R-CTR-055).

## 6. Warranties (goods)
- **Title** — good title, no liens (R-CTR-056).
- **Express** — affirmation, description, sample; basis of the bargain; not puffery (R-CTR-057).
- **Merchantability** — merchant seller; fit for ordinary purposes (R-CTR-058).
- **Fitness** — seller knows the purpose and the buyer's reliance (R-CTR-059).
- **Disclaimers** — "merchantability" (conspicuous if written); fitness by conspicuous writing; "as is" (R-CTR-060).
- **Notice / privity / limitations** — notice within a reasonable time; § 2-318; 4 years from tender (R-CTR-061).

## 7. Performance and breach
- **Express condition** — strict compliance; doubtful language read as a promise (R-CTR-062).
- **Constructive condition** — the work that takes time goes first; concurrent conditions need tender (R-CTR-063).
- **Excuse** — waiver, estoppel, prevention, repudiation, forfeiture (R-CTR-064).
- **Substantial performance** — price minus damages; not for goods or express conditions (R-CTR-065).
- **Material breach** — § 241 factors; suspend, then terminate; minor breach = damages only (R-CTR-066).
- **Divisible** — recover per completed part (R-CTR-067).
- **Anticipatory repudiation** — unequivocal; sue now, wait, or urge retraction; must mitigate (R-CTR-068).
- **Retraction** — until reliance, acceptance or suit (R-CTR-069).
- **Assurance** — reasonable grounds; written demand; 30 days (R-CTR-070).
- **Good faith** — in every contract; cannot override express terms (R-CTR-071).

## 8. UCC performance
- **Perfect tender** — reject all, accept all, or accept commercial units; timely, with notice (R-CTR-072).
- **Cure** — before the deadline, or later if the seller reasonably believed the tender acceptable (R-CTR-073).
- **Acceptance** — signify, fail to reject, or act as owner; then pay and give notice of breach (R-CTR-074).
- **Revocation** — substantial impairment + latent defect, or failed cure + timely notice (R-CTR-075).
- **Installment** — substantial impairment of the installment or of the whole (R-CTR-076).
- **Delivery** — shipment (carrier) vs. destination; F.O.B. (R-CTR-077).
- **Risk of loss** — agreement → breach → carrier → bailee → merchant receipt / non-merchant tender (R-CTR-078, R-CTR-079).
- **Casualty** — identified goods destroyed before risk passes: contract avoided (R-CTR-080).
- **Title** — voidable title to a good-faith purchaser; entrustment to a merchant (R-CTR-081).

## 9. Excuse and discharge
- **Impossibility** — objective; destruction, death, illegality (R-CTR-082).
- **Impracticability** — extreme, unforeseen cost or difficulty; not mere price rise (R-CTR-083).
- **Frustration** — principal purpose, known to both, substantially destroyed (R-CTR-084).
- **Rescission** — mutual agreement while both sides are executory (R-CTR-085).
- **Release** — writing; CA § 1542 unknown claims (R-CTR-086).
- **Accord and satisfaction** — executory accord suspends the duty; satisfaction discharges (R-CTR-087).
- **Substituted contract / novation** — immediate discharge; new party (R-CTR-088).

## 10. Remedies
- **Expectation** — loss in value + incidental + consequential − costs avoided (R-CTR-089).
- **Construction** — cost to complete (economic waste → diminution); builder: profit + costs − payments (R-CTR-090).
- **Employment** — salary minus comparable earnings (R-CTR-091).
- **Consequential** — foreseeable at contracting (*Hadley*) (R-CTR-092).
- **Certainty** — reasonable certainty; new business (R-CTR-093).
- **Incidental** — costs of handling the breach (R-CTR-094).
- **Mitigation** — no recovery for avoidable loss (R-CTR-095).
- **Liquidated damages** — reasonable forecast + hard to estimate; else penalty; CA § 1671 presumption (R-CTR-096).
- **§ 2-719** — exclusive remedy; failure of essential purpose; consequential exclusions (R-CTR-097).
- **No punitives or distress damages** — nominal damages; fees under § 1717 (R-CTR-098).
- **Seller (UCC)** — resale, market, lost volume, price, incidental (R-CTR-099 to R-CTR-101).
- **Buyer (UCC)** — cover or market; accepted goods: value difference (R-CTR-102, R-CTR-103).
- **Specific performance** — inadequate legal remedy, feasible, no defenses; not personal services (R-CTR-104).
- **Rescission** — ground + prompt notice + restoration (R-CTR-105).
- **Reformation** — writing ≠ agreement (mutual mistake or fraud) (R-CTR-106).
- **Reliance** — expenditures minus loss on a losing contract (R-CTR-107).
- **Restitution** — reasonable value of the benefit (R-CTR-108).
- **Breaching party** — benefit minus damages; § 2-718(2) (R-CTR-109).

## 11. Third parties and California tort overlay
- **Intended beneficiary** — may sue; incidental may not; CA *Goonewardene* (R-CTR-110).
- **Vesting** — assent, suit, reliance; promisor's contract defenses (R-CTR-111).
- **Assignment** — rights assignable unless material change, law, or contract (R-CTR-112).
- **Anti-assignment** — "the contract" = delegation only; need "void" (R-CTR-113).
- **Assignee** — subject to defenses; payment before notice discharges (R-CTR-114).
- **Gratuitous / successive** — revocable; first in time (R-CTR-115).
- **Delegation** — allowed unless personal; delegator stays liable; delegate liable on assumption (R-CTR-116).
- **CA fraud tort** — economic loss rule; *Rattagan* concealment during performance (R-CTR-117).
