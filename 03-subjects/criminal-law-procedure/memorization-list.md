## Ten one-line triggers to recite before every Criminal essay

1. Crime = act + mental state + causation + no defense.
2. Murder has four malice states; manslaughter is the fallback.
3. Felony murder: dangerous felony, independent, during, agency rule.
4. Attempt = specific intent + substantial step; impossibility is no defense.
5. Conspiracy = agreement + intent + overt act; no merger; Pinkerton.
6. Accomplice = aid + purpose; not mere presence.
7. Fourth Amendment: standing → search → warrant/exception → exclusion.
8. Gant for cars; Riley for phones; Terry for stops.
9. Miranda = custody + interrogation; Edwards stops everything.
10. Fruits: independent source, inevitable discovery, attenuation, good faith.
