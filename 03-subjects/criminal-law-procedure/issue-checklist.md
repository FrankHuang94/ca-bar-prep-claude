# Criminal Law and Procedure — issue checklist

Use this in the order printed. **Read the call first**: California Criminal calls are either "What crimes / defenses?" (Part A), "Should the evidence / statement be suppressed?" (Part B), or both. Answer the call, item by item or defendant by defendant (grader-expectations §5).

## Part A — Crimes and defenses (per defendant)

### A0. Threshold
- [ ] **Which defendants?** One heading per defendant. **Which law?** General principles (common law + MPC) unless the call says California [S-077].
- [ ] **Act / omission** (R-CRM-002, R-CRM-003). Triggers: sleepwalking, seizure, a failure to help a child, parent, patient, someone the defendant endangered.
- [ ] **Mental state** for each element (R-CRM-004, R-CRM-005). Triggers: "she thought," "he did not know," a quoted statute.

### A1. Inchoate crimes and parties (analyze early — they set up the rest)
- [ ] **Solicitation** — asking someone to commit a crime ("offered $2,000 to find a hit man") (R-CRM-064).
- [ ] **Conspiracy** — agreement (express or by concert), intent, overt act; bilateral vs. unilateral if a party is an undercover agent or informant; Wharton's rule; protected class (R-CRM-065–R-CRM-068).
- [ ] **Pinkerton** — each crime by a co-conspirator: in furtherance + foreseeable (R-CRM-067).
- [ ] **Withdrawal** — "told one partner he quit"; "called the police" (R-CRM-068, R-CRM-071).
- [ ] **Accomplice** — lookout, driver, lending tools, encouraging words; purpose vs. knowledge; mere presence; natural and probable consequences (R-CRM-069, R-CRM-070).
- [ ] **Accessory after the fact** — hiding the felon or evidence afterward (R-CRM-071).

### A2. Homicide (for each death)
- [ ] **Causation** — actual and proximate; intervening medical negligence, victim's flight, police shot (R-CRM-012, R-CRM-013).
- [ ] **Murder** — each malice state that fits: intent to kill (deadly weapon at vital area); intent to cause serious bodily injury; depraved heart (shooting into a crowd/house/car); felony murder (R-CRM-031–R-CRM-036).
- [ ] **Felony murder** — qualifying felony; merger; during the felony (flight; temporary safety); who killed (agency vs. proximate cause); co-felon death (R-CRM-039–R-CRM-041). California § 189(e) only if asked (R-CRM-043).
- [ ] **First degree** — premeditation (planning, motive, manner); means (poison, lying in wait); enumerated felony (R-CRM-033).
- [ ] **Voluntary manslaughter** — provocation (adultery, battery, mutual combat; cooling time); imperfect self-defense (R-CRM-034, R-CRM-023).
- [ ] **Involuntary manslaughter** — criminal negligence; misdemeanor manslaughter (R-CRM-037, R-CRM-042).

### A3. Other crimes (by trigger facts)
- [ ] Taking property → **larceny / embezzlement / false pretenses / larceny by trick** (R-CRM-044–R-CRM-047).
- [ ] Buying "hot" goods; a sting → **receiving** and **attempted receiving** (R-CRM-048, R-CRM-062).
- [ ] Force, gun (even fake), threat while taking → **robbery**; force after the taking (R-CRM-049).
- [ ] Entering a building → **burglary** (common-law elements; intent at entry) (R-CRM-050, R-CRM-051).
- [ ] Threat, swing, punch, weapon → **assault / battery / aggravated** (R-CRM-052, R-CRM-053).
- [ ] Fire, smoke, charring → **arson**; reckless burning (R-CRM-058).
- [ ] Moving or confining a person → **kidnapping / false imprisonment**; incidental movement (R-CRM-056, R-CRM-057).
- [ ] Sexual conduct; minor → **rape / statutory rape** (R-CRM-054, R-CRM-055).
- [ ] Drugs or guns found → **possession** (knowledge; constructive; joint) (R-CRM-059).
- [ ] **Attempt** of any of the above — specific intent + substantial step / dangerous proximity; impossibility; abandonment (R-CRM-060–R-CRM-063).

### A4. Defenses (for each charge)
- [ ] **Self-defense / defense of others / property** — imminence, proportionality, retreat, aggressor (R-CRM-020–R-CRM-025).
- [ ] **Necessity** — cold, storm, fire, emergency; greater harm; no alternative (R-CRM-027).
- [ ] **Duress** — a human threat; not for murder (R-CRM-028).
- [ ] **Entrapment** — police or informant inducement; predisposition (subjective) vs. normally law-abiding person (objective) (R-CRM-029).
- [ ] **Intoxication** — voluntary (specific intent only) vs. involuntary (R-CRM-018).
- [ ] **Insanity / diminished capacity** — mental illness facts (R-CRM-015–R-CRM-017).
- [ ] **Mistake of fact / law; claim of right** (R-CRM-010, R-CRM-011).
- [ ] **Consent; infancy; law enforcement** (R-CRM-030, R-CRM-019, R-CRM-026).
- [ ] **Merger / double jeopardy** if multiple convictions are proposed (R-CRM-068, R-CRM-116).

## Part B — Procedure (per item of evidence or per statement)

### B1. Physical evidence (Fourth Amendment)
- [ ] **Government action** (private searcher? school official?) (R-CRM-072).
- [ ] **Standing** — whose place or item? Owner, overnight guest, passenger, visitor (R-CRM-075).
- [ ] **Search?** — REP or trespass; technology; open fields, trash, public view, third-party records; dog sniff (R-CRM-073, R-CRM-074, R-CRM-089).
- [ ] **Seizure of the person?** — consensual encounter vs. stop vs. arrest (R-CRM-076).
- [ ] **Stop and frisk** — reasonable suspicion (tip? flight? nervousness?); armed and dangerous; plain feel (R-CRM-077, R-CRM-078).
- [ ] **Traffic stop** — basis; prolonged for a dog sniff? (R-CRM-079).
- [ ] **Arrest** — probable cause; in the home without a warrant? statute later held invalid (DeFillippo)? (R-CRM-080).
- [ ] **Warrant** — probable cause (informant: Gates), particularity, Franks, execution, scope (R-CRM-082).
- [ ] **Exceptions** — incident to arrest (Gant; phone → Riley); automobile; plain view; consent (authority, scope); exigency/emergency aid; inventory; special needs (R-CRM-083–R-CRM-088).
- [ ] **Exclusion** — fruits; independent source; inevitable discovery; attenuation; good faith; impeachment; California Truth-in-Evidence (R-CRM-090–R-CRM-092).

### B2. Statements
- [ ] **Voluntariness** (threats, promises, length) (R-CRM-093).
- [ ] **Miranda** — custody → interrogation → warnings → waiver → invocation (silence: Mosley; counsel: Edwards) → public safety → two-step (Seibert) → fruits and impeachment (R-CRM-094–R-CRM-100).
- [ ] **Sixth Amendment** — charged? deliberate elicitation? same offense? informant? (R-CRM-102).
- [ ] **Fruit of an illegal arrest or search** (R-CRM-091).

### B3. Identification
- [ ] Post-charge live lineup without counsel? (R-CRM-106).
- [ ] Suggestive police procedure + Biggers reliability factors; in-court identification (R-CRM-107).

### B4. Trial, plea, sentence (when the call asks "Did the court err?")
- [ ] Counsel: appointment, self-representation, counsel of choice, ineffective assistance, conflicts (R-CRM-103–R-CRM-105).
- [ ] Jury, Batson, Apprendi, confrontation, Brady, competency, public trial (R-CRM-110–R-CRM-114).
- [ ] Guilty plea record; bargain; withdrawal (R-CRM-115).
- [ ] Double jeopardy; Eighth Amendment; burden and presumptions; harmless error (R-CRM-116–R-CRM-120).
