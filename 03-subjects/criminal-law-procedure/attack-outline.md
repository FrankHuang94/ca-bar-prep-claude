# Criminal Law and Procedure — attack outline

One line per issue. Full rules: [rule-statements.md](rule-statements.md). Essays: common law + MPC for crimes; U.S. Constitution for procedure.

## Crimes

**Building blocks**
- **Act** — voluntary act or omission with a legal duty (statute, contract, relationship, assumption, creation of peril) (R-CRM-002, 003).
- **Mental state** — specific intent / general intent / malice / strict liability; MPC purpose > knowledge > recklessness > negligence; default recklessness (R-CRM-004, 005).
- **Causation** — but-for + proximate; foreseeable intervening causes do not break the chain (R-CRM-012, 013).
- **Concurrence** — intent must actuate the act (R-CRM-009).

**Homicide**
- **Murder** — unlawful killing with malice: intent to kill, intent to cause SBI, depraved heart, felony murder (R-CRM-031).
- **First degree** — premeditated and deliberate (planning, motive, manner), specified means, enumerated felony (R-CRM-033).
- **Felony murder** — inherently dangerous felony; independent (no merger); during the felony until temporary safety; agency rule — no liability for killings by non-felons (R-CRM-039–041).
- **Voluntary manslaughter** — heat of passion from adequate provocation, no cooling time; or imperfect self-defense (R-CRM-034, 023).
- **Involuntary manslaughter** — criminal negligence; unlawful-act (misdemeanor) killing (R-CRM-037, 042).
- **MPC** — murder: purposely, knowingly, extreme-indifference recklessness; manslaughter: reckless or EMED; negligent homicide (R-CRM-038).

**Property and person**
- **Larceny** — trespassory taking and carrying away of another's property with intent to permanently deprive at the time of taking (R-CRM-044).
- **Embezzlement** — fraudulent conversion by one in lawful possession (R-CRM-045).
- **False pretenses / larceny by trick** — fraud obtaining title / possession (R-CRM-046).
- **Receiving** — receiving stolen property knowing it is stolen, intending to deprive; police-recovered goods → attempt (R-CRM-048).
- **Robbery** — larceny from person or presence by force or threat of immediate harm (R-CRM-049).
- **Burglary** — breaking and entering dwelling of another at night with intent to commit felony inside (modern: any structure, any time) (R-CRM-050, 051).
- **Assault** — attempted battery or intentional creation of apprehension; **battery** — unlawful force causing injury or offensive touching (R-CRM-052, 053).
- **Rape** — intercourse without consent by force/threat or with incapacitated victim; **statutory rape** — strict liability as to age (R-CRM-054, 055).
- **Kidnapping** — unlawful confinement + movement or concealment; incidental movement excluded (R-CRM-056, 057).
- **Arson** — malicious burning (charring) of dwelling of another (R-CRM-058).
- **Possession** — knowing actual or constructive possession (R-CRM-059).

**Inchoate and parties**
- **Attempt** — specific intent + substantial step (MPC) / dangerous proximity; factual impossibility no defense; abandonment only under MPC (voluntary, complete) (R-CRM-060–063).
- **Solicitation** — asking another to commit a crime with intent it be committed; merges (R-CRM-064).
- **Conspiracy** — agreement + intent + overt act; bilateral vs. unilateral; no merger; Pinkerton; withdrawal communicated to all (R-CRM-065–068).
- **Accomplice** — aid or encouragement with purpose that the crime be committed; liable for foreseeable crimes; withdrawal by neutralizing aid (R-CRM-069–071).

**Defenses**
- **Self-defense** — reasonable belief in imminent unlawful force; deadly force only vs. death/SBI; no retreat (majority) except minority/MPC; aggressor must withdraw (R-CRM-020–022).
- **Defense of others / property** — reasonable belief; never deadly force for property alone (R-CRM-024, 025).
- **Necessity** — greater harm avoided, no alternative, not at fault; not for intentional killing (R-CRM-027).
- **Duress** — threat of imminent death/SBI, reasonable person would yield; not for murder (R-CRM-028).
- **Entrapment** — government inducement + no predisposition (majority); objective test (MPC, California) (R-CRM-029).
- **Insanity** — M'Naghten (nature/quality or wrong); irresistible impulse; MPC substantial capacity; federal clear-and-convincing (R-CRM-015).
- **Intoxication** — voluntary: specific intent only; involuntary: all crimes if incapacitating (R-CRM-018).
- **Mistake of fact** — honest for specific intent; reasonable for general intent; none for strict liability (R-CRM-010).
- **Mistake of law** — no defense except official reliance or negated element (R-CRM-011).

## Procedure

**Fourth Amendment (item by item)**
- **Gov't action → standing** (own REP: owner, overnight guest, not short-term visitor) (R-CRM-072, 075).
- **Search** — Katz REP or Jones trespass; not open fields, trash, public view, third-party records (R-CRM-073, 074).
- **Seizure** — reasonable person not free to leave; submission or physical force (R-CRM-076).
- **Terry** — reasonable suspicion for stop; armed-and-dangerous for frisk; plain feel (R-CRM-077, 078).
- **Arrest** — probable cause; warrant to enter home (Payton); good-faith reliance on a later-invalidated law keeps the arrest valid (DeFillippo) (R-CRM-080).
- **Warrant** — neutral magistrate, probable cause (Gates), particularity; Franks (R-CRM-082).
- **SIA** — person and wingspan; car (arrestee a recent occupant, Thornton) only if unsecured/within reach or evidence of arrest offense (Gant); phone needs warrant (Riley) (R-CRM-083).
- **Automobile** — probable cause → whole car and containers (R-CRM-084).
- **Plain view** — lawful vantage, lawful access, immediately apparent (R-CRM-085).
- **Consent** — voluntary; actual or apparent authority; present objector controls (R-CRM-086).
- **Exigency** — hot pursuit, evidence destruction, emergency aid (objectively reasonable belief) (R-CRM-087).
- **Special needs** — inventory, checkpoints, border, schools, parolees (R-CRM-088).
- **Exclusion** — fruits excluded unless independent source, inevitable discovery, attenuation; good faith; impeachment OK; California follows federal rule (R-CRM-090–092).

**Statements**
- **Voluntariness** — police coercion overbearing the will (R-CRM-093).
- **Miranda** — custodial interrogation requires warnings and a knowing, voluntary waiver (R-CRM-094–096).
- **Invocation** — silence: unambiguous, scrupulously honored (Mosley); counsel: unambiguous, all questioning stops unless suspect reinitiates (Edwards); 14-day break (R-CRM-097, 098).
- **Public safety** — Quarles (R-CRM-099).
- **Fruits** — physical fruits admissible (Patane, plurality); Elstad vs. Seibert; impeachment OK (Harris) (R-CRM-100).
- **Sixth Amendment** — after charges, no deliberate elicitation on the charged offense (R-CRM-102).

**Identification, counsel, trial**
- **Lineups** — counsel at post-charge live lineups; due process: suggestive + unreliable (Biggers factors) (R-CRM-106, 107).
- **Counsel** — felony or actual imprisonment; Faretta; Strickland deficient + prejudice (R-CRM-103–105).
- **Jury** — > 6 months; unanimous; Batson; Apprendi (R-CRM-110, 111).
- **Confrontation** — testimonial hearsay needs unavailability + prior cross (R-CRM-112).
- **Brady** — favorable, material evidence must be disclosed (R-CRM-113).
- **Plea** — knowing, intelligent, voluntary on the record; bargain enforceable (R-CRM-115).
- **Double jeopardy** — attaches when jury sworn; Blockburger; lesser-included; separate sovereigns; mistrial rules (R-CRM-116, 117).
- **Eighth Amendment** — gross disproportionality; death-penalty limits; no status crimes (R-CRM-118).
- **Burden** — every element beyond reasonable doubt; no burden-shifting presumptions (R-CRM-119).
- **Appeal** — harmless beyond reasonable doubt; structural errors reverse automatically (R-CRM-120).
