# Civil Procedure — issue checklist

**Use.** Read the call first; organize under the call's headings. Then run this list **in order** against the facts — threshold issues come first because a court without jurisdiction never reaches the rest. Rule IDs point to [rule-statements.md](rule-statements.md).

## 0. Frame the answer
- [ ] **Which court?** Federal district court → FRCP and Title 28; California superior court → CCP. *Trigger:* "filed in the U.S. District Court for…", "superior court", "under California law". (R-CIV-103; [ca-distinctions.md](ca-distinctions.md))
- [ ] **Which motion or ruling is being reviewed?** Name it ("D's Rule 12(b)(2) motion"). *Trigger:* "How should the court rule on…", "Did the court properly…".

## 1. Subject-matter jurisdiction (federal court) — always first
- [ ] **Federal question?** Is the claim created by federal law, on the face of the well-pleaded complaint? Embedded federal issue (Grable)? *Trigger:* federal statute named; federal issue appears only as a defense. (R-CIV-002, R-CIV-003)
- [ ] **Diversity?** List each party's citizenship (individual domicile; corporation incorporation + nerve center; LLC/partnership members; aliens; estates). Complete diversity at filing. *Trigger:* recent moves, dual-State corporations, LLCs, foreign parties, "plans to return". (R-CIV-004 to R-CIV-007)
- [ ] **Amount** > $75,000; aggregation; legal certainty. *Trigger:* several plaintiffs or claims, damages near the line. (R-CIV-008)
- [ ] **Supplemental** for each remaining claim: common nucleus; § 1367(b) in diversity cases; § 1367(c) discretion. *Trigger:* state claim alongside federal claim; impleaded or intervening parties. (R-CIV-010 to R-CIV-012)
- [ ] **Removal/remand**: original jurisdiction; defendant removed; forum-defendant rule; 30 days; unanimity; one-year cap; remand timing; post-removal amendments (Royal Canin). *Trigger:* case "began in state court". (R-CIV-013 to R-CIV-016)

## 2. Personal jurisdiction — each defendant separately
- [ ] **Statute:** long-arm (CCP § 410.10 in California) via Rule 4(k)(1)(A); bulge; 4(k)(2). (R-CIV-017, R-CIV-027)
- [ ] **Traditional bases:** in-state service, domicile, consent (forum clause, registration), waiver by appearance. *Trigger:* defendant "visited", "was served at the airport", contract clause. (R-CIV-018, R-CIV-019)
- [ ] **General jurisdiction:** at home? *Trigger:* large forum sales by an out-of-state company. (R-CIV-020)
- [ ] **Specific jurisdiction:** purposeful availment (stream of commerce; effects test; internet) → relatedness (Ford/BMS) → fairness factors. *Trigger:* product made elsewhere, sold through distributors; online sales; defamatory article. (R-CIV-021 to R-CIV-025)
- [ ] **In rem / quasi in rem**. *Trigger:* attachment of forum property. (R-CIV-026)

## 3. Service and notice
- [ ] Proper method for the defendant type (individual, entity, foreign); proper server; 90 days; waiver request; California methods if applicable. *Trigger:* papers left with a neighbour, mailed, handed to a receptionist. (R-CIV-028 to R-CIV-030, R-CIV-104)

## 4. Venue, transfer and forum non conveniens
- [ ] § 1391(b)(1)/(2)/(3) with residence rules; removal venue. *Trigger:* events in one district, defendants in another. (R-CIV-031, R-CIV-032)
- [ ] Transfer: § 1404(a) (proper venue) vs. § 1406(a) (improper venue); forum-selection clause (Atlantic Marine); law applied after transfer. (R-CIV-033 to R-CIV-035)
- [ ] FNC for a foreign forum (Piper; CCP § 410.30 in California). (R-CIV-036, R-CIV-105)

## 5. Governing law (Erie)
- [ ] State-law claim in federal court? Federal Rule or statute on point (Hanna/Shady Grove/Berk)? Otherwise twin aims and Byrd. Choice of law (Klaxon). *Trigger:* "Under State X law…", state procedural requirement (affidavit, limitations, damages cap). (R-CIV-037 to R-CIV-041)

## 6. Pleadings and pretrial motions on the pleadings
- [ ] Complaint: plausibility (8(a)); fraud particularity (9(b)); California fact pleading and demurrers. *Trigger:* conclusory allegations, fraud claim. (R-CIV-044, R-CIV-045, R-CIV-106)
- [ ] Rule 12 defenses, consolidation and waiver. *Trigger:* sequence of motions. (R-CIV-047, R-CIV-084)
- [ ] Amendment and relation back; Doe defendants in California. *Trigger:* limitations period ran; wrong defendant named. (R-CIV-048, R-CIV-049, R-CIV-107)
- [ ] Rule 11 / CCP § 128.7. *Trigger:* no investigation, harassing filing, safe-harbor timing. (R-CIV-050, R-CIV-051, R-CIV-119)
- [ ] Anti-SLAPP (California claim arising from speech/petition). *Trigger:* defamation over statements to government or on public issues. (R-CIV-108)

## 7. Multiple parties and claims
- [ ] Counterclaims (compulsory?), crossclaims, Rule 18; California cross-complaints. (R-CIV-052, R-CIV-109)
- [ ] Party joinder: Rule 20, Rule 19 (required? feasible? 19(b)), impleader, intervention, interpleader. *Trigger:* "moved to dismiss for failure to join". (R-CIV-053 to R-CIV-058)
- [ ] Class action: 23(a), 23(b), notice, CAFA. *Trigger:* "on behalf of all purchasers". (R-CIV-059 to R-CIV-061, R-CIV-009)
- [ ] For every added claim or party: SMJ, PJ, venue.

## 8. Provisional relief
- [ ] TRO / preliminary injunction (Winter; Rule 65). *Trigger:* urgent request, reinstatement, asset freeze. (R-CIV-042, R-CIV-043)

## 9. Discovery — request by request
- [ ] Prerequisites: meet and confer; timing; disclosure duties. (R-CIV-062, R-CIV-063, R-CIV-072)
- [ ] Scope: relevance + proportionality (CA: subject matter). (R-CIV-064, R-CIV-110)
- [ ] Protections: privilege and log; work product (CA: attorney work product, Coito); experts (testifying vs. consulting). (R-CIV-065 to R-CIV-067, R-CIV-112)
- [ ] Device limits: depositions, interrogatories, RFAs, Rule 35 exams (CA: demand vs. leave). (R-CIV-068 to R-CIV-070, R-CIV-110)
- [ ] ESI and spoliation (37(e)). (R-CIV-071)
- [ ] Sanctions and expenses (37; CA $1,000 mandatory sanction). (R-CIV-073, R-CIV-111)

## 10. Disposition without trial
- [ ] Voluntary/involuntary dismissal; default. (R-CIV-074 to R-CIV-076)
- [ ] Summary judgment (Rule 56; CA § 437c). *Trigger:* "after discovery, D moved for summary judgment". (R-CIV-085, R-CIV-086, R-CIV-113)

## 11. Trial
- [ ] Jury right and demand (legal vs. equitable; Beacon; CA 1850 test). (R-CIV-078 to R-CIV-080, R-CIV-116)
- [ ] Jury selection: for-cause challenges, peremptories, Batson. *Trigger:* juror's relationship or statements. (R-CIV-082)
- [ ] Instructions: request and objection. (R-CIV-083)
- [ ] JMOL / directed verdict. (R-CIV-087)

## 12. Post-trial
- [ ] Renewed JMOL (needs prior 50(a)); new trial (grounds, 28 days, remittitur); Rule 60(b); juror non-disclosure (McDonough); CA §§ 629, 657, 659–660. *Trigger:* evidence discovered after trial; concealed documents; excessive award. (R-CIV-088 to R-CIV-092, R-CIV-117)
- [ ] Verdict form problems (Rule 49). (R-CIV-091)

## 13. Effect of judgment
- [ ] Claim preclusion (same claim — transactional vs. primary rights; same parties; final on merits). (R-CIV-094, R-CIV-114)
- [ ] Issue preclusion; mutuality; non-parties; which system's law applies. (R-CIV-095 to R-CIV-098, R-CIV-115)

## 14. Appeal
- [ ] Final judgment? 54(b)? Interlocutory route (§ 1292(a)(1), (b), 23(f), collateral order, mandamus)? Timeliness (30 days; tolling motions)? Standard of review? California § 904.1 and writs. (R-CIV-099 to R-CIV-102, R-CIV-118)

## 15. Finish
- [ ] Conclusion for **every** issue; a California comparison line where invited; overall conclusion on each motion in the call.
