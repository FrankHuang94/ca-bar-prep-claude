# Civil Procedure on the MBE

## 1. NCBE's assumptions
- Questions assume the **Federal Rules of Civil Procedure as currently in effect** and the sections of **Title 28** on jurisdiction, venue and transfer [S-040].
- **Approximately two-thirds** of scored questions come from categories **I** (jurisdiction and venue), **III** (pretrial procedures) and **V** (motions); about one-third from **II, IV, VI and VII** [S-040]. With 25 scored questions, that is roughly 17 and 8 (`../../05-mbe/subject-weighting.md`).
- "Currently in effect" for July 2027 means the FRCP as amended through Dec. 1, 2025 (Rule 16.1; privilege-log amendments to 16(b) and 26(f)) [S-107]. No civil rules changed on Dec. 1, 2026 [S-103]. Proposed 2027 amendments (7.1, 26, 41, 45, 81) are not testable [S-110].
- Unless a question says otherwise, assume the case is in **federal district court**, the forum State's long-arm statute reaches the constitutional limit, and state substantive law is unremarkable.

## 2. Category-by-category: what the MBE favours

### I. Jurisdiction and venue (heaviest category)
- **Diversity:** complete diversity; corporate dual citizenship (incorporation + nerve center); LLCs and partnerships take **every member's** citizenship; domicile vs. residence; "exceeds $75,000" (exactly $75,000 fails); aggregation (one plaintiff v. one defendant only); time-of-filing rule. (R-CIV-004 to R-CIV-008)
- **Federal question:** well-pleaded complaint — a federal **defense** never counts. (R-CIV-002)
- **Supplemental:** § 1367(b) — plaintiff's claim against an impleaded third-party defendant in a diversity case fails (Kroger); defendant's impleader claim is fine; Exxon Mobil co-plaintiffs. (R-CIV-010, R-CIV-011)
- **Removal:** only defendants; forum-defendant rule (diversity only, "properly joined and served"); 30 days from formal service; all served defendants consent; one-year limit for diversity; remand for procedural defects within 30 days. (R-CIV-013 to R-CIV-015)
- **PJ:** general jurisdiction only where "at home"; specific jurisdiction three steps; stream of commerce split; tag jurisdiction valid; consent and waiver; the 100-mile bulge for Rule 14 and 19 parties. (R-CIV-017 to R-CIV-027)
- **Service:** abode service with a person of suitable age and discretion who **resides** there; waiver timing (30 days to return, 60 to answer); 90-day limit. (R-CIV-029, R-CIV-030)
- **Venue:** § 1391(b)(1)–(3); entity residence = where subject to PJ; § 1404 vs. § 1406; Van Dusen/Ferens; Atlantic Marine; FNC for foreign forums. (R-CIV-031 to R-CIV-036)

### II. Law applied by federal courts
- Sorting rules into **state** (limitations, tolling, choice of law, burden of proof, elements) and **federal** (service, pleading, joinder, class actions, jury right). (R-CIV-037 to R-CIV-040)
- Hanna: a valid Federal Rule on point wins. Byrd: judge/jury allocation is federal. Klaxon: forum State's choice-of-law rules. Berk v. Choy (2026): Rule 8 displaces state affidavit-of-merit rules [VERIFY V-034].
- Federal common law enclaves (U.S. commercial paper, contractor defense). (R-CIV-041)

### III. Pretrial procedures (heaviest with I and V)
- **TRO/PI:** 14-day limit; notice and attorney certification; Winter factors; who is bound. (R-CIV-042, R-CIV-043)
- **Pleadings:** plausibility; Rule 9(b); answer within 21 days; failure to deny = admission; amendment as of course within 21 days; relation back for new defendants (notice within 90 days + knew of mistake). (R-CIV-044 to R-CIV-049)
- **Rule 12 waiver:** the single most reliable MBE point. (R-CIV-047)
- **Rule 11:** 21-day safe harbor; no monetary sanction on represented party for frivolous legal contentions; does not apply to discovery. (R-CIV-050, R-CIV-051)
- **Joinder:** compulsory counterclaim (same transaction) barred if omitted; crossclaims never compulsory; impleader only for derivative liability; Rule 19 joint tortfeasors not required; intervention as of right elements; interpleader jurisdiction. (R-CIV-052 to R-CIV-058)
- **Class actions:** 23(a) four prerequisites; (b)(3) notice and opt-out; 23(f) 14 days; CAFA. (R-CIV-059 to R-CIV-061, R-CIV-009)
- **Discovery:** proportionality; work product (substantial need/undue hardship); consulting experts (exceptional circumstances); Rule 35 requires motion and good cause; deposition limits and use at trial (100 miles); deemed admissions; 37(e) intent to deprive. (R-CIV-062 to R-CIV-073)
- **Dismissals and defaults:** notice dismissal before answer or SJ motion; two-dismissal rule; 41(b) dismissal on the merits; default judgment cannot exceed the demand. (R-CIV-074 to R-CIV-076)
- **Pretrial orders:** modified only to prevent manifest injustice. (R-CIV-077)

### IV. Jury trials
- Legal vs. equitable classification; Beacon Theatres sequencing; demand within 14 days after the last pleading; 6–12 jurors, unanimous unless stipulated; three peremptories; Batson applies in civil cases; Rule 51 objection preservation. (R-CIV-078 to R-CIV-083)

### V. Motions (heaviest with I and III)
- 12(b)(6) plausibility and 12(d) conversion; summary judgment standard and Celotex burden; JMOL standard; renewed JMOL requires a pre-verdict motion and comes within 28 days; new trial within 28 days, remittitur yes, additur no; Rule 60(b) categories and the one-year limit for (1)–(3). (R-CIV-084 to R-CIV-090)

### VI. Verdicts and judgments
- Rule 49 inconsistent answers; Rule 52 clear-error review; McDonough juror non-disclosure; claim preclusion (transactional test; on the merits); issue preclusion (actually litigated and essential); Parklane offensive non-mutual estoppel; Taylor v. Sturgell non-party preclusion; § 1738 and Semtek. (R-CIV-091 to R-CIV-098)

### VII. Appealability and review
- Final judgment rule; Rule 54(b); § 1292(a)(1) injunctions; § 1292(b) certification (10 days); 23(f); collateral order doctrine (Mohawk: privilege rulings not appealable; GEO Group: contractor immunity denials not appealable [VERIFY V-150]); standards of review. (R-CIV-099 to R-CIV-102)

## 3. Recurring MBE fact patterns and the answer they want
| Fact pattern | Answer |
|---|---|
| Corporation incorporated in A, headquarters in B, most employees in C | Citizen of A and B only |
| LLC with a member from the defendant's State | No diversity |
| Plaintiff claims exactly $75,000 | No diversity jurisdiction |
| Two plaintiffs each with $40,000 claims against one defendant | No aggregation; dismiss unless another basis |
| Defendant files federal defense; plaintiff's claim is state law | No federal-question jurisdiction |
| Diversity case; defendant impleads non-diverse third party; plaintiff asserts claim against third party | Plaintiff's claim lacks SMJ (§ 1367(b)) |
| Forum-State defendant removes a diversity case | Improper; but plaintiff must move to remand within 30 days |
| Defendant served Jan. 1; removes Feb. 15 | Untimely (30 days) |
| Defendant moves to dismiss for improper venue, later for lack of PJ | PJ objection waived |
| Corporation's continuous sales in forum, claim arises elsewhere | No general jurisdiction (not at home) |
| Individual served while briefly in the forum | Jurisdiction (tag; Burnham) |
| Product reached forum through distributor; no targeting | Analyze both stream-of-commerce views; usually no jurisdiction if isolated |
| Papers left with a doorman, or with a relative who does not live there | Not valid abode service under 4(e)(2)(B) |
| Transfer under § 1404(a) after plaintiff chose a proper forum | Transferee applies transferor's choice-of-law rules |
| Valid forum-selection clause | Transfer nearly automatic; no deference to plaintiff's choice |
| Rule 11 motion filed without 21-day notice | Denied |
| Party fails to serve answer to request for admission within 30 days | Admitted; must move to withdraw |
| Plaintiff requests mental exam of defendant without court order | Improper; Rule 35 requires a motion and good cause |
| Expert hired to advise, not to testify | Facts and opinions discoverable only on exceptional circumstances |
| ESI deleted negligently | Curative measures only; no adverse inference without intent to deprive |
| Jury demand served 20 days after the answer | Untimely; court may still allow under Rule 39(b) |
| Claim for damages and injunction share a fact issue | Jury decides the common issue first |
| No Rule 50(a) motion; party files Rule 50(b) after verdict | Denied |
| Trial judge raises an inadequate award | Additur not permitted in federal court |
| Rule 60(b)(1) motion 14 months after judgment | Untimely |
| Second suit on a different theory from the same accident | Claim preclusion bars it |
| New plaintiff uses a judgment against a defendant who lost | Offensive non-mutual estoppel — Parklane factors |
| Order granting preliminary injunction | Immediately appealable (§ 1292(a)(1)) |
| Discovery order compelling privileged documents | Not appealable as collateral order (Mohawk) |

## 5. Method tips for CIV questions
- **Identify the Rule first.** Many wrong answers state a correct rule from the wrong context (e.g., a state-court standard, the pre-2015 discovery scope, or a criminal-procedure analogue).
- **Count days precisely.** Look for dates in the stem; a one-day difference often decides the item.
- **Ask "who is objecting and when?"** Waiver turns on the order of filings.
- **For jurisdiction, test every party and every claim.** One non-diverse pair or one unsupported claim changes the answer.
- **Distinguish "may" from "must".** Rule 19 joinder, Rule 37(a)(5) expenses and remand for lack of SMJ are mandatory; § 1367(c), Rule 39(b) and offensive non-mutual estoppel are discretionary.
