# Civil Procedure — attack outline

> One line per rule. Federal first; **CA:** marks the California variation. Full text: [rule-statements.md](rule-statements.md).

## A. Subject-matter jurisdiction
- **Limited jurisdiction** — Art. III + statute; never waived; raise any time (12(h)(3)). R-CIV-001
- **Federal question** — claim arises under federal law on the face of the well-pleaded complaint; defenses don't count (§ 1331; Mottley). R-CIV-002
- **Embedded federal issue** — necessarily raised, disputed, substantial, consistent with federal–state balance (Grable; Gunn). R-CIV-003
- **Diversity** — complete diversity at filing + more than $75,000 (§ 1332(a); Strawbridge). R-CIV-004
- **Citizenship** — individuals: domicile; corporations: every incorporation State + nerve center (Hertz); unincorporated entities: every member (Carden); representatives: the represented. R-CIV-005–007
- **Amount** — good faith unless legal certainty; aggregate only one P v. one D. R-CIV-008
- **CAFA** — 100+ members, > $5M, minimal diversity; exceptions. R-CIV-009
- **Supplemental** — common nucleus (§ 1367(a); Gibbs); no plaintiff claims against Rule 14/19/20/24 parties in diversity cases (§ 1367(b); Kroger); Exxon Mobil co-plaintiffs; discretion (§ 1367(c)); Royal Canin remand. R-CIV-010–012
- **Removal** — defendants only; original jurisdiction; forum-defendant rule (diversity); 30 days from service; unanimity; one-year cap for later-removable diversity cases; district embracing state court (§§ 1441, 1446). R-CIV-013–014
- **Remand** — procedural defects within 30 days; SMJ any time; not appealable; Hain Celestial. R-CIV-015
- **Federal officer** — "relating to" = plausible close relationship (Chevron v. Plaquemines). R-CIV-016
- **CA:** superior court has general jurisdiction; limited (≤ $35,000) vs. unlimited. R-CIV-103

## B. Personal jurisdiction
- **Framework** — statute (4(k)(1)(A) + long-arm; CA § 410.10 = constitutional limit) + due process. R-CIV-017
- **Tag / domicile / consent / waiver** — Burnham (unanimous result, no majority rationale); Milliken; forum clauses; 12(h)(1). R-CIV-018
- **Registration consent** — Mallory (express statute). R-CIV-019
- **General** — "at home": domicile; incorporation + PPB (Daimler). R-CIV-020
- **Specific** — purposeful availment (WWVW; Burger King) → arise out of or relate to (Ford; BMS) → fairness factors. R-CIV-021–023
- **Stream of commerce** — Asahi split; Nicastro: one sale not enough. R-CIV-024
- **Effects** — express aiming at forum (Calder); plaintiff not the only link (Walden). R-CIV-025
- **In rem / quasi in rem** — Shaffer: minimum contacts required. R-CIV-026
- **Federal reach** — 100-mile bulge (Rule 14/19 parties); 4(k)(2). R-CIV-027
- **CA:** motion to quash (§ 418.10); general appearance waives. R-CIV-104

## C. Notice and service
- **Notice** — reasonably calculated to apprise (Mullane). R-CIV-028
- **Service** — non-party 18+; personal, abode with resident of suitable age and discretion, agent, state-law method; entities via officer/managing agent; 90 days (4(m)). R-CIV-029
- **Waiver** — 30 days to return; answer 60 days from request; costs if refused. R-CIV-030
- **CA:** substituted service after diligence + mailing; notice and acknowledgment; publication by order; 30 days to respond. R-CIV-104

## D. Venue, transfer, FNC
- **Venue** — any district where a defendant resides if all reside in the State; substantial events/property; fallback (§ 1391(b)). R-CIV-031
- **Residence** — individuals: domicile; entities: where subject to PJ for the action; non-U.S. residents: anywhere. R-CIV-032
- **§ 1404(a)** — proper venue; convenience + justice; transferor's law follows (Van Dusen; Ferens). R-CIV-033
- **§ 1406(a)** — improper venue; dismiss or transfer (even without PJ). R-CIV-034
- **Forum clause** — enforce absent extraordinary circumstances; public factors only (Atlantic Marine). R-CIV-035
- **FNC** — adequate alternative forum + private/public factors; less favourable law not decisive (Piper). R-CIV-036
- **CA:** county venue (§§ 392, 395, 395.5); § 397 transfer; § 410.30 FNC, stay for residents (Stangvik). R-CIV-105

## E. Erie and federal common law
- **Erie** — state substantive law, federal procedure (RDA § 1652). R-CIV-037
- **Rule on point** — apply if sufficiently broad and really regulates procedure (Hanna; Shady Grove; Berk v. Choy). R-CIV-038
- **No rule** — twin aims (forum shopping, inequitable administration); Byrd federal interests; Gasperini. R-CIV-039
- **Standard answers** — SOL, tolling, choice of law (Klaxon), burden of proof = state; service, pleading, joinder, jury = federal. R-CIV-040
- **Federal common law** — uniquely federal interests + significant conflict (Clearfield; Boyle). R-CIV-041

## F. Pretrial
- **Preliminary injunction** — likely success, likely irreparable harm, balance of equities, public interest (Winter). R-CIV-042
- **TRO** — specific facts + attorney certification; 14 days; security; binds parties and those in active concert with notice. R-CIV-043
- **Complaint** — short and plain statement; plausible, not conclusory (Twombly; Iqbal). R-CIV-044
- **Fraud** — particularity: who, what, when, where, how (9(b)). R-CIV-045
- **Answer** — 21 days; failure to deny = admission; affirmative defenses (8(c)). R-CIV-046
- **Rule 12 waiver** — PJ, venue, process, service waived if omitted from first response; 12(b)(6) and 19 through trial; SMJ never. R-CIV-047
- **Amendment** — once as of course within 21 days; leave freely given (Foman). R-CIV-048
- **Relation back** — same transaction; new party: notice within 90 days + knew of mistake (Krupski). R-CIV-049
- **Rule 11** — improper purpose, legal and factual support after reasonable inquiry; 21-day safe harbor; deterrence. R-CIV-050–051
- **CA:** fact pleading; general/special demurrers; motion to strike; anti-SLAPP two prongs; § 128.7. R-CIV-106–108, R-CIV-119

## G. Joinder and class actions
- **Claims** — Rule 18 any claims; compulsory counterclaim = same transaction, or barred; crossclaims never compulsory. R-CIV-052
- **Parties** — Rule 20 same transaction + common question; misjoinder not fatal. R-CIV-053
- **Required parties** — complete relief / impairment / inconsistent obligations; joint tortfeasors not required; 19(b) equity and good conscience. R-CIV-054–055
- **Impleader** — derivative liability; 14 days without leave. R-CIV-056
- **Intervention** — timely + interest + impairment + inadequate representation. R-CIV-057
- **Interpleader** — statutory: $500, minimal diversity, nationwide service; Rule 22: ordinary jurisdiction. R-CIV-058
- **Class** — numerosity, commonality (Dukes), typicality, adequacy + (b)(1)/(2)/(3); (b)(3) notice and opt-out; 23(e) approval; 23(f) appeal in 14 days. R-CIV-059–061
- **CA:** cross-complaints; compulsory for related claims against plaintiff (§ 426.30). R-CIV-109

## H. Discovery
- **Initial disclosures** — witnesses, documents, damages, insurance; 14 days after 26(f). R-CIV-062
- **Experts / pretrial** — reports 90 days before trial; pretrial 30 days; supplement. R-CIV-063
- **Scope** — relevant to claim or defense + proportional; admissibility not required. R-CIV-064
- **Privilege** — expressly claim + log; 2025 log-timing amendments; clawback; FRE 502(b). R-CIV-065
- **Work product** — anticipation of litigation; substantial need + undue hardship; opinion work product protected (Hickman). R-CIV-066
- **Experts** — testifying: deposable; consulting: exceptional circumstances only. R-CIV-067
- **Depositions** — 10 per side, 7 hours; use at trial if unavailable (> 100 miles). R-CIV-068
- **Written discovery** — 25 interrogatories; RFAs deemed admitted after 30 days. R-CIV-069
- **Rule 35** — motion, good cause, condition in controversy (Schlagenhauf). R-CIV-070
- **ESI** — 37(e): prejudice → cure; intent to deprive → adverse inference/dismissal. R-CIV-071
- **Protective orders** — good cause + conferral. R-CIV-072
- **Compel and sanctions** — conferral; expenses mandatory; 37(b) menu; 37(c)(1) exclusion. R-CIV-073
- **CA:** subject-matter scope; disclosures on demand (60 days); 35/35 limits; demand physical exam; attorney work product (Coito); $1,000 mandatory sanction. R-CIV-110–112

## I. Ending without trial
- **Voluntary dismissal** — notice before answer or SJ motion; two-dismissal rule. R-CIV-074 (**CA:** before trial begins)
- **Involuntary dismissal** — on the merits unless jurisdiction, venue or Rule 19. R-CIV-075
- **Default** — clerk for sum certain; no more than demanded (54(c)); set aside for good cause / Rule 60(b). R-CIV-076
- **Pretrial orders** — scheduling order (good cause to modify); final pretrial order (manifest injustice); Rule 16.1 MDL. R-CIV-077
- **Motions on the pleadings** — 12(b)(6)/12(c) plausibility; 12(d) conversion. R-CIV-084
- **Summary judgment** — no genuine dispute of material fact; reasonable jury; Celotex burden. R-CIV-085–086 (**CA:** separate statement; 81/20/11 days; summary adjudication of whole causes only — R-CIV-113)

## J. Jury and trial
- **Seventh Amendment** — suits at common law in 1791; remedy decisive. R-CIV-078 (**CA:** 1850; three-fourths verdict — R-CIV-116)
- **Mixed claims** — legal issues to the jury first (Beacon; Dairy Queen). R-CIV-079 (**CA:** court may try equitable issues first)
- **Demand** — 14 days after last pleading; waiver; Rule 39(b). R-CIV-080
- **Size and verdict** — 6–12; unanimous unless stipulated; poll. R-CIV-081
- **Selection** — cause (actual/implied bias); 3 peremptories; Batson. R-CIV-082 (**CA:** 6 peremptories)
- **Instructions** — object on the record, distinctly, before jury retires; else plain error. R-CIV-083
- **JMOL** — no legally sufficient evidentiary basis for a reasonable jury. R-CIV-087

## K. Post-trial
- **Renewed JMOL** — 28 days; only grounds in 50(a). R-CIV-088 (**CA:** JNOV without prior motion)
- **New trial** — clear weight, excessive damages, error, misconduct, new evidence; 28 days; remittitur, no additur. R-CIV-089 (**CA:** § 657; 15/180/75 days; additur — R-CIV-117)
- **Rule 60(b)** — six grounds; reasonable time; one year for (1)–(3); void judgments too (Coney Island). R-CIV-090
- **Verdict forms** — Rule 49 inconsistency responses. R-CIV-091
- **Juror non-disclosure** — dishonest answer + valid cause challenge (McDonough); FRE 606(b). R-CIV-092
- **Bench findings** — special findings; clear error (Rule 52). R-CIV-093

## L. Preclusion
- **Claim** — same claim (transaction), same parties, final judgment on merits. R-CIV-094 (**CA:** primary rights — R-CIV-114)
- **Issue** — same issue actually litigated, determined, essential; full and fair opportunity. R-CIV-095
- **Non-mutual** — defensive generally OK; offensive discretionary (Parklane). R-CIV-096
- **Non-parties** — Taylor's six exceptions only. R-CIV-097
- **Across systems** — § 1738; Semtek. R-CIV-098 (**CA:** not final while appeal pending — R-CIV-115)

## M. Appeals
- **Final judgment** — § 1291; Rule 54(b); FRAP 4: 30 days. R-CIV-099
- **Interlocutory** — injunctions (§ 1292(a)(1)); certified questions (§ 1292(b)); 23(f). R-CIV-100
- **Collateral order** — conclusive, separate, unreviewable later; narrow (Mohawk; GEO Group); mandamus. R-CIV-101
- **Review** — law de novo; findings clear error; discretion abuse; jury verdict reasonable-jury. R-CIV-102
- **CA:** § 904.1 list; 60 days after notice of entry; writs. R-CIV-118
- **Arbitration waiver** — no prejudice needed (Morgan; Quach). R-CIV-120
