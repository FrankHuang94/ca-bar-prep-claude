## Scope
Everything a court must decide before, during and after a civil trial: **which court** (subject-matter jurisdiction, personal jurisdiction, service, venue, removal), **which law** (Erie and federal common law), **how the case is framed** (pleadings, Rule 11, joinder, class actions), **how facts are gathered** (disclosure, discovery, sanctions), **how it ends without trial** (dismissal, default, summary judgment), **trial** (jury right, selection, instructions, JMOL, new trial, relief from judgment), **the judgment's effect** (claim and issue preclusion) and **appeal**. California adds its own versions of most of these, compared in [ca-distinctions.md](ca-distinctions.md).

## How it is tested
- **MBE.** About two-thirds of the 25 scored questions come from categories I (jurisdiction and venue), III (pretrial procedures) and V (motions); the rest from II (Erie), IV (jury), VI (verdicts, judgments, preclusion) and VII (appeals) [S-040]. Questions are rule-precise: day counts (14, 21, 28, 30, 90), waiver rules, and which statute or Rule governs. See [mbe-rules.md](mbe-rules.md).

## Crossover map
Civil Procedure has rarely been crossed with other subjects on California essays: the frequency table shows one **Civil Procedure + Evidence** essay (Feb 2019: admissibility of testimony plus a motion to dismiss for lack of SMJ). Natural pairings to expect:
- **Evidence** — discovery privilege and work product overlap with FRE 501–502 and attorney–client privilege.
- **Remedies** — preliminary injunctions and TROs (Rule 65) share the Remedies merits test.
- **Professional Responsibility** — Rule 11 and discovery abuse overlap with candor and fairness duties.
- **Constitutional Law** — personal jurisdiction (due process), the Seventh Amendment and standing.
- **Business Associations** — derivative suits (Rule 23.1) and entity citizenship for diversity.

## Study order (first pass, about 25–30 hours)
1. [primer.md](primer.md) — the map and vocabulary (1 h).
2. **Jurisdiction block** — outline I.A–I.D with R-CIV-001 to R-CIV-036 and [flowcharts.md](flowcharts.md) charts 1–5 (8 h). Highest MBE and essay yield.
3. **Erie** — outline II with R-CIV-037 to R-CIV-041 (2 h).
4. **Pleadings, Rule 11, joinder, class actions** — outline III.A–III.D (5 h).
5. **Discovery** — outline III.E with R-CIV-062 to R-CIV-073 (4 h); then the discovery essay template.
6. **Dispositive and trial motions, jury, judgments, preclusion, appeals** — outline III.F–VII (6 h).
7. **California layer** — [ca-distinctions.md](ca-distinctions.md) and R-CIV-103 to R-CIV-120 (3 h).
8. Practice — [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md), then timed MBE sets and one past essay per week.
