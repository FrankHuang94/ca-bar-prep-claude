## Numbers to know cold
- **$75,000** (exceed) · **$5,000,000** and **100** members (CAFA) · **$500** (statutory interpleader) · **$35,000** (California limited civil case) · **$12,500 / $6,250** (California small claims) · **$1,000** (California mandatory discovery sanction).
- **14 / 21 / 28 / 30 / 90 days** and **1 year** — see the day-count table in [mbe-rules.md](mbe-rules.md).
- **California:** 30 days to respond; 60 days for initial disclosures after demand; 45 days to move to compel further responses; 81/20/11 days for summary judgment; 15/180/75 days for new-trial motions; 60/180 days to appeal; 35 interrogatories and 35 RFAs; 75 miles for the demand physical exam; three-fourths verdict; 6 peremptories.
