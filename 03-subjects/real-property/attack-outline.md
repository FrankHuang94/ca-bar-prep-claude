# Real Property — attack outline

**Step 0.** Headings from the call. Timeline of every transfer. Majority rule first; "In California, …" only where it changes the result.

## 1. Estates and future interests
- **FSA:** forever; "to A" is enough (R-PRP-001).
- **FSD + possibility of reverter:** "so long as"; automatic (R-PRP-002). **CA:** treated as condition subsequent; 30-year power of termination (R-PRP-005).
- **FSSCS + right of entry:** "but if … may re-enter"; must elect (R-PRP-003).
- **FS subject to executory limitation:** gift over to a third party; RAP applies (R-PRP-004).
- **Life estate:** no waste; pays taxes and interest up to income (R-PRP-006, R-PRP-007).
- **Vested remainder** (ascertained, no condition precedent) vs. **contingent** (unascertained or condition precedent) (R-PRP-009, R-PRP-010).
- **Executory interest:** divests (R-PRP-011).
- **Class closes** when any member can demand possession (R-PRP-013).
- **RAP:** must vest, if at all, within 21 years after a life in being; **CA:** or within 90 years, then reform (R-PRP-014 to R-PRP-016).
- **Restraints:** disabling restraint on a fee void (R-PRP-017).

## 2. Concurrent ownership
- **TIC:** default; no survivorship (R-PRP-019).
- **JT:** four unities + clear survivorship words; **CA:** expressly declared (R-PRP-020).
- **Severance:** conveyance severs; will does not; **CA:** self-conveyance severs if recorded per § 683.2 (R-PRP-021).
- **Mortgage by one JT:** lien theory → no severance, survivor takes free (*Nogarr*); **lease** ends at lessor's death (*Tenhet*) (R-PRP-022).
- **TBE:** not in California (R-PRP-023).
- **Possession:** no rent absent ouster; share third-party rents (R-PRP-024).
- **Contribution:** taxes and mortgage yes; repairs and improvements only in accounting or partition (R-PRP-025).
- **Partition:** in kind preferred; **CA:** buyout right (R-PRP-026).

## 3. Landlord-tenant
- **Tenancies:** years / periodic / at will / sufferance (R-PRP-027, R-PRP-028).
- **Holdover:** landlord elects eviction or new periodic tenancy (R-PRP-029).
- **Delivery:** English rule (actual possession) majority (R-PRP-030).
- **Rent; deposits** (**CA:** one month's rent cap; 21 days) (R-PRP-031).
- **Tenant duties:** no waste; repairs; lawful use (R-PRP-032).
- **Abandonment:** landlord must mitigate; **CA:** § 1951.2, tenant's burden; lock-in § 1951.4 (R-PRP-033).
- **Quiet enjoyment / actual eviction:** total → no rent; partial by landlord → no rent (R-PRP-034).
- **Constructive eviction:** substantial interference + notice + vacate within a reasonable time (R-PRP-035).
- **Habitability:** residential; non-waivable; tenant may stay (*Green*) (R-PRP-036).
- **Remedies:** terminate; repair and deduct (**CA:** one month's rent, twice a year); withhold; damages (R-PRP-037).
- **Retaliation:** **CA:** 180-day presumption (R-PRP-038).
- **Assignment vs. sublease:** whole term vs. less; privity map (R-PRP-039, R-PRP-040).
- **Covenants running with leasehold:** intent + touch and concern + privity of estate (R-PRP-041).
- **Consent:** **CA:** not unreasonably withheld (*Kendall*; commercial leases; residential only by analogy, unsettled) (R-PRP-042).
- **Landlord transfers:** grantee bound by and benefits from running covenants (R-PRP-043).
- **No self-help;** unlawful detainer (R-PRP-044).
- **Tort:** common-law exceptions; **CA:** ordinary negligence (R-PRP-045).
- **CA TPA:** just cause after 12 months; rent cap (R-PRP-046).
- **Surrender, destruction, condemnation** (R-PRP-047).

## 4. Covenants and servitudes
- **Real covenant burden:** writing, intent, touch and concern, horizontal + vertical privity, notice → damages (R-PRP-050).
- **Benefit:** writing, intent, touch and concern, relaxed vertical privity (R-PRP-051).
- **Equitable servitude:** writing (or common scheme), intent, touch and concern, notice → injunction (R-PRP-052).
- **Common scheme:** plan + notice (*Sanborn*); **CA:** recorded instrument needed (*Riley*), recorded CC&Rs suffice (*Citizens*) (R-PRP-053).
- **Touch and concern;** HOA assessments run (*Neponsit*) (R-PRP-054).
- **Scope** of "residential purposes" (R-PRP-055).
- **Defenses:** changed conditions, acquiescence, abandonment, laches, unclean hands, release, merger, prescription (R-PRP-056).
- **Unenforceable:** discriminatory covenants (R-PRP-057).
- **CID:** recorded restrictions presumed reasonable (*Nahrstedt*) (R-PRP-058); condos and co-ops (R-PRP-059).

## 5. Easements, profits, licenses
- **Classify:** affirmative/negative; appurtenant/in gross; profit; license (R-PRP-060).
- **Express:** writing; **CA:** reservation for a third party OK (*Willard*) (R-PRP-061).
- **Prior use:** common owner, apparent and continuous, reasonably necessary (R-PRP-062).
- **Necessity:** common owner, strict necessity at severance; ends with necessity (R-PRP-063).
- **Prescription:** open, continuous, adverse, period; **CA:** 5 years, no taxes, § 1008 signs (R-PRP-064).
- **Estoppel:** reliance on permission (R-PRP-065).
- **Scope:** degree yes, kind no; no non-dominant land (R-PRP-066).
- **Transfer:** appurtenant runs; commercial in gross assignable (R-PRP-067).
- **Termination:** merger, release, abandonment (act + intent), estoppel, prescription, end of necessity (R-PRP-068).

## 6. Fixtures, zoning, rights incident to ownership
- **Fixtures:** annexation, adaptation, intent; trade fixtures removable; UCC 9-334 (R-PRP-069).
- **Zoning:** rational basis (*Euclid*); nonconforming uses (no expansion; amortization); variances (no use variances in CA); spot zoning (R-PRP-070 to R-PRP-072).
- **Takings:** physical, *Lucas*, *Penn Central*, *Nollan/Dolan/Sheetz* (R-PRP-073; R-CON-077 to R-CON-081).
- **Support, water, light and air** (R-PRP-074 to R-PRP-076).

## 7. Land-sale contracts
- **SOF;** part performance (R-PRP-078).
- **Time not of the essence** unless made so (R-PRP-079).
- **Marketable title** at closing (R-PRP-080).
- **Remedies:** specific performance; benefit of bargain; deposit as liquidated damages (R-PRP-081).
- **Equitable conversion:** buyer bears risk; **CA:** UVPRA, seller bears risk until title or possession (R-PRP-082).
- **Options / ROFR** (R-PRP-083).
- **Disclosure** of known latent defects; new-home warranty (R-PRP-084, R-PRP-085).
- **Merger** into deed (R-PRP-086).

## 8. Mortgages
- **Lien theory**; deed of trust (R-PRP-087).
- **Equitable mortgage** (R-PRP-088); **installment contracts** (R-PRP-089).
- **PMM priority** (R-PRP-090); **future advances** (R-PRP-091).
- **Pre-foreclosure rights** (R-PRP-092); **equity of redemption, no clogging** (R-PRP-093).
- **Subject to vs. assume;** surety; subrogation (R-PRP-094, R-PRP-095).
- **Due-on-sale** enforceable (R-PRP-096); **mortgage follows note** (R-PRP-097).
- **Discharge;** deed in lieu keeps juniors (R-PRP-098).
- **Foreclosure:** types; acceleration (R-PRP-099); priorities (R-PRP-100); juniors out, seniors stay (R-PRP-101); proceeds and deficiency (R-PRP-102); statutory redemption (R-PRP-103).
- **CA:** one action; no deficiency after trustee's sale or on purchase money (R-PRP-104).

## 9. Titles
- **Adverse possession:** actual, open, exclusive, hostile, continuous (R-PRP-105); **CA:** 5 years + taxes (R-PRP-106); tacking, disabilities (R-PRP-107); agreed boundary (R-PRP-108).
- **Deed formalities** (R-PRP-109); **delivery** (R-PRP-110).
- **Covenants of title:** present vs. future; **CA:** grant deed § 1113 (R-PRP-111).
- **Estoppel by deed** (R-PRP-112); **void vs. voidable** (R-PRP-113); **authority** (R-PRP-114); **will and operation of law** (R-PRP-115).
- **Recording acts:** race / notice / race-notice; **CA:** race-notice § 1214 (R-PRP-116).
- **BFP:** value + no notice (actual, record, inquiry) (R-PRP-117).
- **Chain of title:** wild, late, early deeds (R-PRP-118); **shelter** (R-PRP-119).
- **Title insurance** (R-PRP-120); **judgment and tax liens; lis pendens** (R-PRP-121).
