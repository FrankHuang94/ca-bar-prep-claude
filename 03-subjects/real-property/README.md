## Scope
- **MBE** (NCBE outline [S-040]), about one-fifth of the questions from each category:
  - I Ownership (estates and future interests, cotenancy, landlord-tenant, RAP, alienability, fair housing, conflict of laws);
  - II Rights in land (covenants and servitudes, HOAs, easements, fixtures, zoning);
  - III Real estate contracts (brokerage, statute of frauds, marketable title, equitable conversion, options, fitness, merger);
  - IV Mortgages and security devices (types, theories, transfers, discharge, foreclosure, priorities);
  - V Titles (adverse possession, deeds, operation of law, recording, title insurance, liens).
- **California essay outline** [S-090]: nature and ownership of land; landlord and tenant; fixtures; non-possessory interests; adverse possession; rights incident to ownership; conveyancing and security devices. Numbering unconfirmed [VERIFY V-024].

## Study order (you completed Property coursework)
1. **Primer** ([`primer.md`](primer.md)) to reset vocabulary, then the **attack outline** ([`attack-outline.md`](attack-outline.md)).
2. **Estates and future interests + RAP** (outline I.A, I.D): drill classification with [`flowcharts.md`](flowcharts.md) until automatic. High MBE yield.
3. **Landlord-tenant** (I.C): the essay favorite. Learn the remedy menu and the privity table.
4. **Concurrent ownership** (I.B), with the California joint-tenancy points.
5. **Recording acts and deeds** (V.B, V.D): the three statute types, BFP status, chain of title, covenants of title.
6. **Easements and servitudes** (II.A, II.B): creation tables and the termination defense checklist.
7. **Mortgages** (IV): priorities, transfers, foreclosure distribution; then the California anti-deficiency overlay.
8. **Contracts, zoning, adverse possession, fixtures** (III, II.C, II.D, V.A).
9. Throughout: [`worked-examples.md`](worked-examples.md), [`traps.md`](traps.md), licensed MBE questions, and past essays with [`essay-templates.md`](essay-templates.md).
