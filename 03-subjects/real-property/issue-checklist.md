# Real Property — issue checklist

**Before you read the facts:** turn the call into headings (one per call; party by party if the call names parties). Then build a **dated timeline** in the margin: every conveyance, lease, mortgage, recording, payment and possession. Most Real Property essays are won or lost on the timeline.

## Threshold issues (always first)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| What law governs? | Call says "under California law" or names a recording-act type | Apply it as the main rule; otherwise majority rule + CA sentence ([`ca-distinctions.md`](ca-distinctions.md)) |
| Is there a valid transfer at all? | Unsigned or oral agreement; deed not handed over; forged signature | Statute of frauds (R-PRP-078); delivery (R-PRP-110); void vs. voidable (R-PRP-113) |
| What did each instrument create? | "So long as," "but if," "for life," "and her heirs," "to the children of" | Estate classification (R-PRP-001 to R-PRP-011) |
| Is the land owned concurrently? | Conveyance "to A and B"; spouses; inheritance by siblings | Cotenancy type (R-PRP-019 to R-PRP-023) |

## Ownership (I)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Defeasible fee and future interest | Use restriction with a reverter or gift over; school/church/park grants | R-PRP-002 to R-PRP-005; California § 885.020 |
| Life estate and waste | "To W for life"; life tenant not repairing, cutting timber, not paying taxes | R-PRP-006, R-PRP-007 |
| Remainders and class gifts | "To A for life, then to B's children" | R-PRP-009, R-PRP-010, R-PRP-013 |
| RAP | Contingent gift tied to an event or age over 21, a class, or an option | R-PRP-014 to R-PRP-016 |
| Restraint on alienation | "Shall never sell"; ROFR at a fixed price | R-PRP-017 |
| Severance | A joint tenant deeds, mortgages, leases, contracts to sell, or records a declaration | R-PRP-021, R-PRP-022 |
| Cotenant rights | One co-owner lives there, rents it out, pays taxes, builds | R-PRP-024, R-PRP-025 |
| Partition | Co-owners disagree about selling | R-PRP-026 |

## Landlord-tenant (I.C)
Run in this order for each party:
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Type of tenancy; statute of frauds | Oral lease over a year; month-to-month; holdover | R-PRP-027 to R-PRP-029 |
| Delivery of possession | Prior tenant still in possession at start | R-PRP-030 |
| Quiet enjoyment; actual eviction | Landlord locks out or takes part of premises | R-PRP-034 |
| Constructive eviction | Flooding, no heat, noise the landlord could stop; tenant moves out | R-PRP-035 |
| Habitability (residential) | Code violations; tenant stays and withholds or repairs | R-PRP-036, R-PRP-037 |
| Retaliation | Eviction notice soon after a complaint | R-PRP-038 |
| Rent and deposits | Unpaid rent; deposit withheld | R-PRP-031 |
| Assignment vs. sublease; liability | Tenant transfers; new occupant defaults | R-PRP-039, R-PRP-040 |
| Lease covenants running | Covenant to insure, repair, option to renew | R-PRP-041 |
| Consent to transfer | "No assignment without consent"; landlord refuses | R-PRP-042 |
| Abandonment and mitigation | Tenant leaves early; landlord does not relet | R-PRP-033 |
| Landlord's remedies | Lockout, utility shutoff, unlawful detainer | R-PRP-044 |
| Landlord tort liability | Injury on stairs, in common area, from a known dog | R-PRP-045 |
| California tenant statutes | Residential tenancy of 12+ months ended or rent raised | R-PRP-046 |

## Non-possessory interests (II)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Classification | Right to cross, take timber, use a beach, park | R-PRP-060 |
| Express easement | Written right-of-way; reservation in a deed | R-PRP-061 |
| Implied easement | Common owner; visible pipe or path before division | R-PRP-062 |
| Necessity | Landlocked parcel after division | R-PRP-063 |
| Prescription | Years of use without permission | R-PRP-064 |
| Estoppel / license | Oral permission plus expensive improvements | R-PRP-065 |
| Scope | Subdivision of dominant land; new utilities; bigger trucks | R-PRP-066 |
| Transfer and notice | Dominant or servient land sold | R-PRP-067 |
| Termination | Nonuse, fence across the path, merger, release | R-PRP-068 |
| Covenant / servitude: creation | Deed restriction; recorded CC&Rs; subdivision plan | R-PRP-050 to R-PRP-053 |
| Running to successors | Promise by a former owner; plaintiff wants damages or injunction | R-PRP-050 to R-PRP-052, R-PRP-054 |
| Scope of restriction | Home business, daycare, short-term rental | R-PRP-055 |
| Defenses | Widespread violations, changed neighborhood, delay | R-PRP-056 |
| HOA / CID | Association rule or recorded restriction challenged | R-PRP-058 |
| Fixtures | Removal of installed items at sale, lease end or foreclosure | R-PRP-069 |
| Zoning | Permit denied, nonconforming use, variance | R-PRP-070 to R-PRP-072 |
| Takings | Permit condition, physical occupation, total loss of use | R-PRP-073 |
| Rights incident to ownership | Excavation, water diversion, blocked view, smoke and odors | R-PRP-074 to R-PRP-076; Torts (nuisance) |

## Contracts and conveyancing (III, V)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Statute of frauds; part performance | Oral deal; buyer in possession | R-PRP-078 |
| Time; tender | Late closing | R-PRP-079 |
| Marketable title | Encumbrance, zoning violation, gap in chain found before closing | R-PRP-080 |
| Risk of loss | Fire or flood before closing | R-PRP-082 |
| Disclosure | Seller hid defect; "as is" | R-PRP-084, R-PRP-085 |
| Merger | Buyer sues after closing on a contract promise | R-PRP-086 |
| Deed covenants | General warranty, grant or quitclaim deed; encumbrance or eviction | R-PRP-111 |
| Estoppel by deed | Grantor acquires title after conveying | R-PRP-112 |
| Recording priority | Two buyers, or buyer vs. mortgagee | R-PRP-116 to R-PRP-119 |
| Adverse possession | Long possession, fences, cultivation, taxes | R-PRP-105 to R-PRP-108 |
| Remedies | Damages, specific performance, ejectment, quiet title | R-PRP-081; Remedies files |

## Mortgages (IV)
| Issue | Trigger facts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Type of security | Deed absolute but loan; installment contract | R-PRP-088, R-PRP-089 |
| Priorities | Several mortgages, judgment liens, refinancing | R-PRP-090, R-PRP-091, R-PRP-100 |
| Transfer by mortgagor | Sale "subject to" or with assumption; due-on-sale | R-PRP-094 to R-PRP-096 |
| Foreclosure effects | Who is wiped out, who gets proceeds | R-PRP-099, R-PRP-101, R-PRP-102 |
| Redemption | Before sale; after sale | R-PRP-093, R-PRP-103 |
| California deficiency limits | Lender seeks the shortfall | R-PRP-104 |

## Finish
- A conclusion on every issue ("likely," "probably").
- Re-read the call: did you answer **every** party and **every** question asked?
