# Exam-day logistics

> **Read this first.**
> - Every fact below comes from **July 2026** State Bar documents, verified through a search index on 2026-11-04 ([sources.md](../00-exam-intel/sources.md)).
> - The State Bar publishes a new **Admittance Ticket Bulletin** for each exam. The July 2026 one is [S-003], and notices began on June 30, 2026.
> - **When the July 2027 bulletin appears (expected around late June 2027), re-check every row** and update this file with `/update-exam-intel`.
> - Anything not confirmed for 2027 is marked `[VERIFY]`.

## 4. Laptop preparation (from now)

1. **Use one laptop for all practice from now on**, the same one you will use on the exam. Your typing speed on that keyboard is part of your exam speed.
2. Check it against the current Examplify minimum requirements every few months. The State Bar points to ExamSoft's published requirements [S-008].
3. **No detachable-keyboard tablet and no screen over 17.3 inches** [S-008].
4. Practise in a distraction-free setup: full-screen editor, no internet, no spell-check if Examplify will not have it [VERIFY V-051]. Spelling and grammar are not graded [S-010], so do not slow down to fix typos.
5. **Battery health:** if the battery cannot last 3.5 hours, plan around the outlet (bring the charger) or replace the battery well before July.
6. **Back-up plan:** learn the State Bar's laptop-failure procedure from the July 2027 bulletin [VERIFY V-051]. Write at least one practice essay **by hand** under time so you know your handwritten speed.

### Handwriting fallback
- **When it applies:** if you miss the mock-exam deadline you **must** handwrite [S-008] [S-009]. You can also choose handwriting on the application [S-008].
- **What it costs:** for most people handwriting is much slower than typing. Measure your own words per minute both ways. If typing is clearly faster, **protect the laptop route by doing the mock exam early.**

## 6. Hotel and travel (San Diego area)

You live in San Diego, so the question is commute risk, not distance.
- **Stay near the site if** your normal drive is more than about 30 minutes, or crosses a corridor with unpredictable morning traffic. One accident must not put you near the **one-hour refusal line** [S-003].
- **If you stay:** book as soon as your site is confirmed. The 2026 Mission Valley site was itself a hotel [S-029], but its rooms may sell out to other applicants. Ask for a quiet room away from elevators and ice machines. Book **Monday to Wednesday nights**.
- **If you commute:** do the trial drive (T−7). Leave 30 minutes earlier than the trial suggests. Have a plan B route. Ride-share is a backup if parking is uncertain.
- **Parking:** 2026 Mission Valley parking was paid, with a validation discount [S-029]. Bring a payment card.

## 7. The night before each day
- Pack the bag and put it by the door, with the ticket and ID **in** the bag.
- Set two alarms.
- No new material. A 20-minute look at a one-page list is the maximum.
- Same bedtime as your rehearsal weeks. See [mindset-and-fatigue.md](mindset-and-fatigue.md).
