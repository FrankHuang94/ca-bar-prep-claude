# Time management

Part A is the exam itself. Part B is the next nine months of studying around a full-time job.

## Part A: Exam-day time plans

### A1. The fixed structure

| Day | Session | Content | Length | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tue July 27, 2027** | AM | Essays 1–3 | 3 h | [S-003] [S-001] |
| | PM | Essays 4–5 + Performance Test (90 min) | 3.5 h | [S-003] |
| **Wed July 28, 2027** | AM | MBE questions 1–100 | 3 h | [S-006] [S-003] |
| | PM | MBE questions 101–200 | 3 h | [S-006] [S-003] |

**Reporting times** (July 2026 bulletin; confirm for 2027 [VERIFY V-055]):
- Be seated by **8:20 a.m.** each day. Instructions began at about 8:30 a.m., and the exam started immediately after.
- Afternoon re-entry was at about 1:30 p.m., with instructions at about 1:45 p.m.
- Anyone arriving **one hour or more** after a session starts is refused admission [S-003].

**Rule for every session:** the exact start time varies with the instructions. **Write the actual start time at the top of your scratch paper** and compute your hard stops from it.

### A2. Day 1 morning: Essays 1–3 (3 hours)

| Elapsed | Clock if the session starts at 8:40 (example) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 8:40 | Essay 1: read the call, read, outline ([essay-method.md](essay-method.md)) |
| 0:15 | 8:55 | Essay 1: start writing |
| **1:00** | **9:40** | **HARD STOP**, then Essay 2 |
| 1:15 | 9:55 | Essay 2: start writing |
| **2:00** | **10:40** | **HARD STOP**, then Essay 3 |
| 2:15 | 10:55 | Essay 3: start writing |
| **3:00** | **11:40** | Session ends. Exit Examplify as instructed. |

**Order.**
- The July 2026 bulletin says you may answer a session's questions in any order and allocate the time within the session as you see fit; once the session ends you cannot return to its questions [S-003]. Confirm in the July 2027 bulletin.
- **Default: answer in order.** Reordering costs reading time and invites mistakes.
- **Exception:** after reading all three calls (2 minutes), if one essay is in a subject you know badly, you may do it **last**. It still gets its full 60 minutes. Doing it last only stops it from unsettling the other two.

### A3. Day 1 afternoon: Essays 4–5 + PT (3.5 hours)

You can do the PT first or last. Test both orders in SIM-01 and SIM-02 and use the better one in SIM-03 and on the exam. **Default: PT first.**
- The PT counts as two essays [S-010].
- It needs the most sustained reading, which is easier when you are fresher.
- Its 90-minute hard stop protects the essays that follow.

**Plan A (PT first, default):**

| Elapsed | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:30 | PT ([04-performance-test/method.md](../04-performance-test/method.md)): ~40 min read and outline, ~50 min write |
| **1:30** | **HARD STOP**, then Essay 4 |
| 1:30–2:30 | Essay 4 |
| **2:30** | **HARD STOP**, then Essay 5 |
| 2:30–3:30 | Essay 5 |

**Plan B (essays first):**

| Elapsed | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Essay 4 |
| 1:00–2:00 | Essay 5 |
| 2:00–3:30 | PT |

Plan B's risk: an essay overrun takes time from the double-weighted PT. Use B only if your simulations show you finish essays reliably on time.

### A4. Day 2: MBE (2 × 3 hours)

|---|---|
| 1:00 | 33 / 133 |

Full method: [05-mbe/strategy.md](../05-mbe/strategy.md) §3–§4. There are no scheduled breaks inside a session [S-006].

### A5. Hard stops: why they are absolute

- Using the Feb 2026 formula for illustration, one raw point on one essay is worth about **2 total scaled points**, and on the PT about **4**, because the PT is doubled ([format-and-scoring.md](../00-exam-intel/format-and-scoring.md) §2).
- An essay overrun of 10 minutes rarely adds more than 5 raw points to that essay. Losing 10 minutes on the next essay can cost 10 or more, especially if a call is left blank.
- **The overrun almost always costs more than it gains.**

**How to enforce hard stops:**
- Write the stop times on scratch paper before you start. The State Bar has provided scratch paper in the written sessions [VERIFY V-050].
- At each **stop minus 10 minutes**, apply the run-out-of-time protocol ([essay-method.md](essay-method.md) §8).
- At the stop, finish the sentence and leave.

### A6. If you fall behind

| Where | Symptom | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Essay outline | Still outlining at 0:20 | Stop outlining. Write headings for every call now, then fill them in. |
| Essay writing | At 0:50 with issues not started | Headline IRAC for each remaining issue, 60–90 seconds each ([essay-method.md](essay-method.md) §8). |
| Essay | You overran into the next essay's time | Do **not** try to win it back on that essay. Give the next essay its normal outline time and shorten its minor issues. |
| PT reading | Still reading the File at 0:45 | Stop reading. Outline from what you have, then start writing by 0:50. Look up specifics while writing. |
| PT writing | At 1:20, sections missing | Write each missing section as a heading plus 2–3 sentences. "Compliance with instructions" is graded [S-076]: every requested part must exist. |
| MBE | 5 or more questions behind at 1:00 | Apply the 2-minute cap strictly. Stop re-reading stems. Predict, choose, move on. |
| MBE | 10 or more behind at 2:00 | Cap at 90 seconds. On long stems, read the call, then skim for the triggering fact. |
| MBE | 3 minutes left, 10 or more questions unread | Bubble a guess for **every** remaining question first (no penalty for wrong answers [S-007]), then read as many as time allows. |

## Part B: Study-time management for a full-time worker

The weekly hours and the phase dates are set in [config/plan.yml](../config/plan.yml) and the study plan (`01-study-plan/`). This part is about *how* to fit them around work. The November template has about 1.25 h on weekdays (0.75 h Friday) and about 3 h each weekend day. From December the plan runs at 28–38 h/week: 3–4 h every weekday evening and about 9–10 h each weekend day (`01-study-plan/master-plan.md` §3).

### B1. Weekday micro-sessions

**Principle: fixed time slots, fixed content, no decisions in the moment.** The slots below are the November pattern; from December the evening slot becomes the 3–4 h block in `master-plan.md` §3.

| Slot | Duration | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before work | 45–60 min | One timed MBE set (17 questions in 30 min) **plus** a 15–30 min review of that set | Fresh attention; the set and its review in one sitting |
| Lunch or commute (not driving) | 15–20 min | Flashcards (`build_anki.py` deck) or rule cards | Spaced repetition fits small gaps |
| Evening | 20–30 min | Read one outline section, or `/drill` one rule set | Low energy, so use low-intensity tasks |

- **If the morning slot is lost** (an early meeting or deal crunch), move the MBE set to the evening. Drop the reading; do not drop the questions.
- **Two-minute start rule:** on bad days, commit to just opening the material. Starting is the hard part.
- **Corporate development calendar:** when you can see deal-heavy weeks coming, enter them as `blackouts` in `config/plan.yml` so `/today` and `/weekly-review` re-plan around them instead of letting you fall behind silently.

### B2. Weekend blocks

November sizes are shown; from December each weekend day is about 9–10 h, so run these blocks plus longer reading and MBE blocks (`master-plan.md` §3).

| Block | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | ~3 h | One **timed essay** (60) + self-grade or `/essay` (30), then a new-subject outline section or a mixed MBE set (90) |
| Sunday | ~3 h | **Protected PT block** (see B3) **or** a second essay + review, then the weekly review (`/weekly-review`, 15 min) |

### B3. Protect the PT practice block

The PT is worth two essays [S-010], is the least dependent on memorized law, and is the easiest practice to postpone, because it needs an unbroken 2 hours.
- **Book a recurring 2-hour slot** (90-minute PT + 30-minute review) on the same day and time, as a calendar event you treat like a client meeting. Sunday morning is a good default.
- The study plan sets the frequency. When a weekend is lost, **move the PT block to the next free evening rather than skipping it.** Skip an essay before you skip a PT.
- Do PTs on the same laptop and in Examplify-like conditions: one screen, no internet, paper File and Library if you can print them.

### B4. The intensive phase without study leave
- Full-time leave is not possible, so from December the plan runs at **28–38 h/week on top of your job**: **3–4 h every weekday + about 20 h across the weekend**. The intensive phase (May 3 – Jul 11, 2027) is the peak, at 36–38 h (see `01-study-plan/master-plan.md` §3–4).
- **Weekday evenings are for short, timed units:**
  - one timed essay (60 min) + self-grading (30 min);
  - one timed MBE set + review;
  - a `/drill`.
  Do not attempt new reading on a tired weekday evening; schedule it for weekend mornings.
- **Weekends carry the long units:** full PTs, long timed MBE sets, second-pass reading, and the three full simulations (all on weekends).
- **Use PTO surgically:**
  - Mon–Thu July 26–29 (exam week): book it now.
  - If possible, one or two days before each weekend simulation, and the Fridays of the last two weeks.
  - Ask for light work weeks in July if your role allows.
- **Rehearse exam timing on weekends:** at least once a month from May, start at 8:20 a.m. [S-003] and run 3-hour blocks.

### B5. Weekly time hygiene
- **Track hours actually studied** (the dashboard counts attempts; a simple weekly total is enough for hours).
- If you are more than 20% below plan for two weeks running, run `/weekly-review`. Re-plan; do not try to catch up by cramming.
- Keep **one fully off half-day per week** ([mindset-and-fatigue.md](mindset-and-fatigue.md)). It protects the other six and a half days.
