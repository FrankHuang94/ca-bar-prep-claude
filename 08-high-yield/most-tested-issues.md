# Most-tested issues

Three parts: **(a)** California essays, subject by subject; **(b)** the MBE, subject by subject; **(c)** a one-page "10 hours left" list. Every rule ID points to `03-subjects/<slug>/rule-statements.md`, which is the authority if anything here seems to differ.

## How to read the essay counts

- **"Tagged in N of M"**: N = essays (out of all 166, Feb 2012–Jul 2026) whose row in [`index.csv`](../06-past-exams/index.csv) carries that issue tag; M = essays labelled with the subject.
- **†** = at least one tagged essay was labelled with a different subject (e.g., a property-tort issue inside a Remedies essay).
- **Family** rows add up related tags (shown by prefix) where single tags are thin. An essay counts once per family.
- **Last** = the most recent administration with the tag. `2025-03` is the Feb 2025 retest booklet.
- **Caveat.** Issue tags were inferred from the calls and search summaries, not from the selected answers [VERIFY V-071]. All analyses are index-level [VERIFY V-070]. Subject counts are reliable; issue counts are only a guide to emphasis. **Never skip an issue because its count is low.**
- **T1, T2 …** = the numbered template in that subject's `essay-templates.md`.
