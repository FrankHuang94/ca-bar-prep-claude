## The question in brief (my words)

A couple married 20 years while domiciled in another state bought California land with that state's earnings; in 2017 the husband, without the wife's written consent, deeded it to himself and his daughter as joint tenants; he left an undated, unwitnessed handwritten will.

**Call:** Under California law, what rights do the surviving wife, another family member, and the daughter have in the decedent's estate?
