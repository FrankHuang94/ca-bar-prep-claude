## Model approach (my words)

1. **Follow the call.** One top-level heading per call. Under it, one sub-heading per issue, in the order of the issue map.
2. **Time.** About 15 minutes to outline, then about 45 minutes of writing per issue (1 issues mapped). Spend more on the issues the facts develop most.
3. **For each issue:** a 1–3 sentence rule (from the subject's `rule-statements.md`), then element-by-element application with the specific facts, both sides where the facts cut both ways, and a conclusion.
4. **California law.** Use it only where the call asks for it, or where the subject is tested under California law (Community Property, Wills, Trusts, PR, and the CEC/CCP comparisons in Evidence and Civil Procedure; see `00-exam-intel/grader-expectations.md`).
