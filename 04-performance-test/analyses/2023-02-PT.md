## The question in brief (my words)

State v. Hughes (State of Columbia): a defendant charged with murder for stabbing his uncle during a dispute claims self-defense; he made a statement to detectives while hospitalized, and the court already denied a Miranda motion.

**Call:** Draft the prosecution's oral argument opposing the defense motion to exclude the defendant's hospital statement as involuntary (do not address Miranda).
