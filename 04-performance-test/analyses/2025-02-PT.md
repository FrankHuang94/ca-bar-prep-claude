## The question in brief (my words)

A 2024 ladder collapse killed a roofer; ladder parts (rung locks) were destroyed after testing, allegedly at Sunrise's instruction; the widow (Mrs. Jamison) is the client.

**Call:** Write a letter to the client on whether she can obtain a default judgment or other sanctions for lost evidence, and whether she can sue the testing company in tort.
