## The question in brief (my words)

Niesi v. Gosling and Hardy (State of Columbia): a commenter on a blog accused the plaintiff of cable theft and more; the blog host also posted a follow-up asking the commenter for more information.

**Call:** Write an objective memo on whether the plaintiff can prove the poster's statements were defamatory and whether the blog host is immune from liability.
