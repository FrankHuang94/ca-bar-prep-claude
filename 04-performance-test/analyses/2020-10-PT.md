## The question in brief (my words)

In re Potential Wright Litigation (State of Columbia): a 12-year-old ran up an $8,980 bill buying in-game enhancements from a gaming company; his mother sent a letter seeking reversal of the charges.

**Call:** Write a memo helping the supervising attorney advise the client: can the minor disaffirm, did the mother's letter disaffirm, and is she liable for the $8,980 charge?
