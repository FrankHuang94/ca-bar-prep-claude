## The question in brief (my words)

Meaney v. Trustees of the University of Columbia: the firm defends the university trustees against a breach-of-contract suit over a garden donation; the question is contract versus (conditional) gift or charitable trust.

**Call:** Write an objective memorandum analyzing whether the donor's garden transfer to the university was a contract or a gift and, if a gift, what kind.
