## The question in brief (my words)

Columbia dispute (Western Insurance Co. v. SecureTrade, Inc.): an insurer's fraud claim; the opposing party seeks arbitration, and the library addresses whether a non-signatory can compel a signatory to arbitrate via equitable estoppel.

**Call:** Draft a letter for the supervising attorney's signature stating the client will not voluntarily arbitrate its fraud claim and explaining why a motion to compel arbitration would be denied.
