Defendant Dalton is charged with second-degree murder and the lesser included offense of involuntary manslaughter in the shooting death of Laura Vons; he claims he did not know the gun was loaded.

**Call:** Draft the prosecution's closing argument in a bench trial, addressing second-degree murder and involuntary manslaughter separately, element by element.
