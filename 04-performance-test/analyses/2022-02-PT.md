## The question in brief (my words)

In re Price (State of Columbia): a court dismissed an indictment for prosecutorial misconduct after a deputy DA dealt with the represented defendant on two dates, one before and one after indictment.

**Call:** Write a memo for the chief deputy DA on whether a deputy DA violated the no-contact rule (Rule 4.2) in two dealings and can invoke a Rule 5.2 defense.
