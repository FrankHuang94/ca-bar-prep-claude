## The question in brief (my words)

People v. Raymond (State of Columbia): a surety's son posted bail for his father, who failed to appear; after acquittal the question is whether the forfeited bond must or should be exonerated.

**Call:** Draft a brief on whether the bail bond must be exonerated (or should be exonerated in the court's discretion) after the defendant's acquittal despite a failure to appear.
