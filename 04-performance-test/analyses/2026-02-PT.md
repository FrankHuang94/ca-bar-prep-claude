## The question in brief (my words)

In State of Columbia v. John Davis, et al., the State seeks to use handwritten transcripts of text messages whose originals were deleted.

**Call:** Draft a brief supporting a motion in limine to exclude handwritten transcripts of deleted text messages under the original documents rule.
