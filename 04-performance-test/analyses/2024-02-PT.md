## The question in brief (my words)

Truck driver Dahir stopped after a prior accident in fog to offer aid; Panko rear-ended his semi and sued; the firm moves for summary judgment under the Good Samaritan statute.

**Call:** Draft a memorandum of points and authorities supporting defendant's summary judgment motion that plaintiff's action is barred by the Columbia Good Samaritan Act.
