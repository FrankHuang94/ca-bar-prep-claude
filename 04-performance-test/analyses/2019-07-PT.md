## The question in brief (my words)

State of Columbia prosecution of a defendant; the prosecutor wants an objective appraisal of three prior incidents: giving a false name/license at a traffic stop, intoxicated shouting at an officer, and a threatening call urging a witness not to testify.

**Call:** Write an objective memo for the prosecutor on whether three prior incidents involving the defendant are admissible as substantive evidence (Rule 404) or for impeachment (Rule 608).
