# Demand letter

> **How often this appears on California PTs:** to be filled in from [past-pt-index.csv](../past-pt-index.csv) in Phase 3. No frequency is stated until then.

## Purpose
Persuade the **opposing party, or their lawyer**, to do something specific (pay, perform, stop, cure, preserve evidence) by a deadline, by showing that your client's legal position is strong and that refusing will cost more than complying.

It is a persuasive document that the other side will read critically, and that a judge may read later. Every statement must hold up.

## Format template

```text
[Firm letterhead, if given]

[Date]

[VIA EMAIL AND U.S. MAIL, if the task specifies delivery]

[Recipient: opposing counsel if the party is represented; otherwise the party]
[Address]

Re: [Matter; e.g., "Demand for payment: Invoice 2219, Harbor Supply Co."]

Dear [Mr./Ms. Surname]:

[OPENING: 1-2 sentences]
This firm represents [client] regarding [matter]. We write to demand that [recipient]
[specific action] by [specific date].

[FACTS: concise, accurate, and favorable; drawn only from the File]

[LEGAL BASIS: one short section per claim, persuasive]
1. [Claim, e.g., "Breach of the Supply Agreement"]
   Rule (Library authority, briefly) -> the facts that satisfy each element -> the conclusion
   Pre-empt the recipient's obvious defense and explain why it fails.
2. ...

[DAMAGES / RELIEF: the calculation, if the File supports one]

[DEMAND]
Accordingly, [client] demands that [recipient]: (1) [action]; (2) [action]; by [date/time].

[CONSEQUENCES: lawful only; e.g., "If we do not receive ..., our client is prepared to
file suit seeking [remedies], including [fees, if the Library allows]."]

[CLOSING]
We look forward to your prompt response.

Very truly yours,

[Role or placeholder; never your real name]
```

## Tone
- **Firm, confident, professional.** Never insulting, sarcastic or emotional. A judge may later read it as an exhibit.
- **Specific:** amounts, dates, document names.
- **Persuasive but accurate:** frame the facts for your client, but do not misstate them. A lawyer must not knowingly make a false statement of material fact or law to a third person (Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct, rule 4.1; ABA Model Rule 4.1).

## Professional-responsibility guardrails

These matter on the PT, and they are also essay material.

| Guardrail | Authority |
|---|---|
| If the recipient is **represented** in the matter, address the letter to their lawyer, not to them. | Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct, rule 4.2; ABA Model Rule 4.2 |
| Do **not threaten criminal, administrative or disciplinary charges** to gain an advantage in a civil dispute. | Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct, rule 3.10 (California-specific; the ABA Model Rules have no counterpart) |
| If the recipient is **unrepresented**, do not imply you are disinterested. Give no legal advice other than to get a lawyer when their interests conflict with your client's, as a demand recipient's do. | Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct, rule 4.3; ABA Model Rule 4.3 |
| No false statements of material fact or law. | Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct, rule 4.1; ABA Model Rule 4.1 |

On the PT, apply whatever **Library** rules govern. If the task memo flags an ethics constraint, address it expressly.

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| No specific demand or deadline | Say exactly what must happen and by when. |
| Threatening a criminal complaint, or a report to a licensing board | Remove it. Threaten only lawful civil steps. |
| Writing to a represented party directly | Address their counsel. |
| Hedging like an objective memo ("our client may possibly have a claim") | State the position confidently, and keep it accurate. |
| Ignoring the other side's obvious defense | Pre-empt it in one short paragraph. |
| Overclaiming damages the File cannot support | Use only the supported figures, and show the arithmetic. |

## Checklist
- [ ] Correct recipient (counsel if represented); date; "Re:" line.
- [ ] Opening states who you represent and the demand.
- [ ] Facts accurate and drawn from the File.
- [ ] Legal basis per claim, with Library authority.
- [ ] Main defense pre-empted.
- [ ] A specific demand, a specific deadline, lawful consequences.
- [ ] No criminal or disciplinary threats; no misstatements.
- [ ] Professional tone; signed by role or placeholder.
