# Format-change tracker

**Bottom line:** July 2027 and February 2028 both use the current General Bar Exam format: 5 essays, a PT, and the 200-question MBE. Starting July 2028, California switches to the NextGen UBE. This repo covers only the current format. If you have to sit after February 2028, the essay subjects, PT method and MBE subjects carry over only partly, and you would need a new plan.

## What changes under NextGen (short note only, no content built)
- Delivery moves to NCBE's computer-based platform on your laptop over 1.5 days: Day 1 has two 3-hour sessions and Day 2 one 3-hour session. Applicants pay NCBE a technical fee, "currently $145" [S-020].
- The subject mix changes; for example, family law is tested from July 2028 [S-020]. California-only essay subjects drop out of the national portion until the California component launches.
- Score portability under NextGen is still to be announced. NCBE lists California's minimum score and transfer policy as "TBA" [S-024].
- **Today** California accepts **no** transferred UBE, NextGen or MBE scores and has no admission on motion [S-043]. You cannot sit the UBE in another state and bring the score to California, so a California sitting is the only route.

## Why this matters for your plan
- **Take July 2027 seriously as the main attempt.** If you fail, February 2028 is the **only** retake on the same format. That gives you about 7 months, inside the same eligibility window.
- After February 2028, a retake means preparing for a different exam. That makes over-preparing for July 2027 cheap insurance.
- If the State Bar or the Court announces anything new about February 2028, `/update-exam-intel` must flag it immediately.
